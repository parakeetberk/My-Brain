# Brokers and advisors

**What this is:** Eastdil — the recap and Core+ advisor, and the source of the board deck's exit-value argument. The 2026 priority they carry is the one 2025 goal that slipped.

**Status:** current — 2026-08-30

## The facts

- **Eastdil is the recap and Core+ advisor.** The board deck's instruction to itself is explicit: **"ENGAGE WITH EASTDIL ON PLANNING"**, paired with **"PAIR CORE+ VEHICLE WITH JREFI RECAP."**
- **The framing is theirs and it is stated as the strategic headline: "FINAL FRONTIER OF CAPITAL FOR PLATFORM GROWTH."** Under Core+ the deck lists: "OpCo profitable" · "National expansion" · **"Path to 10,000+ units."** Against 6,379 core units today, that is a roughly 1.6× platform.
- **The exit-value argument is sourced to them, verbatim:** "According to Eastdil, the mere proof of concept of being able to execute in-fill substantiates a higher value per vacant pad from recap equity b/c it increases certainty of forecasted cash flows." That is the reason infill matters to valuation and not just to NOI.
- **They are running a full portfolio BOV.** "WE ARE HAVING THE MH/RV TEAM AT EASTDIL DO A FULL PORTFOLIO BOV OF JREFI WITH OUR RECAP IN MIND", on the basis that "Eastdil's MH/RV team is by far the best to evaluate our portfolio given their experience selling MH portfolios for the largest" owners.
- **Their initial commentary shapes what gets fixed.** On POH concentration: "Not an issue for this portfolio. Typically become more of a focal point at 25%+ POH (agency issue). Yes! deal has >25% and getting done." On portfolio size: "No obvious outliers that turn buyers off (not even Geiger, education is key). **Portfolios >$200m in deal size is a good threshold to be above.**"
- **They also set the capex priorities.** Twice in the deck: "Capex focused on projects that **Eastdil has guided us are critical for both Agency debt and Core+ buyers.**" So the advisor's read reaches directly into [[10-Business/departments/construction]]'s project list.
- **The 2026 priority, verbatim: "Recapitalize JREFI portfolio & launch Core+ vehicle – Launch process by year-end with significant operating momentum."**
- **The board deck flags this as the one 2025 goal that slipped.** The same line in the 2025 goals column reads: **"Recapitalize JREFI portfolio & launch Core+ vehicle – Remains a major future initiative, but timing behind plan/uncertain."**
- **What the recap is being judged on is sales momentum, not construction.** On 2026-08-24 the group said plainly that infill had run ahead of plan — 130 homes delivered, 40 listed and ready — so the constraint had moved to sales, and **sales over the next six months determines the recap** ([[10-Business/departments/sales-and-marketing]]).
- **The disposition side runs through the same relationship.** JREFI disposition plans in the deck name Shell Pointe and Congress sales as portfolio repositioning, and Geiger Key Resort as a standalone disposition candidate — highest storm exposure in JREFI, and a restaurant OpCo that "simplifies portfolio" if sold ([[50-Products-Sales/ancillary-income]]).
- **The screen the deck applies to a disposition candidate, verbatim:** "SMALL ASSET, HIGH RV EXPOSURE, LARGE $ CAPEX PROJECT, EXECUTION RISK, OR GENERAL PORTFOLIO OUTLIER (GEIGER)."
- **Who owns the relationship internally:** Jon Wyss on capital, deals and investors, with Zach Schwartz on acquisitions and dispositions ([[10-Business/people/jon-wyss]]).
- **The other advisors in the stack, filed separately:** Woods Oviatt Gilman and Lowenstein Sandler on legal ([[10-Business/outside-help/counsel]]), Citrin Cooperman on tax and audit ([[10-Business/outside-help/citrin-cooperman]]), Baldwin on insurance ([[10-Business/outside-help/baldwin]]).
