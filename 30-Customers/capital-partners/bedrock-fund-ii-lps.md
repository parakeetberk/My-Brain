# Bedrock Fund II LPs

**What this is:** the limited partner base of Bedrock MHC Fund II, LP — a fund in its terminal wind-down phase, with three assets left and a Q4 2026 dissolution date.

**Status:** current, 2026-09-15. The K-1 delivery record was added by the Page Update; the rest is as of 2026-08-30 and 08-31.

## Handling constraint — read this first

**The owner's instruction, in his words: this is a base "that needs to go away but they are litigious so need careful handling during the last few months of the fund."** Every communication, distribution and filing in the remaining months is to be handled on that basis. This is a live constraint on how the wind-down is run, not a footnote to it.

## The facts

- **Bedrock MHC Fund II, LP is in wind-down.** Austin Berk is Managing Partner. Reporting is signed **"Bedrock I Group"**, with **ir@thebedrockgrp.com** as the stated contact.
- **The Southeast portfolio sale closed 2025-12-30 and covered 10 of the 13 portfolio investments** — the most significant milestone of the wind-down.
- **The sale was two transactions, not one:** the sale of the underlying real estate, and the subsequent payoff of a seller note the fund held because the buyer financed part of the price.
- **No cash was distributed from the real estate sale itself.** Gross proceeds of $29,100,000 plus $57,066 of property-level cash were applied entirely to **senior debt retirement and defeasance ($10,093,141), closing costs and settlement items ($82,490), and seller financing ($18,780,000).**
- **The seller note was paid off 2026-03-14, and that is what funded the distribution.** The $18,780,000 payoff went to balance sheet clean-up ($2,917,837), reserves ($270,000) and **$16,060,163 of cash to distribute.** After JV splits that produced roughly **$11M of distributable cash to Fund II LPs, equating to $0.99 per unit**, paid in March 2026.
- **All LPs were made whole on capital and preferred return as of 2026-07-31.** Every investor account has received its final capital and preferred return distribution; Q2 capital account statements showing a zero remaining balance are on the portal.
- **No profits above the preferred return and no sponsor promote have been distributed.** All distributions to date have flowed exclusively to LPs under the waterfall.
- **Three assets remain: Lamberton Lake, Royal Palm and Everglades.** Spring and summer 2026 were spent on curb appeal and deferred maintenance at all three, to position them for the strongest possible sale price. All three are still filed as live communities: [[60-Communities/lamberton-lake-mhc]], [[60-Communities/royal-palm-trailer-park]], [[60-Communities/everglades-mobile-home-park]].
- **The stated path, four milestones, verbatim from the April 2026 letter:** "3rd Quarter 2026: Closing of the three remaining portfolio investments" · "3rd Quarter 2026: Final waterfall calculation and reconciliation of investor accounts" · "4th Quarter 2026: Final LP distribution" · "4th Quarter 2026: Final federal and state tax filings; formal partnership dissolution." **Written confirmation is promised as each milestone is reached, and a final 2026 K-1 follows dissolution.** As of the Q2 2026 letter the fund reported it remained on track across all four.
- **Performance at exit:** inclusive of the Southeast disposition and assuming a base case for the three remaining assets, the fund was tracking to a **MOIC of 1.87x** over its life, with final realised figures to be published with the dissolution notice.
- **Reporting is quarterly partner letters plus ad-hoc milestone letters**, delivered through an investor portal that carries capital account statements and K-1s. The 2025 draft K-1 was posted with a notification email on 23 April 2026.
- **The related-party bidder disclosure was explicit and unprompted.** The Q3 2025 letter states that the single bid to acquire all remaining holdings **"was submitted by an investor affiliated with one of our joint venture partners, who is also affiliated with a Principal of the General Partner."** The stated rationale for proceeding: a party already familiar with the portfolio means "materially reduced transaction costs, an accelerated closing timeline, and no broker fees, while still ensuring that pricing and economic terms remain competitive."
- **The safeguards adopted, verbatim:** "A reputable, independent third-party firm will be engaged to formally appraise the asset values. **Any sale will be completed on arms-length terms, for cash, and at a value no less than the independent appraisal.**"
- **Two acknowledgements of underperformance appear in the letters.** April 2026: "We recognize that the wind-down has been a longer process than any of us anticipated at outset, and we appreciate your continued patience." And in the Q2 2025 letter, on the 2024 return: "tax returns are currently in partner review. We are actively working to finalize and distribute these documents as soon as possible."
- **No LP count appears in any document in this vault.** The number of limited partners is not recorded anywhere in the filed letters, and is not to be inferred from the per-unit figures. **Corrected in scope 2026-08-31, and it is a correction about where the number lives rather than about the number:** Austin has previously stated the wind-down involves **LP capital account statements for 62 partners**, produced as two-page PDFs from a waterfall engine. That is **SINGLE-SOURCE, from him and not from a document**, and it is recorded here because this page has twice said the figure does not exist when what was true is that no *filed letter* carries it. **It is not treated as confirmed** and should be checked against the actual statement run before it is used in anything outward-facing, but "we do not know" was the wrong answer and "no letter says" is the right one.

## Live contradiction, opened 2026-08-31

**An LP is working from a capital account statement showing a $52,000 balance, and Austin has admitted the statement was wrong.** On a 2026-08-31 call with the principal of **WCO Capital** ([[70-Operations/meetings/2026-08-31-bedrock-fund-ii-lp-call]]), Austin opened by naming his own reconciliation error: catch-up payments were added without removing the previously recorded ones, duplicating payments and making it look as though **$30,000** was missing. It was not missing. The payments were pushed to the end of the schedule and accrued the 8% preferred, leaving **$52,000** at the bottom of the statement.

**This does not obviously square with the fact recorded above** from the Q2 2026 letter, that every LP was made whole on capital and preferred return as of 2026-07-31 with **zero remaining balance** statements on the portal. Both can be true if the statement under discussion predates the July zeroing. **Nothing on the recording establishes which statement it is, and Austin never says the account now reads zero.** Recorded as open rather than resolved in either direction, because on this base the difference between "your statement was wrong" and "your balance is wrong" is the whole question.

**Two further facts from the same call.** The LP believed the fund was at a **9% IRR** and says the phantom $30,000 corroborated it; the corrected picture is about **7.9%** against an 8 to 9% target. And a **$7,000 July 2021 distribution went to the LP's former Kadara account rather than WCO Capital and has never been traced** — the old account entry was deleted right after the error, which is why it cannot be inspected now.

**The LP's ask, and it is a new obligation**: a fund-level waterfall **prepared and signed off by the CPAs**, so they can make a representation. Austin agreed. The filed plan promises a final waterfall in Q3 2026; it never promised an outside representation. **Nobody has decided whether this is a one-off for one investor or something every LP now gets.** Carried as [[00-Meta/commitments]] item 25.
