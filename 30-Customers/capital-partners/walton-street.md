# Walton Street

**What this is:** WSRED — Walton Street Real Estate Debt — the largest debt relationship in the platform, holding both portfolio facilities and 41 of the 74 loans on the schedule.

**Status:** current — 2026-08-30 Extended 2026-09-26: a dated block from the closes of 2026-09-22 to 09-25 was added by the Page Update; nothing above it was changed.

## The facts

- **Walton Street holds both portfolio facilities — the JREFI portfolio loan and the JREFII portfolio loan — with Key Bank as debt administrator.**
- **41 of the 74 loans in the loan-by-loan debt schedule are Walton Street: 20 on JREF I and 21 on JREF II.** The schedule runs across 13 lenders in total ([[10-Business/outside-help/lenders]]).
- **JREFI portfolio loan: $113,050,000**, originated May-24 at **2.85% + SOFR**, full-term interest-only, maturing **Jan-27** with two 1-year extensions.
- **JREFII portfolio loan, in three tranches: $62.5m (May-25), $18.175m (Jul-25) and $54.297m (Aug-25)** — all at **2.75% + SOFR**, full-term interest-only, maturing **May-28** with two 1-year extensions.
- **Both portfolios are fully financed with flexible pre-pay floating-rate debt and no maturities until mid-2027.**
- **The earn-outs are DSCR-gated.** The first two JREFI WSRED earn-outs were disbursed in Q3 2025. **The final two JREFI earn-outs ($2.875m) and the first two JREFII earn-outs ($4.4m) were expected to be met in Q1/Q2 2026** — that is, the first half of 2026.
- **Walton Street also reimburses capex.** The JREFI cash flow model carries a **$200,000 Walton Street capex reimbursement** with the note "Submitting request in April."
- **The cost of the structure showed up in 2025.** Interest expense ran **$1.4m over budget — $8,825,916 against $7,421,990, +19%** — because "the SOFR curve did not come down as anticipated in 2025." On floating-rate, full-term interest-only debt that variance goes straight to portfolio cash flow, which came in 25% under budget.
- **Lender obligations reach into the capex plan, not just the balance sheet.** The **$600,000 Northgate WWTP replacement**, estimated completion August 2027, is described as part of "our lender required repairs."
- **Response time is treated as a relationship asset in its own right.** SOP 302: "Parakeet's response time on repossession notices is tracked by lenders and directly impacts the company's ability to secure future line increases and strategic business opportunities" ([[70-Operations/sops/302-home-lender-repo]]).
- **The 2026 plan depends on this relationship in two directions at once:** closing the JREFII refinancing, and recapitalising the JREFI portfolio while launching a Core+ vehicle ([[30-Customers/capital-partners/brokers-and-advisors]]).
- **Who owns the relationship internally:** Austin Berk and Jon Wyss on structure and refinancing; David Berman on debt administration, covenants and treasury; Zach Schwartz on the earn-out DSCR tests ([[10-Business/departments/accounting-and-finance]]).
- **Banking runs separately from the mortgage debt:** J.P. Morgan Commercial Access, with two main physical accounts for the funds and virtual accounts underneath each for every SPV. The affiliates org chart tags most JREFI and JREFII properties **ACCESS**, which is the banking relationship, not the lender.
- **Portfolio coverage: JREFI 22 communities and JREFII 22 communities, 44 of the 67 active communities** ([[60-Communities/portfolio]]).

### What is not documented

- **There are no loan or mortgage documents anywhere in the 430 entity files.** No credit agreement, note, mortgage, guaranty or lender-required-repair schedule is filed in `10-Business/entity-structure/`. The debt terms above come from the board deck and have **no supporting documents in this vault**; the only loan-level detail is an archived spreadsheet, `99-Archive/rock-processes/David/MASTER Debt Overview.xlsx`.
- **Debt Overview is one of the four processes queued in the empty Asset Management SOP band (400–499)**, so nothing about covenant tracking, earn-out submission or lender reporting is documented ([[70-Operations/sops/sop-library]]).
- **No compliance calendar exists**, which is the other queued process in that band and the instrument a lender relationship of this size would normally run on.
