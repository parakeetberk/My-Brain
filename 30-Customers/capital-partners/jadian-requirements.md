### Responsible Contractor

Confirmed by Austin on 2026-08-31, and both governing documents arrived the same day. **It is running, and Parakeet's exposure is much smaller than it first appeared.**

- This is the policy of **Jadian Real Estate Fund II**, applying to US real estate equity investments at **50% or more ownership with a controlling interest**. That points at the **JREFII portfolio, 22 properties** rather than all 67. Confirming scope is the first question.
- **Under the Policy's own minimum contract values, Parakeet probably has no covered contracts:** $25M for new construction capital works, $10M for ongoing capital works, $2.5M annually for operating and maintenance contracts, measured per project contract with no disaggregation. The whole 2026 capital plan is $11.6M across 245 line items.
- So Parakeet sits in the Policy's **good-faith regime**, and Jadian's implementation plan pushes the clause down to **all contracts and renewals over $1,000 annually** — which is why every vendor signs it.
- The mechanism is **section IV(e) of Parakeet's Vendor Guidelines**, signed as part of the mandatory Vendor Package (W-9, COI, signed Guidelines) before any work is authorised. Parakeet's clause carries the base language plus the Good Faith sentence, verbatim from Jadian, including Jadian's own three typos.
- **Cadence is annual** — "at least annually, audit one or more parties." One report exists, as of 30 September 2025. **An earlier reading of this as three missed quarterly periods was wrong.** The 2026 cycle is coming due.
- Four vendors are certified in the 2025 report, all signed. Whether that is the population or a sample is answerable from AP records.
- **Four gaps survive:** no "Responsible Contractor Review" section in contract approval emails, which the plan explicitly requires; no list of interested Responsible Contractors, which the Policy makes an explicit duty; no evidence the annual audit happened; and solicitation documents that do not describe the Policy's terms.
- Full detail, including the verbatim definitions, the three tiers of contract language, the enforcement mechanism and the union-neutrality clause: [[10-Business/vendors/responsible-contractor-program]].

4. **What is the ESG due date**, and are 2H25 and 1H26 outstanding?
4. **Which entities and investments does either obligation cover** — all of JREFI and JREFII, or only majority-owned vehicles?
5. **Is there an annual certificate already being signed by someone?** If Jadian has been receiving one, somebody at Parakeet has been producing it.
6. Both belong on a compliance calendar. That calendar is one of the queued items in the empty 400 Asset Management SOP band — see [[70-Operations/sops/year-end-push]].
