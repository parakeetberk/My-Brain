# Jadian

**What this is:** the fund-level capital partner behind Parakeet HoldCo II — in the owner's words, "our true forward looking partner." Jadian Capital's funds sit on the ownership side of the platform and on the receiving end of most of its reporting.

**Status:** current — 2026-08-30 Extended 2026-09-26: a dated block from the closes of 2026-09-22 to 09-25 was added by the Page Update; nothing above it was changed.

## The facts

- **The owner's framing: Jadian is "our true forward looking partner."** Not a lender and not a broker — the equity relationship the 2026 plan is built around.
- **The two entities named in the incentive plan are the JREF Entities: "Jadian Real Estate Fund I-B, LP, a Delaware limited partnership, and Jadian Parakeet Aggregator LP, a Delaware limited partnership."** That definition is in the Parakeet HoldCo II MIP LLC Agreement.
- **The JREF Entities' units vest in full at grant.** The plan's own drafting note, verbatim: "Award Agreements for the JREF Entities will provide that their Management LLC Units will vest in full on date of grant." Every other participant's units vest over time.
- **They take 50% of every plan distribution.** Section 5.1(b): "the Company shall distribute: (i) fifty percent (50%) of the Plan Distribution Amount to the JREF Entities holding Vested Management LLC Units pro rata… and (ii) fifty percent (50%) of the Plan Distribution Amount to the Other Participants." **If no Other Participant holds vested units, their half is re-allocated to the JREF Entities.** No distribution is made until the Distribution Threshold Condition is satisfied.
- **Lowenstein Sandler LLP prepared and negotiated the Parakeet HoldCo II LLC Agreement representing the Investor Member**, with an explicit conflict-of-interest acknowledgment and waiver by the Members in the agreement itself. The MIP documents were redlined out of Lowenstein's system with a Litera Compare run dated 2024-10-10 ([[10-Business/outside-help/counsel]]).
- **On the Asset Management Agreement, the direction of the relationship is the opposite way round from how it is often described.** The agreement filed at `10-Business/entity-structure/Asset Management Agreement/`, **effective 2024-10-11**, is between **Parakeet HoldCo II, LLC as Owner and Parakeet MHC LLC as Manager** — appointing Parakeet, not Jadian, as exclusive asset manager, at **4% of Effective Gross Income.** Jadian sits on the Owner side: **Jadian Real Estate Fund II Aggregator, LP signs for Owner through Jadian Real Estate Fund II GP, LLC**, and all Owner notices go **c/o Jadian Capital** (legal@jadiancapital.com). Effective Gross Income is defined against the Limited Partnership Agreement of **Jadian Real Estate Fund II, LP, dated 2024-03-15**, as determined by Jadian Real Estate Fund II GP, LLC.
- **What Jadian actually does with Parakeet, per the board deck's own accomplishment slides:** "CORPORATE MODEL & VALUATION – Worked with Jadian team to build robust corporate model and take on quarterly valuations going fwd" and "OCCUPANCY TRACKER – Worked with Jadian team to create granular weekly occupancy & in-fill tracker to show step-by-step progress."
- **Jadian's valuation is the yardstick a disposition is measured against.** On a $5,000,000 offer with a $200,000 deposit of which $10,000 is non-refundable, the deck runs a "Comparison of Offer vs. Jadian Valuation": "Jadian Valuation: $5M offer compares to $5.2M in our valuation model on an immediate sale basis."
- **Weekly reporting to Jadian is a stated 2026 corporate priority**, verbatim: "Enhanced reporting – Weekly internal and Jadian reporting rollout across occupancy/infill, capex and operations KPIs." A 2025 goal in the same document reads "Occupancy & project management reporting – Finalize internal real-time reporting, with Jadian reporting to follow."
- **Jadian carries the D&O and E&O master policy that Parakeet's own policy sits under.** The insurance inventory lists **Peleus** on E&O / D&O, "covered by the Jadian master policy" ([[10-Business/outside-help/baldwin]]).
- **JREFI and JREFII are the two portfolios the relationship covers — 22 communities each, 44 of the 67 active communities** ([[60-Communities/portfolio]]).
- **The debt sits alongside, not inside, this relationship.** Walton Street holds both portfolio facilities with Key Bank as administrator ([[30-Customers/capital-partners/walton-street]]).
- **Who owns the relationship internally:** Jon Wyss on capital, deals and investors, with Zach Schwartz as his operating right hand — anything in that lane goes to Jon and does not need Austin in it ([[10-Business/people/jon-wyss]], [[10-Business/people/zach-schwartz]]).
- **Where the reporting is produced:** [[10-Business/departments/asset-management]] owns the occupancy and infill tracker, the capex tracker and the portfolio reporting, jointly with [[10-Business/departments/accounting-and-finance]] on the monthly financials.
