## The rules for this folder, and they are stricter than the rest of the vault

- **Nothing in here goes on the open board or into any published artifact.** The board publishes to a URL. **Personal items get no reference IDs and no board rows**, on purpose: a `PF-` number is a thing that appears on a shared page. The daily close is a local file and may carry a short personal block; the morning brief is a published page and does not, unless Austin says otherwise.
- **No health information.** Not conditions, not diagnoses, not medications, not the reason for an appointment. **An appointment date at a named practice is a logistics fact and is allowed; why anyone is going is not**, and it gets left out rather than reworded.
- **Nothing about the girls beyond logistics.** No ages, no dates of birth, no grade or school year, no assessments. A practice time, a game date, a coach or club name is logistics.
- **No account numbers, credentials, policy numbers, claim numbers or government identifiers.** Name the institution and that a document exists; leave the number in the source.
- **No balances, no income, no valuations.** **Construction costs, quotes and change orders are in scope**, because he is managing a budget and asked about them.
- **Documents live in Dropbox**, per the 2026-09-02 routing rule. This folder holds context, dates and who to call.
