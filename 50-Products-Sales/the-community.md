# The community

**What this is:** the core product. Parakeet rents land — a maintained, managed community with a lot or a site in it — and everything else the company sells exists to fill those lots.

**Status:** current — 2026-08-30

## The facts

- **The owner's framing, and it governs every page in this folder:** "Our product is the community. And now we are selling homes we brought into the communities. We don't really sell a product someone takes away, we sell a home, a lifestyle, a community, a safe place to live and more. Rent it more than sell it. We rent the land but sell the homes on it. We don't want the homes and the additional overhead and expenses that go along with them."
- **What is rented: 5,293 MH lots and 950 RV sites** across 67 active communities, plus 136 other units — 6,379 total core units, on 934.3 acres (Property Data Sheet, as of 2026-08-30, via [[60-Communities/portfolio]]).
- **Where they are:** Florida 47 communities, South Carolina 7, Massachusetts 5, Georgia 5, Michigan 1, North Carolina 1, Alabama 1. The Michigan property, Lamberton Lake, is being sold.
- **Occupancy: 82.5% unit-weighted** across the 62 communities with a usable December-2025 figure (simple average 81.1%). Five rows — Great Oak RV, Paradise Lakes, River Oaks RV, Nalls Court and Hendersons — show 0% because occupancy was not reliably captured, not because they are empty; 0% must never be presented as fact.
- **2026 MH lot rent runs $373 to $1,367 a month**, and averages **$691** across the 63 communities with a nonzero MH rent (simple average of the 2026 gross figures in the Property Data Sheet). Lowest: Regency I & II at $373. Highest: Triple S Marina at $1,367.
- **2026 RV site rent runs $600 to $2,275 a month**, and averages **$897** across the 18 communities with a nonzero RV rent. Lowest: Belle Haven, Hendersons and Vantage Oaks at $600. Highest: Geiger Key RV at $2,275.
- **The stated pricing philosophy, verbatim from the March deck:** "As a philosophy we want to maximize lot rent and minimize home price. This transfers value to our NOI and land value." That is why [[50-Products-Sales/home-sales]] runs on discounts and this page runs on rent.
- **Rents are being marked to market:** average rents +8% in 2026, already in place, and +6% planned for 2027 (board deck, JREFI NOI bridge).
- **Age mix: 53 all-ages communities, 14 fifty-five-plus, and 1 fifty-plus** (Vantage Oaks). The 55+ communities are where the snowbird demand concentrates — Sea Shells and Southgate are named as the big 55+ snowbird properties.
- **What the resident actually buys.** A maintained community — roads, drainage, lighting, signage, mailboxes, amenities, landscaping. Capital already in the ground: the 2026 capex plan carries $9,954,600 across the 169 line items still marked "Existing" (53 properties). An on-site manager who answers, in 28 of the 29 manager slots ([[10-Business/people/field-roster]]). And a **total monthly payment below a comparable two-bedroom rental** — the benchmark the company prices against is a $1,448 two-bedroom market rent ([[50-Products-Sales/home-sales]]).
- **The website's promise, verbatim:** **"Live Simply. Live Well. Live Parakeet."** and "vibrant neighborhoods where quality, comfort, and a friendly atmosphere make life a little brighter." See [[20-Brand-Marketing/online-presence]].
- **Who the resident is, in the company's own vocabulary:** a **homeowner** owns the home and rents the lot; a **renter** rents a company-owned home. "Resident" is the default word for both. See [[30-Customers/customers]].
- **The screening standard on the way in:** gross monthly income at or above **2.5× the monthly lot rent** ([[70-Operations/sops/006-lead-intake-tracking-application-and-screening]]).
- **The tenancy documents that define the product by state:** the Community Lease, Rules and Regulations, the **Florida prospectus** with a signed receipt as the gate on every Florida lot tenancy, and South Carolina Forms 400 and 500 ([[10-Business/departments/legal-and-compliance]]).
- **How the community is delivered day to day:** the resident lifecycle SOPs, 100 through 108 — collections, lease renewal, move-in, move-out, rehab, site visits, incident reporting — owned by [[10-Business/departments/property-operations]].
- **Landscaping is now a bundled service, not an enforcement problem.** From 2026-08-19 the cost is bundled into rent for rentals and homeowners get a "trim it or we remove it" ultimatum.
- **Two revenue lines sit on top of lot rent:** utility reimbursement, billed pro rata on a total lot basis so it rises with occupancy, and fee and miscellaneous income. See [[50-Products-Sales/ancillary-income]].
- **The strategic point of the product:** 925 recorded vacancies across the 51 properties in the 2026 infill plan, against a 661-occupant gain goal. Every vacant lot is unearned rent on land the company already owns, which is why infill is priority #1.

## Decisions

- 2026-08-19 — Landscaping moves to a centralised service model: bundled into rent for rentals, with a "trim it or we remove it" ultimatum for homeowners, replacing reactive penalty-based enforcement.
