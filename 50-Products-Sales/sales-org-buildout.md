## What's being built

A **permanent sales organization**, explicitly not a temporary one: assistant managers moving into sales/leasing-agent roles, regional sales managers aligned to the existing regionals ("three Anthonys" in the source). Job ads are out now; **seats targeted to be filled within roughly 4 weeks** (i.e. by late September 2026). A Loan Coordinator posting is already live, posted same-day by Demetria.

**Staffing plan**: Vantage Oaks gets a dedicated daily salesperson; River Oaks gets similar coverage; smaller communities are covered regionally rather than individually staffed.

This shares the same 5-week runway to October snowbird season as the systems convergence — see `10-Business/systems-architecture.md`. Hires not landing in time means going into the highest-volume selling window short-staffed.

## The highest-frequency fixable revenue leak of the week

**Call routing.** The problem came up four separate times across the week's conversations: **on-site prospects reach a 24-hour call center while the property manager sits in the office.** Rhonda is named specifically as losing buyers to this. The chosen fix is **RingCentral** — direction is set; the remaining work is onboarding every manager onto the app, not deciding whether to do it.

This is explicitly the single most fixable thing surfaced this week — it doesn't require new hires, new systems, or a vendor decision, only rollout. Related, property-specific instance: the Whispering Pines manager's personal phone number is listed alongside the sales line on two Daisy Bear listings because she lives on-site and was losing walk-ups to the call center before RingCentral existed as a fix — see `60-Communities/portfolio.md`.

## Deliberately paused

**Google Ads stays off** until the infrastructure above (systems + staffing + call routing) is ready. The team is already near capacity on call volume; adding paid lead flow before the routing problem is fixed would make the leak worse, not better.
