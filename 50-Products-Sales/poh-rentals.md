# Park-owned home rentals

**What this is:** 784 company-owned homes rented to residents. The thing the company is deliberately shedding, not growing — every conversion turns a renter into an owner and a rental into lot rent.

**Status:** current — 2026-08-30

## The facts

- **The strategic logic, in the owner's words:** "We don't want the homes and the additional overhead and expenses that go along with them." A park-owned home carries R&M, capex, turn cost, insurance and management that a rented lot does not.
- **784 park-owned homes across the 67 active communities** (Property Data Sheet, as of 2026-08-30, via [[60-Communities/portfolio]]).
- **The 2026 plan is to reduce the POH count by more than 100 units and push renter conversions above that** — a stated 2026 goal in the board deck's Goals & Objectives pillars.
- **Where the concentration is.** In JREFII, POH concentration is **20% of units — 405 POHs plus 100 RTOs, 505 in total across 2,520 units** (board deck, March 2026). The worst three: **Fox Creek at 121 POHs, 94% of its 129 units; Parkwood at 73, 81% of 90; Auburn Ridge at 46, 29% of 157.** Silver Hill carries 38, of which 36 are RTOs. **36 POHs sold or converted and 5 RTOs burned off since acquisition.**
- **The Property Data Sheet's own POH counts differ from the board deck's** because the deck separates POH from RTO and the sheet does not — Fox Creek 112, Parkwood 34, Silver Hill 38 on the sheet. Both are recorded; neither is wrong, they count different things.
- **Rental conversion focus properties: Parkwood, Fox Creek and Silver Hill**, all in the LS region, all 2025 acquisitions ([[10-Business/people/liznette-santiago]]).
- **The conversion vehicle is the "AutoMHatic conversion program", officially launched March 2026.** The board deck's description: "Officially launching our renter conversion program with AutoMHatic in March '26 with multiple credit profile buckets which prospects automatically get filtered into and 'instantly approved'." **Lower credit 500 to 580: 10% to 12% interest, 20-year term. Higher credit above 580: roughly 8%, 20-year term.**
- **The company's language for the whole exercise is "convert renters to owners"**, and commissions for POH conversions are overweighted "due to nature and duration of sale process."
- **10+ additional prospects would likely have converted already** but the conversion lender had only just rolled out its lower credit profile programme; the team was reaching back out to re-engage them.
- **R&M is budgeted at $200 per POH per month.** Actual 2025 spend came in materially below that: **$125 per POH per month in JREFII** and **$84 per POH per month in JREFI**. The board deck states plainly that after a full year of operating communities with high POH concentrations, "we will revisit and if needed revise this assumption." The R&M budget excluding POH expense is in line with 2025 plus growth.
- **The turn standard: any vacated home for sale or lease in under 60 days** (board deck 2026 goal; March deck slide 51, Rehab Strategy).
- **Rehab is how a POH becomes sellable or re-rentable**, governed by [[70-Operations/sops/105-poh-rehab]] and the TBD unit review at [[70-Operations/sops/104-tbd-unit-review-and-allocate]]. **No rehab charge may be booked until a valid Rent Manager issue number exists**, and the rehab is not marked complete until Accounting confirms GL coding.
- **The 2026 rehab target is 100+ rehabs across the portfolios**, owned by [[10-Business/departments/construction]].
- **When a renter leaves, the sequence is move-out, then rehab, then list.** [[70-Operations/sops/102-move-out]] and [[70-Operations/sops/103-move-in]] run the tenancy ends; [[70-Operations/sops/100-ar-and-collections]] and [[70-Operations/sops/107-eviction]] run the money.
- **POH spend has its own approval route: park-owned-home expenditure over $1,000 routes to the Director of Sales** in the AVID workflow, not to Operations. A $950 POH invoice stays standard ([[70-Operations/sops/300-avid-exchange-ap]]).
- **Where title comes back in:** [[70-Operations/sops/005-poh-title-transfer]] on a sale, and [[70-Operations/sops/post-eviction-and-turn-in-title-transfer]] when a home comes back through eviction or a voluntary turn-in.
- **A POH that cannot be sold or converted still has a lender consequence:** [[70-Operations/sops/302-home-lender-repo]] gives three purchase options on a repossession notice — 10% for spec inventory, 10% for rental conversion, or full payoff.
- **The scorecard metric is "POH Conversions"**, carried by both Leslie Croft and the Sales & Marketing / Asset Management pair, with **no target attached in the scorecard file** ([[10-Business/eos/scorecard]]).
- **POH concentration is not a sale-blocker.** Eastdil's read, verbatim from the board deck: "Not an issue for this portfolio. Typically become more of a focal point at 25%+ POH (agency issue). Yes! deal has >25% and getting done." See [[30-Customers/capital-partners/brokers-and-advisors]].

### Named problems

- **The rehab process is unfinished.** SOP 105 is in review with two different process owners, two different clocks, an undefined one-week extension process, and placeholder `[GL-XXXX]` GL codes throughout 105b.
- **The form platform is unresolved inside a single document** — SOP 105 names the FastField punchlist in one task and the JotForm punchlist four lines later.
- **No rehab completion count exists anywhere in the vault**, so the Q1 rock "complete all backlogged rehabs outstanding as of 3/31" cannot be assessed. Three homes at The Oaks still had rehabs to start on 2026-08-22.
- **The March deck asks its own question:** "HOW ARE WE KEEPING TRACK OF THE REHAB PROCESS, UNITS, COSTS, ETC?"
- **Sell versus rent has no decision rule:** "At what # of homes available for sale per community do we start pushing rentals? Is there a time-on-market threshold as well?"
- **The make ready board does not record rentals.** The deck's own note: "Sale vs. rent recording (need to track rentals)."
- **Legacy renters repeatedly fail credit approval.** From 2026-08-18: "I see in the conversions that these people that were already here, they can't get approved or struggling with it. It's just been **denial after denial**."

## Decisions

- 2026-08-22 — At The Oaks: no new incoming renters; renew existing renters and keep converting renters to owners, with buyout options considered to accelerate LOP conversions. (Source: [[70-Operations/meetings/2026-08-southeast-tour/2026-08-22-the-oaks-site-visit]])
