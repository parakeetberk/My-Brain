This document outlines the proposed rollout sequence for building Parakeet's full operations system in QuickBase (QB) and FastField. It is being shared with the team prior to finalizing the plan so everyone can weigh in on order, priority, and anything that may be missing before we commit to a build sequence.

The system covers 46 workflows spanning every department — Operations, Construction, Sales, Legal, Accounting, Acquisitions, and HR. Five workflows are already specced or in progress (Home Ordering, Rehab, TBD Process, Move Out, and Listings). The remaining 41 are organized into 8 build waves in the proposed order below.

Why does order matter? Many workflows depend on data created by earlier ones. A Collections record, for example, needs to look up a Resident — which means Resident records have to exist first. The order below is designed to ensure no workflow has to be rebuilt later because a dependency was missed. That said, we want your input before locking this in.

How We Organized the Build Order
1

Field Team First

Waves 1–4 prioritize workflows that CMs, RMs, and construction staff touch every day — move-ins, site visits, inspections, and maintenance. High daily visibility = faster adoption.

2

Dependencies Drive Order

You can't build a Collections workflow before Resident records exist. Each wave creates the data the next wave needs, so nothing has to be rebuilt due to a missed dependency.

3

Shared Tables Are Pre-Work

Five core data tables (Residents, Lease Records, Work Orders, Title Records, Utility Accounts) must be built before Wave 1. Every workflow reads from at least one of them.

4

Revenue Protection Follows Field

Collections, Stipulations, and Evictions (Wave 5) are high-stakes workflows that build on the resident and field data established in Waves 1–4. Legal teams get a fully integrated system.

Field Operations & Construction

The highest FastField usage wave — these are the workflows CMs and construction staff interact with most. Site visits, weekly maintenance, rental inspections, utility activation, demo, and lot prep. Building these early drives field-team adoption of the whole system.

Wave 4

Compliance & Legal

Compliance workflows are largely field-adjacent and calendar-triggered, making them a natural follow-on to Wave 3. Violations connect to the Residents table (built in Wave 1). License/permit and insurance renewals are simpler to build because they run on a calendar, not events. Can run partially in parallel with Wave 3.

Wave 5

Revenue Protection — Collections & Legal

Collections, Stipulations, Evictions, Post-Eviction Title, and Rent Increases. These workflows carry the highest financial stakes but require the full resident and field data layer to work correctly. Building on top of an established system means the team is already comfortable with QB before the most complex workflows go live.

WF017 Collections / AR Monthly collections workflow. QB auto-calculates cure period deadline from state on Lease record (FL 5 bd, SC 5 days, GA 3 bd, NC 10 days, MA 30 days). Demand notices coordinated through Ops. If not cured: triggers Stipulation (WF018) or Eviction (WF019). QB Only
WF018 Stipulation Process Payment plan agreement when a resident cannot pay in full. Regional Manager approval required. QB tracks the full payment schedule and compliance. Missed payment auto-triggers escalation to WF019 Eviction. QB Only

WF020 Post-Eviction Title Process Triggered by WF019 when writ of possession is granted on a POH home. Liened path (Vector Law) vs. non-liened path (FL Fast Title). QB tracks title status through Title Records table. Connects to WF003 TBD Process once title clears. QB Only
WF021 Rent Increase & Tax Pass-Throughs State-specific notice requirements: FL Ch. 723 (90-day), GA (60-day), NC/SC (60-day), MA (most restrictive). Wave 6

Accounting & Financial Operations

Most accounting workflows depend on resident-level data (security deposits, utility recapture) or asset-sale data that comes from earlier waves. Month End Close Out is the integrating workflow — it reads from Work Orders, Listings, and Lease Records. Note: RM remains the system of record for all financial data; QB tracks workflow status and operational metrics only.

Wave 7

Acquisitions & New Community Onboarding

Acquisitions workflows bring new communities into the system — they feed all prior waves by populating Communities, Lots, and Residents records for newly acquired properties. They don't depend on day-to-day operational data, so they can be specced in parallel with Waves 4–6. The Acquisitions Transition workflow (WF039) is the most complex single workflow in the entire OS — it touches every shared table.

Wave 8

HR, Training & Operational Cadence

HR, training, and cadence workflows support the team that runs every other workflow — they don't depend on operational data and don't block any core process. Wave 8 means "build last," not "unimportant." Manager Onboarding (WF042) should be ready before any new CMs start, and Prospect Screening (WF045) needs to be live before Offer to Purchase (WF007) goes fully active.

WF043 Team Onboarding & Training Tracking Broader training log covering all staff and all training programs. QB record per training event per employee. Connects to WF031 (Fair Housing) and WF042 (Manager Onboarding). Compliance training completion tracked for legal audit purposes. QB Only
