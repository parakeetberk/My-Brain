1.1 Purpose

WF004 formalizes the move-out process across all 68 Parakeet Communities. It captures every move-out event — regardless of reason — through a single FastField form, routes it to the Regional Manager for approval, triggers a daily batch export to Rent Manager, and ensures a Move Out Inspection is completed within 48 hours and stored in SharePoint for review at the weekly Monday Asset Disposition meeting (WF003).

1.2 Scope

In ScopeAll 3 move-out triggers; FastField Move Out Form with Regional approval routing; QB Move Out Records table; daily RM batch CSV export; FastField Move Out Inspection with SharePoint storage; 48-hour SLA monitoring; WF003 feed (Asset Status = TBD / Needs Rehab triggers Monday meeting agenda item)
Out of ScopeSecurity deposit processing (stays in RM); eviction legal proceedings; resident re-application; new lease creation (WF005 Move In); any RM record changes other than the daily batch import
System of RecordRent Manager. QB is a workflow coordinator and batch collector — it does not own resident or financial records. Community Manager does not change any QB or RM status fields directly.

1.3 Triggers (All 3 — Same Path)

TriggerDescriptionWho Initiates
Voluntary NoticeResident submits written notice of intent to vacate. Manager confirms receipt and fills the Move Out Form.Community Manager
Lease Non-RenewalLease expires without renewal — either resident declines or Parakeet declines to renew. Manager fills the Move Out Form when the decision is made.Community Manager
Eviction OutcomeLegal eviction process concludes with possession returning to Parakeet. Manager fills the Move Out Form once the unit is vacated.Community Manager
Same workflow path for all three triggers. The reason is captured in the Move Out Form and flows through to the RM batch export for proper RM record categorization — but the QB workflow stages are identical regardless of trigger type.
Section 02Workflow Summary

WF004 runs two parallel tracks after the Move Out Form is submitted: the Approval & RM Export track and the Inspection track. Both must complete before the record is Closed. The inspection SLA clock starts from the move-out date regardless of the approval state.

Section 03Stage Detail

3.1 Returned Forms (Stage 2 → Back to Stage 1)

If the Regional Manager returns the Move Out Form in FastField (does not approve), the QB record reverts to Record Status = Initiated and a notification is sent to the Community Manager with the Regional's notes. The Manager corrects and resubmits in FastField. See open item O-WF004-005 for the exact return/correction flow confirmation.

3.2 Parallel Track Timing

The Inspection track (Stages 5–6) runs independently of the Approval track (Stages 2–4). The 48-hour inspection SLA clock starts from the move-out date field on the Move Out Form — not from the Regional approval timestamp. This means:

Inspection can be completed before approvalIf the manager completes the inspection on the day of move-out, it syncs to SharePoint immediately — the approval process runs in parallel and does not block the inspection.
SLA alert fires regardless of approval stateIf 48 hours pass from the move-out date and no inspection has been submitted, A-MO-06 fires whether or not the approval process is complete.
Closure requires both tracks completeA record cannot reach Stage 7 (Closed) until both RM Export Status = Exported AND Inspection Status = Complete.

3.3 RM Status Update After Export

Justin's team processes the RM import and updates the Asset Status in Rent Manager. QB reads the updated Asset Status as a reference-only lookup. When RM Asset Status = TBD or Needs Rehab, the unit becomes visible in QB as ready for the next Monday WF003 Disposition Meeting. The field recorder manually adds the unit to the WF003 meeting agenda (per D-030).

QB does not write to RM. All RM record changes — resident status, asset status, financial records, deposit processing — are handled exclusively by Justin's team via the batch CSV import. QB reads RM data for reference only.

Section 07RM Batch Export

WF004's primary output to Rent Manager is a daily CSV batch export of all approved move-outs from that day. Justin's team imports this CSV into RM to process all required field updates.

7.1 Export Logic

ScheduleDaily at 6:00 PM ET, every day (including weekends — move-outs occur any day)
Included RecordsAll Move Out Records where Regional Approval Status = Approved AND RM Export Status = Queued as of 6:00 PM ET
FormatCSV — column spec TBD (O-WF004-001). Expected columns: Asset ID, Community, Lot, Resident Name, Move-Out Date, Move-Out Reason, Forwarding Address, Notice Date, Notes, Batch ID
DeliveryAutomated email to Justin + RM team distribution. Email subject: "Parakeet Move Out Export — [Date] — [N] Records." CSV attached.
After SendQB sets RM Export Status = Exported and RM Export Date on all included records. Sets RM Export Batch ID = BATCH-YYYYMMDD.
Empty BatchIf no records are Queued at 6:00 PM ET, no email is sent. No empty CSV generated.
CorrectionsOnce exported, a record's RM Export status is immutable in QB. Corrections require a new amended Move Out Record with a note referencing the original MO-ID. The RM team handles the correction in RM directly.
Open Item O-WF004-001: Exact CSV column spec and RM import field mapping must be confirmed with Justin / RM Admin before A-MO-04 can be built. This is the critical build-phase dependency for this workflow.
Section 08Automations

WF004 uses seven QB Pipelines automations (A-MO-01 through A-MO-07). Automations A-MO-01 through A-MO-03 handle the approval track; A-MO-04 the RM export; A-MO-05 through A-MO-06 the inspection track; A-MO-07 closes the record.

IDNameTriggerAction
A-MO-01 Move Out Form Received FastField Move Out Form submitted (webhook) Creates QB Move Out Record from webhook payload. Sets Record Status = Pending Regional Approval; Regional Approval Status = Pending; Inspection Status = Not Started. Sends notification to Regional Manager: "Move Out form submitted for [Community] Lot [Lot] — [Resident Name]. Review required in FastField."
A-MO-02 Regional Approval Received FastField approval or return webhook If Approved: Sets Regional Approval Status = Approved; RM Export Status = Queued; Record Status = Approved. Confirms to Manager: "Move Out for [Resident] approved by [Regional Name]."
If Returned: Sets Regional Approval Status = Returned; Record Status = Initiated. Notifies Manager: "Move Out form returned by [Regional Name] — [Regional Notes]. Please review and resubmit."
A-MO-03 Inspection SLA Clock Start Move-Out Date reached (scheduled daily check) For all records where Move-Out Date = today AND Inspection Status = Not Started: sets Inspection Status = Pending. 48-hour SLA clock now running. Sends reminder to Manager: "Move Out Inspection due within 48 hours for [Community] Lot [Lot]. Please complete in FastField."
A-MO-04 Daily RM Batch Export Scheduled daily at 6:00 PM ET Queries all records where RM Export Status = Queued. If count > 0: generates CSV export, assigns RM Export Batch ID = BATCH-YYYYMMDD, emails CSV to Justin + RM team. Sets RM Export Status = Exported; RM Export Date = today; Record Status = RM Export Sent on all included records. If count = 0: no action.
A-MO-05 Inspection Submission Received FastField Move Out Inspection submitted (webhook) Receives webhook payload. Identifies parent Move Out Record via Move Out Record ID field. Pushes inspection file to SharePoint (confirmed path — O-WF004-004). Updates QB: Inspection Status = Complete; Inspection Date; Inspector Name; Inspection SharePoint Link. Triggers A-MO-07 check.
A-MO-06 Inspection Overdue Alert Scheduled daily at 8:00 AM ET Queries all records where Inspection Status = Pending AND Days Since Move-Out > 2. Sends alert to Community Manager AND Regional Manager: "Move Out Inspection overdue for [Community] Lot [Lot] — [N] days since move-out. Please complete in FastField immediately."
A-MO-07 Record Closure Inspection Status changes to Complete (triggered by A-MO-05) Checks if RM Export Status = Exported. If yes: sets Record Status = Closed. If no (RM export not yet sent — inspection completed before end-of-day export): record remains at current status and A-MO-07 re-evaluates after A-MO-04 fires that evening.
Section 09Gate Rules
IDRule NameApplies AtCondition & Error
G-MO-01 Approval Required for Export RM Export batch generation (A-MO-04) Condition: Regional Approval Status ≠ Approved.
Rule: Only records with Regional Approval Status = Approved are included in the daily RM CSV export. Pending and Returned records are never exported — they are silently skipped. No error shown; records simply remain in Queued state until approved.
G-MO-02 Both Tracks Required for Closure Record Status → Closed (A-MO-07) Condition: RM Export Status ≠ Exported OR Inspection Status ≠ Complete.
Rule: QB will not set Record Status = Closed until both the RM Export and the Inspection are complete. Record remains open and visible in reports until both conditions are met.
G-MO-03 Inspection Requires Parent Record FastField Inspection form submission Condition: Move Out Record ID field on Inspection form is blank or does not match an existing QB Move Out Record.
Error: "Inspection cannot be processed — no matching Move Out Record found. Ensure the Move Out Form has been submitted and the Record ID is entered correctly."
G-MO-04 Export Record Immutability Any edit to an Exported Move Out Record in QB Condition: User attempts to edit a Move Out Record where RM Export Status = Exported.
Rule: Key fields (Asset ID, Resident Name, Move-Out Date, Move-Out Reason, Forwarding Address) are locked to read-only after export. A note field remains editable for amendments. Corrections require a new Move Out Record referencing the original MO-ID.
