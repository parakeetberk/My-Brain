How Parakeet sounds, and how the world finds it. Brand and Marketing were two folders with two pages between them. They are one folder now, because voice and channel are the same job: the words a resident reads on a sign, a listing, a notice and the website all come out of the same place.

## Where this sits in the org

Marketing & Listings reports to the Director of Sales & Marketing, Leslie Croft, alongside Sales, Leasing & Special Projects. That is why brand, marketing and sales sit next to each other in this Brain: one director owns all three.

**Not in this folder, on purpose:** colours, typography and logo rules. Those live in the `parakeet-brand` skill and are applied automatically to every file deliverable. Duplicating them here would create two sources of truth.

**One derived asset, added 2026-09-02:** `branding-assets/parakeet-communities-logo-trimmed.png`, the wordmark cropped to its bounding box (2401x618). The supplied files in `branding-assets/Logo Files PNG (2026-09 quickbase drop)/` are **3600x3600 square canvases** with the wordmark in a narrow band, so placing one at a 0.6 inch height renders it too small to read - which is exactly what the first build of the roadtrip one-pagers did. **Use the trimmed file for any Word, Excel or PowerPoint deliverable.** The vendor drop folder is untouched.
