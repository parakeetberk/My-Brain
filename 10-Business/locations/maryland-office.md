# Maryland office

**What this is:** Parakeet's headquarters — Austin Berk's office, the home of Accounting, HR, Ops and back-office data, and the physical destination every original home title is mailed to.

**Status:** current — 2026-08-30

## The facts

- **This is HQ.** In the owner's words it holds **Accounting, HR, Ops and back-office data**. Austin Berk, CEO and co-founder, is based here.
- On the December-2025 org chart the office location is recorded as **Potomac** for the corporate roles based here — Accounting and Finance, HR, Titles and Licensing, and Asset Management data.
- **It is the third-party-facing control point in a real process, not just an address.** Across the title-transfer SOPs, the Maryland office — called "the MD office" or "the home office" — is where the physical paperwork lands:
  - **Original documents are signed in blue ink.** Three Powers of Attorney per home sale; the buyer signs all three in blue ink and scans go back to the Title Department by email.
  - **The originals are mailed to the Maryland office on a label provided by the Title Department or the Operations Coordinator.** Community Managers do not choose the address or the carrier; they request the label.
  - **Titles received back are uploaded to Assets in Rent Manager**, emailed to the Community and Regional Manager, and the asset is checked off on the make ready board where applicable.
  - The same rule governs eviction payments: a resident's cashier's check or money order is mailed to the home office on a label from the Operations Coordinator, and photographed for the Regional Manager and Evictions first.
- **If a title never reaches Maryland, the home cannot be resold.** The title-transfer chain terminates here, which makes mail handling in this office a real single point of failure in the home-sales process.
- **The Employee Handbook names a "Home Office Representative" as a travel-approval role**, alongside the Regional Manager — out-of-norm or last-minute travel, and air fare over $350 round trip, needs RM or Home Office Representative pre-approval.
- **Maryland is one of the six states covered by the handbook's state addendum**, alongside Alabama, Florida, Massachusetts, North Carolina and West Virginia.
- **The functions based here are the ones the owner names as his own lane.** Routing follows the office: anything field or resident goes to the Director of Property Operations, anything capital or investor goes to the CIO in Miami, and Accounting, HR and back-office data answer here. (See [[10-Business/people/how-we-work-together]].)
- **It is also where the accounting problem sits.** The function based in this office is the one the owner names as one of his top three problems — transactions not booked until month-end, leaving the company 30 to 45 days in arrears — and described on 2026-08-22 as "a black box. It doesn't communicate." Stated at function level, not as a verdict on any person.
- **The vault itself lives in the Maryland SharePoint estate**, synced by OneDrive to Austin and Jon. The migration to the Parakeet Birdcage SharePoint is run from here.

### The field is the third location

- **Neither office is where most of the company works.** There are **67 on-site community offices across three regions**, plus the corporate offices — roughly 125 employees in total against about 42 corporate.
- **The Director of Property Operations, Natalie Magurne, is based in New Smyrna Beach, Florida and on the road constantly.** Field ops is run from a car and a phone, not from either office.
- Most director-level roles are recorded on the org chart as **"Florida Remote"** rather than attached to Maryland or Miami.
- The practical consequence: any process that assumes a person is in an office — a signature, a physical document, a walk-over conversation — has to be designed for three locations, one of which is mobile.
