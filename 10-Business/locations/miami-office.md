**What this is:** Jon Wyss's office — Acquisitions, Asset Management, Capital Markets and Finance, and where the analyst bench sits.

**Status:** current — 2026-08-30

## The facts

- **This is the co-founder and CIO's office.** Jon Wyss runs it, and it holds **Acquisitions, Asset Management, Capital Markets and Finance**.
- **Zach Schwartz sits here — employee number one, Director of Asset Management & Acquisitions, and Jon's right hand.** In Austin's words, Zach is "Jon's Natalie."
- **The analyst bench is here:** Alex Fals (Acquisitions Analyst), Alex Stuart (Acquisitions & Asset Management Analyst) and Auzzi Dixon (Sales, Leasing & Special Projects) are all recorded with Miami as their office on the December-2025 org chart.
- **Auzzi Dixon is the only dual-reporting role on the chart** — to Leslie Croft and to Zach Schwartz — and in practice Zach oversees him when Leslie is not in the Miami office.
- **The two founders mirror each other:** one office each, one director as right hand each, and neither expects to be looped into the other's lane by default. Routing follows from that — anything capital, deal or investor goes to Jon; anything field or resident goes to Natalie. (See [[10-Business/people/how-we-work-together]].)
- **The office is referred to on the tour recordings simply as "the Miami office."** A 2026-08-22 call records that its energy is tied to who is physically present: "there is a little bit of a lull in the Miami office right now because Zach's clearly not there… when I am there, we're having fun, we're working a lot."
- **Two weeks of direct observation in the Miami office was the recorded plan for deciding a sales role in August 2026** — the assessment approach agreed on 2026-08-22 before any decision on fit, with Zach looped in to keep an eye on it while Jon was travelling.
- Deal work is done from here. The March 2026 board deck's acquisitions and capital-markets material — the JREFI recap, the Core+ vehicle, the Eastdil engagement, the WSRED earn-outs and the disposition slate — is this office's workload.

### The field is the third location

- **Neither office is where most of the company works.** There are **67 on-site community offices across three regions**, plus the corporate offices — roughly 125 employees in total against about 42 corporate.
- **The Director of Property Operations, Natalie Magurne, is based in New Smyrna Beach, Florida and on the road constantly.** Field ops is run from a car and a phone, not from either office.
- Most director-level roles are recorded on the org chart as **"Florida Remote"** rather than attached to Miami or Maryland.
- The practical consequence: Miami owns the capital side while the assets it underwrites sit in five states and are run by people it rarely sees in person.
