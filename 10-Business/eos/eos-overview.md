# EOS at Parakeet

**What this is:** how Parakeet runs on EOS — the quarterly rocks, the scorecard, the to-dos, the meeting cadence that reviews them, and where each one lives in this folder.

**Status:** current — 2026-08-30

## The facts

Parakeet runs on EOS (the Entrepreneurial Operating System). Four moving parts: **quarterly Rocks** per executive, a **scorecard** of KPIs, a running **to-do list**, and a **weekly Level 10 style executive meeting** where the first three get reviewed.

### The meeting cadence

- **Monday 8:30am — founders call.** Austin and Jon.
- **Tuesday 2:00pm to 5:00pm — team one-on-ones.**
- **Friday 2:00pm — Executive EOS call.**

Two standing rules, and they are rules, not preferences:

- **No meetings before 9:00am.**
- **No meetings without prior documentation or a stated reason.**

### How the three parts relate

- A **rock** is a quarterly commitment owned by one executive. Rocks are the work that moves the year. There are three quarters of rocks on file for 2026 (Q1, Q2, Q3) and they live on [[10-Business/eos/rocks-2026]].
- The **scorecard** is the weekly number, not the quarterly project. A rock finishes; a scorecard metric keeps getting measured. It lives on [[10-Business/eos/scorecard]].
- A **to-do** is a single action with one owner, usually due inside a week or two. To-dos are what come out of meetings and site visits. They live on [[10-Business/eos/to-dos]].
- **Department plans** hold the year-end goals, rocks, KPIs and named problems for each of the five departments, and the cross-departmental workflows that run between them. They live on [[10-Business/eos/department-plans]].
- Problems the Brain finds get raised on [[00-Meta/open-items]], with a risk rank, an owner and the page where the detail sits. Drafted emails routing a finding to the person who owns the rock go in `10-Business/eos/drafts/`.

### The review rhythm

- **Weekly, Friday 2:00pm** — scorecard read, then rocks on or off track, then to-dos. Anything that cannot be closed in the meeting becomes a to-do with an owner.
- **Weekly, Tuesday one-on-ones** — one person's to-do list and their rocks. The to-do register is grouped by owner for exactly this.
- **Quarterly** — score the closing quarter's rocks, set the next quarter's.
- **Ongoing** — the Brain routes findings. When a page turns up a problem, it goes on the open-items register, an email gets drafted to the rock owner, and the task gets an owner on the to-do register.

### Two things to know before using these pages

- **No rock in the vault carries a completion status.** There is no source anywhere in the Brain that says a 2026 rock is done, on track or missed. Every rock on the rocks page therefore reads **"not assessed"**, with an Evidence column saying only what the vault actually shows. A guessed status would be worse than a blank one.
- **The scorecard is not scoreable as it stands.** The KPI names live in the Rocks and KPIs spreadsheet and the numeric year-end targets live only in the March 2026 executive strategy deck. The two have never been reconciled to each other. This is the single biggest hole in the EOS setup and it is stated at the top of [[10-Business/eos/scorecard]].
