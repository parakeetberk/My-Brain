# 2026 Scorecard

**What this is:** what Parakeet measures in 2026 and what the target is — the KPI names by executive, and the numeric year-end targets, which live in a different file.

**Status:** current — 2026-08-30

## The facts

**Read this first. The KPI names and the numeric targets live in two different files and have never been reconciled.**

The KPI names are in [[80-Finance/planning/2026-executive-strategy/2026 Rocks & KPIs.xlsx]], column headed "2026 KPIs / Scorecard". That file contains **no numbers at all** — no targets, no units, no measurement frequency, no definitions of how any metric is calculated. The numeric year-end targets are in [[80-Finance/planning/2026-executive-strategy/Parakeet - Executive Strategy Meeting v1.pptx]], on the five department overview slides, where they appear as goal statements rather than as scorecard rows. Nothing links the two.

**A scorecard with metric names but no targets cannot be scored.** Today nobody can open the scorecard file and say whether a number is good. Two of the five executives with rock rows carry no KPIs at all, and none of the named KPIs has a definition, so even the metrics that do exist would be measured differently by different people. Fixing this is Zach Schwartz's Q1 rock, "Internal KPI Reporting (EOS Scorecard)", which is why that rock matters more than its one-line description suggests. See [[10-Business/eos/rocks-2026]].

### What this comparison shows

- **Six of the twenty targets have no KPI name to attach to**, including the two hardest year-end commitments in the plan: order ~230 new homes and list ~400 homes.
- **Every Accounting & Finance and Asset Management target is unmeasured on the scorecard**, because David Berman, Zach Schwartz and the joint David & Zach row all have blank KPI cells. That is a whole side of the business — K-1s, the insurance RFP, healthcare, cash reporting, utility optimisation — with year-end commitments and no scorecard line.
- **Three targets are a different shape from the metric they would score.** Eviction turnover is a duration measured against a count; kick-off is measured against a completion rate; the home sale target carries a 30% maximum loss that the metric name ignores.
- **Nothing in either file states a reporting frequency.** EOS scorecards are read weekly; the only cadence stated anywhere is the Friday 2:00pm Executive EOS call ([[10-Business/eos/eos-overview]]).
- **No current value exists in the vault for any of these metrics.** Not one KPI has a measured number filed against it, which is why every rock on [[10-Business/eos/rocks-2026]] reads "not assessed".
