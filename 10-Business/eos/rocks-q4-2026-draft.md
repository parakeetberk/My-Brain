### The SMART test each rock below has to pass

A rock is SMART here only if all five hold. Most rocks in the 2026 file fail on M and T, which is why none of them can be scored.

- **S** — one sentence, one outcome, one owner. If two people own it, it is two rocks or it is nobody's.
- **M** — a number and a denominator. *"Complete the mapping"* is not measurable; *"classify 6,379 of 6,379 lots"* is.
- **A** — the person has the hours and the dependency has landed. Noted per rock.
- **R** — names the 2026 goal or the register item it serves.
- **T** — a date, and dated milestones so 10/31 is a real checkpoint.

And one addition, because it is where these usually die: **the deliverable**. A rock is not done when the work feels done. It is done when a named artifact exists, in a named place, that someone other than the owner can open and check.
