# Department plans 2026

**What this is:** one section per department — the stated 2026 goals verbatim, the rocks, the KPIs and the problems the March 2026 deck named out loud — plus the cross-departmental workflows that run between them.

**Status:** current — 2026-08-30

## The facts

Source is the March 2026 executive strategy deck, [[80-Finance/planning/2026-executive-strategy/Parakeet - Executive Strategy Meeting v1.pptx]]. The goals come from the five DEPARTMENT OVERVIEW slides (50 to 54), quoted verbatim. The problems come from the deck's own deep dive slides on each department, also verbatim, plus what the vault holds today.

### How the departments fit together

The deck's framing, and it is a load-bearing one: **"CROSS-FUNCTIONING DEPARTMENTS ARE CRITICAL"** and **"MAJOR WORKFLOWS SPAN ALL DEPARTMENTS — AM + ACCTG & FINANCE ARE CENTRAL HUB."**

Five workflows cross every department: **Home Sale · Home Demo · Vacant Lot In-fill · Capex Project · Home Rehab.**

Who does what on all five, verbatim from the deck:

- **Property Operations** is the initial source for project initiation, in collaboration with other departments.
- **Sales & Marketing and Construction** lead execution of their respective projects, with communication and collaboration.
- **Accounting & Finance** is involved in all projects from start to finish, reviewing and processing invoices.
- **Asset Management** sets the tone and is tracking projects to budget, reporting on progress and holding departments accountable.

The consequence worth naming: five workflows, five departments, and every one of them runs through Accounting & Finance and Asset Management — which are the two departments with **no KPIs on the scorecard** and, in Asset Management's case, **no documented processes at all** (band 400 to 499 of the SOP library is an empty scaffold). The hub is the least measured part of the plan.
