```
Subject: SOP library, where we actually are

Natalie,

I went through the whole SOP library this week and I want to give you the
real picture before we talk. Two things stood out and they point in
opposite directions.

The library is in better shape than it looks from the outside. There are
109 processes tracked, the nine band numbering scheme is real and it holds
up, and 22 processes have actual documents behind them. That is not a pile
of drafts. Your Q1 rock was to list all department processes across sales,
operations and construction. That one is done.

Here is the problem. One of the 109 is approved. Just one, demo and lot
prep, and even that one carries no approver name and no approval date. So
your Q3 rock, complete process documentation and implementation across all
departments, is sitting on a foundation nobody has signed. The gap between
Q1 and Q3 is the whole issue, and it isn't a writing problem. It's a
decisions problem.

Four things are blocking approvals and none of them get fixed by drafting
more documents.

1. Florida's cure period has four different numbers in four live SOPs, all
citing the same statute. 5 business days in 100, 10 business days in 107,
7 days plus the mailbox rule in 802 and 803, and 5 days in the old 800. We
can't approve 100, 107 or the Florida block until counsel gives us one
number. Wrong cure period voids the eviction, so this is the one I care
most about.

2. Massachusetts and North Carolina have no compliance SOPs. Both are live
states. SOPs 100 and 101 already carry the statutory detail, including the
Massachusetts 72 hour disclosure package and the five year lease offer,
with nothing reviewed sitting behind it.

3. There is no Chapter 513 process anywhere in the library. RV is a big
piece of what we own, and the Florida SOPs explicitly route RV to Legal,
which in practice routes it nowhere. Geiger Key, Great Oak and River Oaks
have no documented path on increases, notices, violations, evictions or
deposits.

4. Two closing processes are both live and they disagree. The newer one
adds a Title Coordinator role and a make ready board gate the older one
doesn't have. Nobody can tell me today whether that role exists. Same mess
means 002 has no sales SOP at all, only flowcharts.

One more, separate from all of that and more urgent. SOP 302 publishes a
lender's full wire instructions, account name, account number, bank and
ABA routing number, in a document that circulates through the wiki. That
needs to come out this week. I'll take that one with David.

I'd rather work these with you than hand you a list. Can we take the first
three in our Tuesday one on one and get counsel on the phone the same
week? What I need from you is which attorney you want on the Florida and
RV questions, and your call on the closing process, since you and Leslie
own that jointly and it only takes one of you to decide.

The rest of what I found is cleanup. I don't think any of it needs you
personally.

Austin
```
