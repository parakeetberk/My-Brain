# Management incentive plan (MIP II)

**What this is:** the Parakeet HoldCo II MIP — the equity incentive plan for the JREFII platform: what the instrument is, how distributions split, how units vest, what forfeits them, and the fact that every award document on file is still a blank form.

**Status:** current — 2026-08-30. Plan effective 2024-10-11.

## The facts

### The vehicle

- **Parakeet HoldCo II MIP, LLC** — a Delaware limited liability company, **organised 2024-10-10** and **effective as of 2024-10-11**. Counsel is **Lowenstein**; the execution versions are signed by **Bryan LaPlant**.
- **The instrument is non-voting "Management LLC Units," structured as profits interests** within the meaning of Rev. Proc. 93-27 and Rev. Proc. 2001-43. Each unit's participation is limited to the extent necessary to keep it qualifying as a profits interest.
- **The structure is two-tier: the MIP LLC holds Class P Units in Parakeet HoldCo II, LLC, and distributions flow HoldCo → MIP LLC → participants.** Participants hold interests in the MIP LLC and are **not members of HoldCo** — they have no HoldCo voting or information rights.
- **The Asset Manager is Parakeet MHC, LLC**, under the **Asset Management Agreement of the same date (2024-10-11)**, in which HoldCo II is Owner and Parakeet MHC is Manager. Definitions of Cause and Good Reason for the Asset Manager are inherited from that agreement.
- **"Business" is defined as** "the business of, directly or indirectly, acquiring, re-developing, developing, owning, operating and monetizing (i) manufactured housing communities across the United States, and (ii) real estate opportunities adjacent to manufactured housing communities, including RV parks, marinas, and campgrounds." That definition sets the scope of the restrictive covenants.

### How money reaches a participant

- **Distributions split 50/50: 50% to the JREF Entities holding vested units, 50% to the Other Participants holding vested units**, each pro rata to vested units held.
- **If no Other Participant holds vested units, the JREF Entities take the whole distribution.**
- **Participants receive nothing until all HoldCo members other than the MIP LLC have received distributions equal to all HoldCo capital contributions.** The MIP sits behind the return of all invested capital.
- Each award also carries a **Distribution Threshold** per unit, adjustable on a HoldCo Crystallization Payment Date.

### Vesting and exit

- **20% vests on the first anniversary of the grant; the remaining 80% vests in 48 equal monthly instalments over the four years following that first anniversary** — five years to full vesting.
- **JREF Entity units vest in full at grant.**
- **On a Sale of HoldCo, an active employee who is offered continued employment receives 67% of the distribution at closing and the 33% balance on the first anniversary**, conditional on accepting the offer and remaining in continuous employment for those 12 months.

### Forfeiture and covenants

- **Termination for cause, or resignation without good reason, forfeits everything — vested and unvested.**
- **Breach of a restrictive covenant forfeits everything and triggers clawback** of amounts already distributed.
- **The Restricted Period runs to the first anniversary of the latest of** (1) consummation of a Sale of HoldCo, (2) termination of the participant's employment or service, or (3) the date the participant ceases to hold any units — and it is tolled during any alleged breach.
- **A Section 83(b) election is mandatory.** A participant who fails to file a fully executed 83(b) election with the IRS, and provide a copy to the Company and the Manager, **within 30 days of issuance** gives the Company the right to **cancel all or part of their units for no consideration.**

### What is not filled in

- **Every award document in the folder is a blank form.** The Form of Award Agreement carries `Awardee's Name: ____________`, `Number of Management LLC Units Awarded: _____`, `Date of Award: ___________` and `Distribution Threshold: $[●]`. **No awardee is named and no unit count or threshold is filled in anywhere in the folder.**
- **No unit pool size is stated.** The Unit Pool is "such number as is set by the HoldCo Manager from time to time," and **the Manager may change the aggregate number at any time** — so there is **no anti-dilution protection** for a participant, and no way to compute what a given unit count is worth as a percentage.
- **There is no MIP I folder** in the vault despite the MIP II naming, and no MIP I documents anywhere.
- **The folder holds seven documents and no executed award:** two execution versions (the LLC Agreement with the Incentive Plan attached, and the Incentive Plan), three counsel redlines, and two versions of the blank Form of Award Agreement.
- **Related, and separate from this plan:** the March 2026 board deck's equity table footnotes that most of what it calls "equity compensation" is **cash compensation linked to equity ownership**, and that only a small number of positions are "true MIP." Those per-person figures are not reproduced here.
