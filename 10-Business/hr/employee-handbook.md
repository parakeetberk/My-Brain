# Employee handbook

**What this is:** the readable layer over the Parakeet Communities Employee Handbook — the mission and core values as written, the leave and benefit terms, the escalation and signing authority, and the two gaps that belong to HR.

**Status:** current — 2026-08-30. Source document has no version or effective date; see the gaps below.

## The facts

### Who it is from

- **Employer entity: Parakeet Communities LLC** ("Parakeet" or "the Company"). Signed by **Austin Berk, CEO** and **Jon Wyss, CIO**.
- **12 sections across 61 pages, plus Appendix A, a state addendum.** Drafted by outside counsel (Doner Law, PLC) with HR.
- Employment is **at-will** throughout, and nothing in the handbook creates a contract of employment.

### Mission and values, verbatim

- **Mission: "To bring outstanding, affordable living to our current and future residents without sacrificing quality and efficiency."**
- **Eight core values: Do the Right Thing · Be Open Minded · Fly as a Flock · Put Customers First · Invest Time Wisely · Keep it Simple · Make Informed Decisions · Have Fun.**
- **"The flock" is defined and it is deliberately wide:** "Our team includes our residents, employees, vendors, and stakeholders—together we are a 'flock,' working together and soaring together to new heights."

### Time off

- **Vacation: 5 days / 40 hours annually, full-time employees only** (scheduled 40+ hours per week). Accrues from the start date but **not usable until the 90-day introductory period ends**. **Carryover capped at 5 days**; anything above that is forfeited. **Never paid out on termination except where law requires.** Requests are due **at least two weeks before** the leave begins.
- **Sick leave: 5 paid days, front-loaded as a lump sum on the hire anniversary**, usable after the 90-day introductory period, **no carryover** unless law requires it. **Sick leave must be exhausted before vacation is used for a sick-qualifying reason.**
- **9 paid holidays:** New Year's Day, MLK Day, Presidents' Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day, Christmas Eve, Christmas Day. Saturday holidays are observed the preceding Friday; Sunday holidays the following Monday.
- **Bereavement: 5 days paid** for regular full-time employees.
- **FMLA: 12 weeks, with paid leave running concurrently.** Military (USERRA), jury duty, court attendance and voting leave are all covered separately.

### Pay and benefits

- **Payroll is biweekly, every other Friday, through Paychex**, by direct deposit. If a payday falls on a holiday the date shifts.
- **Overtime is 1.5× the regular rate for hours worked over 40 in a workweek**, with holidays, vacation and sick days excluded from the 40-hour calculation. **Unauthorised overtime is a disciplinary matter** — it must be approved in advance, in writing, by the manager.
- **Health, dental and vision at 30+ hours per week, effective the first day of the month following 60 days of employment.** Plans are viewed on Paychex.
- **401(k) at age 21 with 3 months of service.**

### Authority and escalation

- **The escalation spine is employee → manager → HR → CEO.** The CEO is the terminal escalation where HR is the subject of the complaint or fails to act: a harassment or discrimination complainant "must promptly elevate the matter to the Chief Executive Officer," and an accommodation denial can be appealed to the CEO.
- **"No contracts are to be signed by anyone but the CEO, unless otherwise authorized by the CEO in writing."** The handbook adds: no automatic renewals, and most agreements are month to month.
- **Only the CEO may promise guaranteed or continued employment**, and only in writing.
- **The spend authority matrix is in the handbook**, split into facility/repair approvals and credit-card authorisation levels, running from District Manager up to CEO/CIO at $5,000 and above with dual approval. **The full table lives on [[10-Business/people/how-we-work-together]]** — read it there rather than restating it.

### Travel and vehicles

- **Hotels at or below $109.00 per night before tax and fees.** All travel needs written supervisor approval by email before booking; out-of-norm or last-minute travel and air fare over $350 round trip need Regional Manager or Home Office Representative pre-approval.
- **Per diem: $10 breakfast, $20 lunch, $25 dinner** per person per day. Breakfast is excluded where the hotel provides it, and per diem may not be pooled into one meal without advance approval.
- **Itemised receipts are required, marked with the Community Name and business purpose**, and a meal receipt must list every person covered.
- **Hired-auto insurance is never waived** — Parakeet does not carry hired auto physical damage coverage, so anyone using a rental for Company business must purchase both hired auto liability and hired auto physical damage from the rental company. Mileage forms must be submitted within 15 days or they are not reimbursed.
- **Residents are never transported in an employee vehicle.** Rental cars for Community Managers require prior Regional Manager approval and are for out-of-region or out-of-state travel only; no family members may travel in a rental car unless approved in advance in writing.

### Two gaps, both belonging to HR

- **The handbook carries no effective date, no revision date and no version number anywhere in 61 pages of policy.** The only date in the document is an e-signature timestamp — **08/29/2024 11:48:41 EDT** — on the acknowledgement page. There is no way to tell which version an employee signed, or whether a given policy is current.
- **The state addendum covers Alabama, Florida, Maryland, Massachusetts, North Carolina and West Virginia — and omits Georgia and South Carolina**, where the company operates communities and holds dealership licences. Both states appear in the operating SOPs with their own statutory notice periods and deposit clocks; neither appears in the handbook.
