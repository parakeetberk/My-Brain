**What this is:** Director of Sales & Marketing — owner of sales, marketing, listings and the approval gate on every offer and closing.

**Status:** current — 2026-08-30

## The facts

- Director of Sales & Marketing, reporting to Austin Berk. 12+ years of experience — Blair, Cove, Bedrock.
- **Team on the December-2025 org chart:** Auzzi Dixon (Sales, Leasing & Special Projects, dual-reporting to Leslie and Zach Schwartz), Tonya Thompson (Marketing & Listings), and Melanie Sanchez-Soto — though Melanie in fact sits under Natalie Magurne, and the chart is wrong there. - **Process owner** on SOP 001 (home ordering, delivery and set — "Sales owns this process"), SOP 002 (home sales), and jointly with the Director of Operations on SOP 003 (closing prep) and SOP 006 (lead intake and screening).
- **What she decides without Austin:** approval of an offer or a closing, within 24 hours of receiving the PSA or LOP, with the closing date not moving if the buyer misses the e-sign window (SOP 003); park-owned-home expenditure over $1,000 in the POH approval workflow in AVID (SOP 300); home listing readiness, with cleaning as the trigger gate. She owns the seven-part repo data pack and the 1-page Sales summary due within 3 business days of a lender repossession notice, and escalates to David Berman if the pack is incomplete (SOP 302). She and David co-lead the mandatory post-mortem on every repo notice.
- **2026 rocks:** Q1 — implement the CRM, get the sales department trained and processes fully implemented, stand up robust sales and leasing reporting, get the standalone RV websites and the Parakeet Retreats page live for RV site marketing, finish the website fixes. Q2 — explore AI and other technology to enhance the sales organisation. Q3 — sell.
- **2026 department goals** from the March deck: order roughly 230 new homes, list roughly 400, sell roughly 400 at a maximum 30% loss, finish the CRM implementation, and get to a fully functioning sales organisation.
- **Current state:** the pace is outside the company's comfort zone, so Jon Wyss has stepped in to help and Austin has gotten involved too. That is a pace problem, not a verdict on capability — the CRM was a Q1 rock and MAV only landed in August 2026, which is the shape of the issue.
- **How to work with her:** give her the target and she runs with it. She pushes back, and that is useful. Follow-through needs checking.
