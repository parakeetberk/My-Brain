# Clarissa Andres

**What this is:** Operations Coordinator and admin, offshore — the person who runs two of the company's hard monthly clocks.

**Status:** current — 2026-08-30

## The facts

- Operations Coordinator and admin. Offshore. Named in the March-2026 board presentation under Property Operations; the org chart's "Ops Support" page is empty, so she appears in the SOPs but not in the drawn structure.
- **The month-end clock (SOP 301).** After the Director of Operations approves the Month End Tracker Report on the 29th, Clarissa distributes the Excel summary to Justin Minkoff, copying the Regional Managers and the Director. Once Justin confirms all updates are entered in Rent Manager, she performs the final verification check that everything is correctly updated. Two control steps in one process.
- **The collections clock (SOP 100).** On the 6th of each month she produces the state-specific demand notices in Rent Manager, which Community and District Managers then post or mail. Every state has a different cure period and a different statutory notice — Florida, South Carolina, Georgia, Massachusetts and North Carolina — and Massachusetts requires a 30-day termination notice built around the most tenant-protective timeline in the portfolio.
- **Why the clock matters:** both the collections cadence and the eviction process depend on the 6th happening. Intake forms are submitted the day after the notice deadline, so a late notice shifts the whole eviction chain.
- She also generates every notice from the state templates and calendars the deadlines in the FL, GA and SC compliance SOPs.
- **How to work with her:** she is the operational metronome, not an escalation point — she executes a fixed calendar. If a date needs to move, that is a Director of Operations decision, not hers.
- **Structural note:** SOP 301 flags that "Clarissa" is defined nowhere in the SOP library — she holds two control steps and appears in no role table or org description. This page is the fix.
