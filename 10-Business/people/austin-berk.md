# Austin Berk

**What this is:** CEO and Co-Founder, based in the Maryland head office, owner of operations (property and corporate), finance, legal and HR.

**Status:** current — 2026-08-30

## The facts

- CEO & Co-Founder, Parakeet Communities. Maryland office — HQ, and the home of Accounting, HR, Ops and the back-office data function. Co-founder is Jon Wyss, who runs the Miami office.
- On the December-2025 org chart he sits at the top of the Property Operations column alongside Jon Wyss. His one drawn direct report on that chart is Demetria Loukas, HR Manager.
- **What he owns:** operations, both property and corporate; finance; legal; HR. The visionary and the systems builder of the two founders.
- **Current obsession:** process, procedure, and pushing AI and automation through every corner of the company so the team runs like a well oiled machine.
- **His team** is the executive team plus Justin Minkoff and Albert. The core OG team inside that is Austin, Zach Schwartz, Jon Wyss and Natalie Magurne.
- **What only he can decide:** no contract may be signed by anyone but the CEO unless he authorises it in writing, and only the CEO may make promises about guaranteed or continued employment, effective only in writing and signed by him (Employee Handbook). CEO/CIO spend authority is $5,000 and above with dual approval.
- Joint decision-maker with David Berman on which of the three lender repossession purchase options Parakeet takes, by the internal deadline two weeks ahead of the lender's expiration date (SOP 302). He and David also still owe that SOP its missing consequences-of-delay terms.
- **Cadence:** Monday 8:30am founders call with Jon. Tuesday 2–5pm one-on-ones. Friday 2pm Executive EOS call. No meetings before 9am. No meetings without documentation or a stated reason. 7–8am weekday school run.
- **Working style:** direct, no fluff. Wants holes poked in ideas rather than agreement — critical beats agreeable. Ships finished v1s rather than drafts; hand him a working version he can react to, not a half-built thing open to debate. Builds himself with AI tools rather than delegating technical work, and wants to understand every step — no black boxes.
- **How to work with him productively:** break work into the smallest actionable step. Long explanations stall progress. Everything needs backup and proof; assumptions are not affordable.
- **Structural observation:** decision rights between the two founders are undefined, and he names that as a problem — see the Related link below.
- **Structural observation:** he is the integrator on most named initiatives at once, and project state currently lives in at least three places — email, ClickUp and the vault.
