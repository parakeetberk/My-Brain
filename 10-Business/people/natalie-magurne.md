# Natalie Magurne

**What this is:** Director of Property Operations — owner of all of field ops, and in practice the go-to for anything that needs doing across the company.

**Status:** current — 2026-08-30

## The facts

- Director of Property Operations, reporting to Austin Berk. 20+ years of experience — Vornado, Pacific Retail Capital. Based in New Smyrna Beach, FL and on the road constantly.
- **Span of control:** three Regional Managers — Ashlyne Godinez (AG), Liznette Santiago (LS) and Alex Pontes (SR) — 29 on-site community managers, and the property-level maintenance staff under them. The whole resident lifecycle sits with her.
- **Process owner** on SOP 100 (AR & collections), 101 (lease renewal), 102 (move-out), 103 (move-in), 106 (regional 30-day site visit), 301 (rent roll & vacancy approval, as approver), and all six Florida compliance SOPs plus the four Georgia and four South Carolina ones. She also owns SOP 300 (Avid Exchange AP) as Director of Operations, and shares 003 and 006 jointly with the Director of Sales.
- **What she decides without Austin:** written pre-approval for Regional Manager facility spend over $500 up to $1,500, and Regional Manager card spend over $300 (Handbook); the Standard Regional Workflow approval on AP invoices at any amount (SOP 300); approval of the Month End Tracker Report on the 29th, which releases the month-end data to Justin Minkoff for system entry (SOP 301); the final corrective-action decision on every incident report, and whether an incident triggers the insurance claim process (SOP 108); co-sign with HR on any disciplinary action or PIP (SOP 704); the final approval step in the QuickBase workflow chain agreed on 2026-08-18 — community manager, then regional or district manager, then Natalie.
- She receives eviction proofs of service and the intake packets that start the eviction process, and is the escalation point when a Regional Manager is unreachable (SOP 108) or a state's applicable notice period is unclear (SOP 100).
- **Austin's words: "she is our director of everything."** She is the go-to for any and all information, and the person he sends things to when something needs doing that is not strictly hers. He also thinks she would be a perfect executive assistant but would not take her out of her role to do it.
- **How to work with her:** structured calls plus random contact all the time. She flags things before they go wrong and gives heads-ups. She also does not tell him things and just gets them done.
- She owns the SOP initiative itself — the March-2026 deck states it plainly, with quarterly rocks running from listing every department process, through flowcharting and documenting them, to complete SOPs and implementation across all departments.
- **Capacity to watch:** three regionals, 29 managers, the entire resident lifecycle, and the fourteen-SOP FL/GA/SC compliance block, all at once. She is the named owner or approver in more SOPs than anyone else in the library.
