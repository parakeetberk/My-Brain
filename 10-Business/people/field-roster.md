### What the chart gets wrong or leaves out

- **Belle Haven's manager is contradicted three ways.** The December-2025 chart says TBD. The August 2026 site visit names a **Julie** on site and credits her with sourcing windows and mini blinds out of the property sheds. The Infill sheet says **"Roman is the manager."** All three are recorded here; none is resolved.
- **Lucky Clover appears on the AG Region page with no manager connector at all** — a property in the region with nobody drawn against it.
- **Four communities have no manager and no region entry anywhere on the chart:** Geiger Key RV Resort, Silver Breeze, Regency I & II, and McGregor Downs. Regency I & II and McGregor Downs were acquired in April and May 2026, after the chart was drawn; Geiger Key and Silver Breeze predate it.
- **A property called "SPACE COAST VILLAGE" appears under Rhonda Skelly and matches no row in the Property Data Sheet.** It may be an alias for a Space Coast property or a stale entry; unresolved.
- **Laurel McKay reports to Jordan Young rather than directly to the regional manager** — the only field manager on the chart with a manager above her who is not a regional.
- **The SR Region page draws a reporting line for only 3 of its 10 managers** — Vicki Buck, Josh Flowers and Rose Hernandez. The other seven appear with property connectors but no line to a regional manager.
- Manager counts by region: AG 12 direct plus Laurel McKay under Jordan Young; LS 6; SR 9 named plus Belle Haven TBD.
