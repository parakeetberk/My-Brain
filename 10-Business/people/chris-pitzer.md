# Chris Pitzer

**What this is:** Facilities Manager under the Director of Construction — regional construction projects, maintenance training and compliance.

**Status:** current — 2026-08-30

## The facts

- Facilities Manager, reporting to John Clark, Director of Construction. One of three, with David Jones and George McGuire, covering 67 communities between them.
- **What he owns:** regional construction projects, plus maintenance training and compliance for the property-level maintenance staff in his area.
- **The readiness gate is a Facility Manager duty.** He completes the Phase 1 Construction/Installation inspection on a newly set home within one week of the vendor's set notification, submits it as the Phase 1 JotForm, and Phase 2 cannot begin until the Director of Construction approves it (SOP 203). He also completes the utilities, mechanical and escalation sections of the readiness checklist.
- He conducts, completes, signs and submits the site condition assessment on any lot identified for potential new home placement (SOP 202).
- **What he decides without Austin:** facility and repair spend up to $3,000, reviewable against the Director of Operations for the first 90 days in role (Handbook). Whether a home passes Phase 1 inspection, item by item — every item is Yes or N/A, and any No needs a written note and resolution before sign-off.
- **Structural note:** SOP 200a lists "Facilities Manager (FM)" and "Facility Manager (FM)" as two roles sharing one abbreviation. They are the same role.
