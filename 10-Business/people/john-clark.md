**Status:** current — 2026-08-30

## The facts

- Director of Construction, reporting to Austin Berk. 15+ years of experience — Lakeshore. Known throughout the company as JC.
- **Three Facilities Managers report to him:** Chris Pitzer, David Jones and George McGuire — regional construction managers overseeing projects plus maintenance training and compliance. George McGuire has no drawn connector on the December-2025 chart but sits in the Facilities Manager row; David Jones is mis-grouped in the org chart JSON as carrying the CIO title, which belongs to Jon Wyss.
- **Process owner** on SOP 200 and 200a (demo and lot prep, demo-to-infill — he authorises every phase transition), SOP 201 (new home delivery and set), SOP 202 (site condition assessment — he schedules, reviews and decides) and SOP 203 (new home readiness). He is named in SOP 001 as the Construction executor on home ordering.
- **What he decides without Austin:** CapEx approval on any invoice of any amount routed to the CapEx Workflow in AVID (SOP 300); Gate 1 clearance on a new home — Phase 2 does not start until he has approved the Phase 1 JotForm and all failed items are corrected and re-inspected (SOP 203); whether a lot passes site condition assessment and what happens next (SOP 202); Facilities Manager spend up to $3,000 sits with his managers, reviewable against the Director of Operations for their first 90 days (Handbook).
- Point on the Oakside/Hershey roadwork warranty dispute — pursuing the warranty claim against the roadwork vendor, gathering prior communications, and pricing the drainage repair independently through national paving vendors.
- **2026 targets from the March deck:** kick off 150+ community improvement projects, complete 100+ rehabs across the portfolios, and deliver seamless lot prep, delivery and installs of roughly 200 homes. Site surveys were already done on roughly 400 lots ahead of schedule.
- **Capacity is the stated constraint** — three facilities managers covering 67 communities against those targets.
- **How to work with him:** deep boots-on-ground credibility. If he says a site is not ready, it is not ready — take it as fact and plan around it.
- **The weak spot is documentation** — photos, permits, COIs, before-and-afters. The March-2026 Executive Strategy deck calls the before/after photo documentation standard for rehabs and CapEx projects "non-negotiable", and it still slips.
- He escalates late. Build the check-in into the schedule rather than waiting for the flag.
