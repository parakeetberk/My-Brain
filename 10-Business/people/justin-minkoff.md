## The facts

- Asset Management & Data Analyst. **The December-2025 org chart puts him under Zach Schwartz. In practice he reports to Austin, with Zach as the backstop because Austin gets distracted.**
- **What he owns — the Rent Manager gate.** Asset records and status, the UDF fields, Make Ready Board issues, Prospect-to-Resident transitions, and month-end system updates. Nothing becomes true in the system of record without going through him.
- **Where the SOPs name him:** updates Rent Manager within 24 hours of any date change from Sales, and is the immediate notification if a delivered home does not fit its assigned lot (SOP 001); moves Prospect to Resident and marks the asset inactive once funds are confirmed (SOP 003a, 004); receives the approved month-end Excel summary from Clarissa Andres and enters all updates in Rent Manager, with his confirmation being the trigger for her final verification (SOP 301); posts attorney fees to a resident account before any eviction-stopping payment is accepted, and receives title on a voluntary turn-in (SOP 107). The 800-series eviction SOP also has him locking and unlocking resident accounts, which SOP 107 says only Evictions and the Director of Operations may do — an unresolved contradiction.
- **What he decides without Austin:** what actually gets entered in Rent Manager and in what form. In a company where three transcriptions sit between the reviewer and the system of record — CM annotates a PDF, Regional retypes into Excel, Justin retypes into Rent Manager — he is the last check.
- **How to work with him:** tech-capable, and one of the few people in the company who can build as well as operate. He is the natural owner for the 600s Systems / IT / Reporting SOP band, which is currently empty.
- **Open issue:** he spends time cleaning up other departments' data problems, and Austin wants that stopped. It is a symptom of missing data-entry standards, not of him being too helpful. The fix is standards at the point of entry — which is what the FastField and QuickBase work is for — not more cleanup capacity.
- Anything Rent Manager goes to Justin. Most other data, systems and tech goes to Zach Schwartz.
