# Melanie Sanchez-Soto

**What this is:** Titles, Inventory & Licensing — the Closing Coordinator in every closing SOP, and the single point through which every closing and title transfer runs.

**Status:** current, 2026-09-15. Two September items added by the Page Update; the rest is as of 2026-08-30.

## The facts

- Titles, Inventory & Licensing. **Technically under Natalie Magurne** — the December-2025 org chart places her under Leslie Croft, which is wrong; the owner corrected it on 2026-08-30.
- **What she owns:** deal-type routing on every home sale (Cash, Financed, Lease with Option to Purchase); compilation of the closing package; POAs; SignEasy e-signature execution; Florida Fast Title coordination; mailing and UPS labels; and title updates in the rehab flows.
- **Named in the SOPs as:** Closing Coordinator (SOP 003, 003a, 004), Operations Coordinator (SOP 001, 003a scope list, 107), and Title Coordinator in the May-2026 closing generation. She maintains the Main Eviction Cases Sheet per SOP 107's role table, and Community Managers request mailing labels from her to send eviction payments to the home office.
- **What she decides without Austin:** which of the three deal paths a signed PSA or LOP routes to, and what goes in the package for that path and that state — including the Florida-only Prospectus and the South Carolina Form 400 and 500. She is the one who knows which documents wet-sign and which go through SignEasy, in a set of SOPs that contradict each other on exactly that point for Georgia.
- **How to work with her:** reliable and high-volume. Give her the deal and the state and she assembles it. The 24-hour e-sign window and the same-day Move-In Request both run through her, so she is the person who knows where a closing actually is.
- **Single point of failure — the most concentrated one in the company.** Every closing and every title transfer routes through her, and no one else in the SOP library does this work. The master tracker lists "Title Work Process (Master)" as a Tier 1 Critical gap, partially drafted, covered only piecemeal by SOP 005, the post-eviction SOP and the resident turn-in SOP. That gap and her being the only person who knows how are the same fact.
- The mitigation is a documented backup: one named second person, trained, and the master title process written down.
- The title function is called five different things across six documents — Title Department, Evictions, Closing Coordinator, Operations Coordinator, Title Coordinator and Corporate Title Specialist — and no document defines the desk or says who staffs it.

- **Georgia is blocking the dealer's license on the Georgia authorized agent requirement**, and Melanie is approaching Wendy Owens to fill it (2026-09-08). `TD-0614`. The board deck's *"licensing cleared in FL, SC and GA"* on `50-Products-Sales/home-sales` is therefore not the current state for Georgia.
- **The Parakeet Home Sales renewal needs the surety bond continuation and garage liability**, which Melanie asked Berman and Moe for on 2026-09-08 (`TD-0615`), and it compounds `TD-0537`, how many bonds *"several others"* is, asked three times and retired unanswered.
