# How we work together

**What this is:** the routing map — where a thing goes, who decides it, and where decisions get recorded. The most useful page in this folder.

**Status:** current, 2026-09-15. Three routing facts observed in September added by the Page Update under *Routes seen in practice*; the rest of the page is as of 2026-08-30.

## The facts

### The core team

- Austin's words: **"my team is the executive team plus Justin and Albert. But the core OG team is Austin, Zach, Jon and Natalie."**
- Executive team: Austin Berk, Jon Wyss, Natalie Magurne, Leslie Croft, John Clark, Zach Schwartz, David Berman, Demetria Loukas. Plus Justin Minkoff and Albert.

### Where things go

- **Anything field or resident → Natalie Magurne.** Field ops, on-site managers, the resident lifecycle, compliance, process.
- **Anything capital, deal or investor → Jon Wyss.** Austin does not need to be in it.
- **Rent Manager specifically → Justin Minkoff.** Asset records, UDFs, the make ready board, month-end entry.
- **Most other data, systems and tech → Zach Schwartz.**
- **Process → Natalie Magurne.** She owns the SOP initiative.
- **Something that needs doing with no obvious owner → depends what it is.** Melanie Sanchez-Soto, Demetria Loukas or Albert; all three are capable.
- **Anything that must move now on Austin's own priorities → Albert.** His priorities, no queue.

### The two offices

- **Maryland (Austin) — HQ:** Accounting, HR, Ops, back-office data.
- **Miami (Jon):** Acquisitions, Asset Management, Capital Markets, Finance.
- Natalie Magurne is Austin's operating right hand. Zach Schwartz is Jon's — Austin's words, Zach is "Jon's Natalie."
- **The two founders mirror each other:** one office each, one director as right hand each, and neither expects to be looped into the other's lane by default. Austin's words: "connected at the hip, work wives, brother from another mother. Trust each other to handle what is needed without communication but both try to over-communicate."

### Decision rights, as documented

- **Only the CEO signs contracts** — no contract may be signed by anyone but the CEO unless he authorises it in writing. **Only the CEO may promise guaranteed or continued employment**, and only in writing, signed by him. (Employee Handbook.)
- **Director of Sales approves offers and closings within 24 hours** of receiving the PSA or LOP; if the buyer misses the e-sign window the closing date does not move. (SOP 003.)
- **The Regional Manager is the sole approver of a payment stipulation** with a delinquent resident. A Community or District Manager may prepare and present one but must not communicate approval before RM sign-off. (SOP 100.)
- **The Director of Operations approves the Month End Tracker on the 29th**, which releases the month-end data to Justin Minkoff for entry in Rent Manager. (SOP 301.)
- **Disciplinary action needs HR plus Director of Operations sign-off before it is issued**, or it is not valid; no Regional Manager may independently issue a PIP. (SOP 704.)
- **CapEx invoices route to the Director of Construction at any amount; park-owned-home spend over $1,000 routes to the Director of Sales; everything else to the Director of Operations.** A $950 POH invoice stays standard. (SOP 300.)
- **Austin and David Berman jointly decide the lender repo option**, by an internal deadline two weeks ahead of the lender's expiration date. (SOP 302.)
- **Spend authority (Employee Handbook):**
  - District Manager — $250 facility and repair, $150 company card (after 90 days).
  - Regional Manager — $500 facility; over $500 up to $1,500 needs written Director of Operations pre-approval, email only, verbal not accepted. $500 card, with anything over $300 approved by the Director of Operations.
  - Maintenance Manager — $300; anything over $300 approved in advance.
  - Facility Manager — $3,000, reviewable against the Director of Operations for the first 90 days.
  - Assistants to Jon and Austin — $200 after 90 days of training, $500 thereafter.
  - CEO / CIO — $5,000 and above, dual approval required.
  - Do not break a cost into two invoices to get under a level.

### The gap

- **Decision rights between the two founders are undefined, and Austin says so.** The March-2026 Executive Strategy deck makes the same complaint one layer down — "clarity on who owns what, close the gap on cross-departmental communication. This must be clear as day." It applies to the founder layer too. This is an open structural item.

### Where decisions get recorded today

- Scattered across email, ClickUp, decks, transcripts, and sometimes nowhere. Austin named this as the problem.
- The fix is `00-Meta/decisions-log.md` — one dated line per decision, with a source, so a decision has one home instead of five.
