## The facts
- **What they supply:** audit and tax. Confirmed by the owner on 2026-08-30.
- **Who they work with:** David Berman (Corporate Controller / CFO promote) and the accounting team — Moe Mehmood on monthly closings and corporate accounting, Derek Flynn on treasury analytics, plus offshore property and AP accountants. Investor-facing delivery runs through the fund reporting cadence.
- **The March 2026 board commitment is explicit on both halves: "Finance & accounting – Improved audit/tax…"** in the 2026 goals, and **"K-1 DELIVERIES IN MAY – On-time and accurate delivery."** May is the stated target for K-1s.
- The 2025 goals recorded the same thing as work in progress: "Finance & accounting – Major improvements underway across the board wrt/**taxes, audit**, financial review and reporting." Improving audit and tax has been a live commitment across two planning cycles.
- The supporting infrastructure the accounting team built for it: a **detailed fiscal-year compliance calendar covering payroll, debt, tax and property tax deadlines**, and **WIP accounts and codes to track CapEx by asset, property and project**.
- **The scope is large: roughly 78 entities.** The structure as stated on the 2026-08-21 Rellit call is two holding companies, two funds and a property management company, with each of about 72 properties in its own SPV with its own LLC and bank arrangement, plus five or six more entities handling home sales and title ownership. The vault holds **430 entity files** across formation documents, filings and agreements.
- **How critical: high.** Audit and tax are not optional for a fund structure with institutional LPs and lender covenants — K-1s have a delivery date investors hold the company to, and audited numbers underpin the recapitalisation and Core+ vehicle plans. A firm change mid-cycle would be months of pain re-establishing scope across ~78 entities, not a week.
- **The constraint on them is Parakeet's close, not their work.** From the 2026-08-21 Rellit conversation: books close **30 to 45 days in arrears**, and John's summary of five years of trying — "making sure that books are closed, making sure that everything is done on time. From our perspective… it's been a massive struggle still." An audit is only as fast as the close feeding it.
- Related open items the deck lists alongside audit and tax: **real-time cash reporting** (monthly internal cash planning), a monthly close described as "working towards robust… for real-time reporting," and property tax reassessments budgeted across the assets bought in 2025, with tax strategy named as a hoped-for source of beat in 2026.
- **Known problems:** no engagement letter, audit report, tax return or K-1 appears anywhere in this vault, so the scope, fee basis and deadlines of the relationship are not documented here — only the commitments in the board deck and the owner's confirmation.
- There is no accounting or finance SOP band beyond the 300s operational set (AVID AP, rent roll, lender repo, utility activation): **no month-end close SOP for Accounting**, no audit-preparation process, no tax calendar SOP. The tracker lists "Month End Close (Accounting)" as **not started**, and SOP 110 covers only the community-manager side of month end.
- SOP 302's own status block undercounts its open items, and its interim tracker is an email thread — the kind of control gap an audit tends to find.
