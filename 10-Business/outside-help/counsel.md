- **How critical: high, and it is the reviewing capacity that is the constraint, not the firms.** Deal counsel is what lets the acquisition and refinancing plan run — the 2026 goals include closing the JREFII refinancing, recapitalising JREFI, launching a Core+ vehicle, expanding into GA, SC, NC, VA and AL, and exiting the legacy asset base. And the compliance side is where the business is most exposed with the least cover, described below.
- **The open need — 14 state compliance SOPs all sit at "counsel review" and none has been signed off.** Florida **801–806** (rent increases and pass-throughs, violations, notice service, prospectus, rules changes, deposits), Georgia **811–814** (rent and term changes, dispossessory, notice service, deposits) and South Carolina **821–824** (rent increases, violations/eviction/abandoned home, notice service, deposits). All fourteen read "DRAFT V1 — Pending Review," drafted 2026-08-06, each routed to that state's counsel **before team review**.
- **The biggest counsel-shaped gap in the vault: Massachusetts and North Carolina are live operating states with no compliance SOPs at all** — confirmed by the owner on 2026-08-30, so this is a live compliance gap, not a documentation artifact. SOPs 100 and 101 already carry full statutory detail for both, including the Massachusetts **72-hour pre-signing disclosure package** and the **bona fide five-year lease offer** under MGL c. 140 § 32P — flagged in the library as the most tenant-protective law in the portfolio — with no counsel-reviewed SOP, no template and no compliance page behind any of it.
- **A third gap sits beside it: RV.** SOPs 801, 802 and 804 explicitly exclude or route RV parks — "RV parks are Chapter 513 — no prospectus applies; route to Legal" — and **no Chapter 513 / RV compliance SOP exists anywhere**, despite RV being a large part of the portfolio.
- **Where the statutory numbers do not reconcile, field staff are following the operating SOPs, not the compliance block.** Florida's cure period has four live values across the library; South Carolina's rent increase notice is 90 days in SOP 101 and 30 days with a 60-day company standard in SOP 821, both citing SC Code 27-47-420; Georgia's 60-day notice cites 44-7-60 in one SOP and 44-7-7 in another.
- **A hard compliance gate no operating SOP implements:** SOP 804 makes prospectus delivery with a signed receipt the gate on every Florida lot tenancy and says the Rent Manager move-in checklist blocks on it — and SOP 103 (Move In) has no prospectus step. The FL/GA/SC deposit SOPs set 15- and 30-day clocks running from termination of tenancy, and SOP 102 (Move Out) has no deposit step.
- **Communities with no prospectus on file must STOP and route to Legal on any rent increase: Geiger Key RV, Great Oak RV, Hunter Springs, Nalls Court, Paradise Lakes, River Oaks RV, Silver Breeze.**
- **Known problems on the routing side:** the eviction process exists at two numbers — 107 (Operations) and 800 (the legacy Legal placeholder) — and **the owner was asked on 2026-08-30 and deferred the decision**, so cure period, account-unlock authority and attorney-submission date each have two answers. Georgia's abandoned homes route to "the Corporate Title Specialist and Legal" with no SOP behind that path. Disciplinary action carries an explicit multi-state legal warning (SOP 704) with **no state employment guidance anywhere in the library** — the 800s block is tenancy law only.
