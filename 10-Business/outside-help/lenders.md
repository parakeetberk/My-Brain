## The facts
- **What they supply:** mortgage debt on every property SPV, plus the banking and treasury structure the funds run on.
- **Who uses it:** Austin Berk and Jon Wyss on structure and refinancing; David Berman on debt administration, covenants and treasury; Zach Schwartz on acquisitions, dispositions and the earn-out DSCR tests; Accounting on payments, wires and the compliance calendar.
- **The affiliates org chart tags every property with a bank or lender code.** Five codes appear: **ACCESS**, **BAML**, **FSB**, **CNB** and **PRIVATE**.
  - **ACCESS** — tagged on most JREFI and JREFII properties. **The owner confirmed on 2026-08-30 that ACCESS is Parakeet's banking relationship.** The 2026-08-21 Rellit call describes it concretely: banking runs through **J.P. Morgan Commercial Access**, with two main physical accounts for the funds and **virtual accounts underneath each for every special purpose vehicle**.
  - **BAML** — Bank of America Merrill Lynch; tagged on **Everglades and Royal Palm**.
  - **FSB** — **confirmed by the owner as Geiger Key's lender.** The board deck's debt summary names it in full: **First State Bank**, $9,150,000 originated Jan-24 at 2.75% + SOFR, 24-month I/O then 25-year amortisation, maturing Jan-28.
  - **CNB** — tagged on **Hendersons and Nalls**. **The owner confirmed on 2026-08-30 that CNB is not active.** Corroborating this, David Berman's Q1 2026 rock list includes **"Transition Henderson's and Nalls"** — the same two properties.
  - **PRIVATE** — the Origins and Massachusetts properties.
- **The mortgage lenders are a different, separate set from the banking tags, and the two do not line up.** The board deck's debt summary names **Walton Street** (the WSRED portfolio loans) on both the JREFI and JREFII portfolio loans, First State Bank on Geiger Key, and a **Seller** note on Silver Breeze. A loan-by-loan schedule filed in the archive lists **74 loans across 13 lenders**: Walton Street on JREF I (20 loans) and JREF II (21) with **Key Bank as debt administrator**; **UBS** on 7 loans with **Trimont** as debt administrator — including **Everglades, Royal Palm, Hendersons and Nalls**, the BAML- and CNB-tagged properties; **Key Bank – Fund II** on 7; **Ascent** on the 10 Parakeet Origins loans; **Capital One** on Lamberton Lake and Mayflower; **Post** on Mayflower mezzanine debt; **Valley National**; **Evermore**; a **CMBS/Wells Fargo** loan at Parkwood; and three seller-financed notes.
- **Terms as the board deck states them:** JREFI portfolio loan **$113,050,000**, originated May-24 at **2.85% + SOFR**, full-term interest-only, maturing **Jan-27** with two 1-year extensions. JREFII portfolio loan in three tranches — **$62.5m (May-25), $18.175m (Jul-25), $54.297m (Aug-25)** — all at **2.75% + SOFR**, full-term I/O, maturing **May-28** with two 1-year extensions.
- **Both portfolios are fully financed with flexible pre-pay floating-rate debt and no maturities until mid-2027.** Earn-outs are DSCR-gated: the first two JREFI WSRED earn-outs were disbursed in Q3 2025; the second two ($2.875m) and the first two JREFII earn-outs ($4.4m) were expected to be met in Q1/Q2 2026.
- **Interest expense ran $1.4m over budget in 2025** — $8,825,916 against $7,421,990, **+19%** — because **the SOFR curve did not come down as anticipated in 2025.** On floating-rate, full-term interest-only debt, that variance goes straight to portfolio cash flow, which came in 25% under budget.
- **The Northgate WWTP replacement is a lender-required repair.** $600,000, estimated completion August 2027, overhauling the first train and adding two digesters — "as part of our lender required repairs." Lender obligations reach into the CapEx plan, not just the balance sheet.
- **How critical: absolute, and unlike a vendor there is no substitution.** Every property sits in its own SPV with its own loan and its own bank arrangement; the debt is what the portfolio is built on, and the 2026 plan depends on it — closing the JREFII refinancing, recapitalising JREFI and launching a Core+ vehicle. Banking is equally structural: the two physical accounts with per-SPV virtual accounts underneath are how money moves at all.
- Response time to lenders is treated as a relationship asset in its own right: SOP 302 states that **"Parakeet's response time on repossession notices is tracked by lenders and directly impacts the company's ability to secure future line increases and strategic business opportunities."**
- **Known problem — there are no loan or mortgage documents anywhere in the 430 entity files.** No credit agreement, note, mortgage, guaranty, or lender-required-repair schedule is filed in `10-Business/entity-structure/`. The debt terms in the board deck have **no supporting documents in this vault**; the only loan-level detail here is a spreadsheet in `99-Archive/rock-processes/David/MASTER Debt Overview.xlsx`, which is archived raw material, not a filed record of the agreements themselves.
- **Known problem — SOP 302 publishes live wire instructions.** The home lender repo SOP prints a lender's full mailing address, account name, account number, beneficiary bank and ABA routing number in a document circulated through the process wiki. Its missed-deadline consequence, payment timing and Repo Tracker are all **[TBD]**, with an email thread as the interim tracker.
