## Summary

`Corporate` is 19.03 GB across 78 top-level children, and the headline number is misleading: **17.56 GB (92.3%) is four raw photo/survey dumps**, PCA site photography and survey rasters for Regency I, Regency II and Riverwalk, sitting in `Replacement Cost Model\_source_data\`. Those are verbatim duplicates of due-diligence material that already exists in `/sites/main/Shared Documents/`; the single 157 MB Riverwalk PCA PDF alone was found in four other locations on the main site. Strip the duplicated source corpus and what remains is roughly **923 MB, and that remainder is almost entirely original**: a bespoke replacement-cost valuation framework (three shipped institutional-grade reports for Jadian Capital), a 242-row provenance-tagged unit-pricing library built by mining executed invoices, a governance layer (Parakeet Brain, Parakeet OS), and a first-party Claude usage/cost economics practice that exists nowhere else in the estate. The branch is **live, not an abandoned batch run**: the newest file is dated 2026-08-26, one day forward of survey. The single biggest problem is that ~95% of the storage is a redundant working copy of documents already held of record, while the ~5% that is irreplaceable original synthesis has no backup, no export, and sits interleaved with the bulk it is buried under.
