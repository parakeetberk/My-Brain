### Named problems

- **Massachusetts and North Carolina are live operating states with no compliance SOPs at all.** SOPs 100 and 101 already carry full MA and NC statutory detail — including the **MA 72-hour pre-signing disclosure package** and the **bona fide five-year lease offer** under MGL c. 140 § 32P, flagged in the library as the most tenant-protective law in the portfolio — with no counsel-reviewed process, no template and no compliance page behind any of it. The owner confirmed on 2026-08-30 that both states are live.
- **No Chapter 513 / RV compliance process exists anywhere.** SOPs 801, 802 and 804 explicitly exclude or route RV parks — "RV parks are Chapter 513 — no prospectus applies; route to Legal" — **and Legal has nothing to route to.** RV appears in the operating SOPs only as a $500 checkbox on the eviction intake form. See [[50-Products-Sales/rv-and-transient]].
- **Florida's cure period has four values across four live SOPs**, all citing FS 723.061: 5 business days (SOP 100), 10 business days (SOP 107), 7 days plus a 5-day mailbox rule (802/803), 5 days (the legacy 800). **A wrong cure period voids an eviction.** Field staff are following the operating SOPs, not the compliance block.
- **South Carolina's rent increase notice is 90 days in SOP 101 and 30 days with a 60-day company standard in SOP 821, both citing SC Code 27-47-420.** Georgia's 60-day notice cites 44-7-60 in SOP 101 and 44-7-7 in SOP 811.
- **The eviction process exists at two numbers and neither is authoritative.** 107 (Operations, Aug 2026) and 800 (the older Legal placeholder) are both on disk and disagree on cure periods and on who may unlock a locked account. **The owner was asked on 2026-08-30 and deferred the decision.**
- **A hard compliance gate that no operating SOP implements.** SOP 103 has no prospectus step. The FL, GA and SC deposit SOPs set 15- and 30-day clocks running from termination of tenancy, and SOP 102 has no deposit step.
- **Georgia's abandoned homes route to "the Corporate Title Specialist and Legal"** with no SOP behind that path, and that title function is called five different things across six documents with no defined desk.
- **Artefacts the compliance processes depend on do not exist:** the Posting Certification, the lease delivery-clause matrix, the Prospectus Review Tracker and the Tenancy Lease Acknowledgment are all named in SOPs and none is in the library.
- **No state employment guidance exists anywhere.** SOP 704 carries an explicit multi-state legal warning; the 800s block is tenancy law only.
- **The reviewing capacity is the constraint, not the firms.** Fourteen documents at counsel review, three states covered, two live states uncovered, and RV uncovered entirely.
