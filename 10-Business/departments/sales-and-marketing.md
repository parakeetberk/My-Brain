# Sales & Marketing

**What this is:** the department that lists and sells homes, runs the marketing channels and approves every offer and closing — SOP band 000–099, and the department the company itself calls its binding constraint into 2027.

**Status:** current — 2026-08-30

### Named problems

- **002 has no sales-process SOP document at all.** The file filed under it, `002-combined-home sale process.docx`, is actually the *closing* process — so closing is written up three times in two live generations and the core sales process exists only as Lucid flowcharts. Tier 1 Critical ([[70-Operations/sops/002-home-sales]]).
- **The sales infrastructure was already at capacity before the paid channel launched.** On 2026-08-24: "We haven't even launched Google Ads and the team's infrastructure is already overwhelmed with the amount of calls."
- **August is dead and the ramp is October.** Same call: "nobody's buying in August, right? I mean Florida's a snowbird market… I really expect the influx of demand to come. Let's just say starting in October." The team gave itself five weeks to align, for a strong 90 days into year end.
- **Infill has outrun sales.** By 2026-08-24, 130 homes delivered and 40 listed and ready — ahead of plan — so the constraint moved to sales, described on the call as the last frontier and, over the next six months, the determinant of the recap.
- **The sales org is being restructured, not staffed up.** Dedicated sales and leasing agents distinct from community managers, on the Fox Creek model, at high-volume properties like Vantage Oaks and potentially across the East Coast — treated as permanent structure rather than a spike hire, sized against turnover of more than 3% a year across around 7,000 units.
- **Transaction coordination has no defined process** (March deck): "Define process of transaction start-to-finish," "Process lead," "Avoiding delays," "Define triggers & timing." The make ready board's update cadence by silo is undefined, and sale-versus-rent recording is not tracked.
- **Sell versus rent is an open policy question** (March deck): "At what # of homes available for sale per community do we start pushing rentals? Is there a time-on-market threshold as well?" RTO structure and price-drop triggers are both undefined.
- **Pay is being converted from commission to a written policy.** As of 2026-08-22 a member of the sales team had not signed the official job description, the performance-based bonus policy was still unwritten, and NAV system training had not happened.
- **There is no lender-registration process anywhere in the library** — no SOP, no tracker, no owner, no list of which communities are registered with which lender — against a documented mortgage denial caused by exactly that gap ([[10-Business/vendors/triad]]).

### Handoffs

- **To Construction** — the order and the delivery date. Sales owns SOP 001 and Construction executes it; Justin Minkoff updates Rent Manager within 24 hours of any Sales date change, and is the immediate notification if a delivered home does not fit its lot. See [[10-Business/departments/construction]].
- **To Property Operations** — jointly owns SOP 003 and SOP 006, and hands the approved buyer to move-in. The Move-In Request goes in the same day as closing.
- **To Accounting & Finance** — Accounting confirms funds and the floorplan payoff, which is the hard gate on marking the asset inactive. The March deck names this seam as its own problem box: "ACCOUNTING Workflow Integration – Where is the disconnect?"
- **To Asset Management** — Prospect moves to Resident and the asset deactivates only after payment is confirmed. Sales reporting is dependent on the CRM being finished.
- **To Legal & Compliance** — the state-specific closing package (Florida prospectus, South Carolina Forms 400 and 500), and any rent increase at a community with no prospectus on file.
