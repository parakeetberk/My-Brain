**What this is:** the department that runs the 67 communities day to day and owns the entire resident lifecycle — SOP band 100–199, the most documented and most contradicted process set in the company.

**Status:** current — 2026-08-30

### Named problems

- **The AR and eviction drag is concentrated in the LS region.** Austin's own portfolio performance analysis found it there. The context that belongs beside it: almost every property in that region was acquired in 2025, so it is stabilisation rather than maintenance ([[10-Business/people/liznette-santiago]]).
- **Cleanliness standards in the field.** On 2026-08-22 the AG region was judged stable but least improved, with a dirty clubhouse and a dead cockroach called unacceptable: "our residents shouldn't walk into a clubhouse and see a dead cockroach on the floor."
- **Florida's cure period has four different values across four live SOPs**, all citing FS 723.061 — 5 business days (SOP 100), 10 business days (SOP 107), 7 days plus a 5-day mailbox rule (802/803), 5 days (the legacy 800). A wrong cure period voids an eviction. Critical, and it blocks approval of three documents ([[00-Meta/open-items]]).
- **Span of control.** Three regionals and 29 managers across seven states, the entire resident lifecycle, the fourteen-SOP compliance block and the SOP initiative itself — all on one director, who is the named owner or approver in more SOPs than anyone else in the library.
- **1 of 109 tracked processes is approved**, and this department owns most of the rest. Nineteen documented conflicts remain unresolved ([[70-Operations/sops/sop-library]]).
- **Two live operating states have no compliance SOPs.** Massachusetts and North Carolina, confirmed live by the owner on 2026-08-30, with MA carrying the most tenant-protective law in the portfolio.
- **The March deck's own complaint, verbatim:** "Clarity on who owns what - close the gap on cross-departmental communication. **This must be clear as day.**" Plus "identify where current processes are breaking down or causing duplicated work" and "ops team optimization - structure, headcount, roles."
- **Three transcriptions sit between the field reviewer and the system of record** on month-end: the community manager annotates a PDF, the regional retypes it into Excel, Justin Minkoff retypes it into Rent Manager.
- **Equipment starvation in the field** shows up as an operations problem as much as a construction one — repeated asks across the tour for zero-turn mowers, a small excavator, a gator or UTV, a basic tool package and a shed, all still unassigned on [[10-Business/eos/to-dos]].

### Handoffs

- **To Construction** — Property Operations is the initial source for project initiation (March deck). Rehab, lot prep and readiness all start with a field observation. See [[10-Business/departments/construction]].
- **To Sales & Marketing** — joint ownership of SOP 003 and SOP 006; the community manager or sales rep submits the offer; the manager makes the financing referral.
- **To Accounting & Finance** — the month-end close chain, the AP approval routing in AVID, and utility activation on new and rehab homes.
- **To Asset Management** — the month-end data, the Make Ready Board, and every occupancy and delinquency number that lands on the scorecard.
- **To HR** — same-day written notification on any incident involving personnel, and joint sign-off on any discipline.
- **To Legal & Compliance** — Natalie is the process owner on the whole FL/GA/SC block, so the handoff is internal to her; the external leg is outside counsel.
