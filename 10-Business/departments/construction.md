# Construction

**What this is:** the department that turns a vacant lot into a set, trimmed-out, occupiable home and executes the capex plan across 67 communities — SOP band 200–299, and the only band with an approved SOP in it.

**Status:** current — 2026-08-30

Every item is Yes or N/A; any No needs a written note and resolution before sign-off.
- **Approval rights that sit here.** **CapEx invoices route to the Director of Construction at any amount** in the AVID workflow (SOP 300). He authorises every phase transition in demo and lot prep (SOP 200/200a), and decides whether a lot passes site condition assessment (SOP 202). Facilities Manager spend runs to **$3,000**, reviewable against the Director of Operations for their first 90 days (Handbook).

### Named problems

- **Homes delivered and left unset.** At Applegate, homes delivered 2026-07-22 and 2026-07-23 were still not set five weeks later, and Champion was a no-show for the second time, blocking at least two more homes ([[70-Operations/meetings/2026-08-southeast-tour/2026-08-19-applegate-site-visit]]). Install quality defects were logged at Paradise Palms (stair and door orientation, exposed wood, missing caps) and Riverwalk (door, stair and tread defects).
- **Permitting is the long pole, and the inspectors are inconsistent.** At Vantage Oaks permit processing was **three weeks behind**; the Okeechobee building department requires two copies of each home's installation manual per packet and physical pickup of paid permits within 72 hours. **One inspector approved a 12-inch pipe pre-pour and a second required 15 inches and a specific concrete slope, forcing a demolition and a re-pour and costing about six weeks.**
- **No expediter will take the work.** On 2026-08-21: "I've spoken to multiple expediters… Not a single one of them will touch mobile home permits. They're too much of a headache and they're too small potatoes." The mitigation in place is a relationship-driven approach with building officials, with weekly calls.
- **Documentation lags the work.** The March deck calls the before-and-after photo standard for rehabs and capex "non-negotiable" and asks for "project documentation standards - one pager per project, photos, description, etc." It still slips — photos, permits, COIs and before-and-afters are the department's named weak spot.
- **Utility companies push code-change costs onto the owner**, and utility locating is a live cost: Paradise Lakes excavation was **stopped on 2026-08-19** pending a roughly $30,000 utility locating and mapping job, after a machine tipped over and rubber boot connectors were pulled loose.
- **Equipment starvation in the field.** Repeated asks across the tour, all still unassigned on [[10-Business/eos/to-dos]]: zero-turn mowers (Regency II, Gulf Breeze), a small excavator, a gator or utility vehicle (Shady & Druid, The Oaks), a basic tool package and a shed (The Oaks).
- **Order tracking, in the deck's own words:** "Order schedule - where does it stand? What's pending, what's delayed? **Better tracking is needed!**" and "Who will be doing the lot prep and home setup? Champion?"
- **A competing ID scheme runs through the 200s** — 200a is CONST-002, 202 is CONST-003, 203 is CONST-004 in its banner and CONST-203 in its table, then cross-references CONST-202 and CONST-200, neither of which exists. None of it aligns with the nine-band numbering scheme.
- **Capacity is the stated constraint:** three facilities managers covering 67 communities against 150+ projects, 100+ rehabs and ~200 lot preps and installs.

### Handoffs

- **From Property Operations** — project initiation. Ops is the initial source; Construction leads execution (March deck).
- **From Sales & Marketing** — the home order and the delivery date under SOP 001. Sales owns that process; Construction executes it. See [[10-Business/departments/sales-and-marketing]].
- **To Sales & Marketing** — the readiness gate. A home is not listable until it passes; the alternate closing generation puts a Make Ready Board gate on the deal itself, with the Director of Construction confirming home status.
- **To Accounting & Finance** — CapEx invoices at any amount route to him for approval, and unit-by-unit home cost tracking (lot prep, home, delivery, setup) is the accounting back end the March deck says must be 100% accurate.
- **To Asset Management** — CapEx Tracker 2.0 is the instrument every project reports into, and Asset Management holds the department to budget.
