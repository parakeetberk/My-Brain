# HR

**What this is:** one HR manager covering hiring, onboarding, the handbook, benefits and discipline for roughly 125 employees across six states plus offshore. SOP band 700–799 is empty except one orphan document.

**Status:** current — 2026-08-30

## The facts

- **HR Manager: Demetria Loukas**, reporting **directly to Austin Berk** — the only drawn direct report to the CEO on the December-2025 org chart. The chart's column heading groups Accounting, Finance & Human Resources together, but her line runs to Austin, not to David Berman. See [[10-Business/people/demetria-loukas]].
- **Headcount she covers:** roughly 125 employees in total, of whom roughly 42 are corporate. The offshore and support group is roughly 20 people across three departments and makes up close to half of corporate ([[10-Business/people/offshore-team]]). States: FL, SC, MA, GA, NC, AL, plus the Maryland head office and the Michigan property being sold.
- **What it owns:** hiring, onboarding, the Employee Handbook, benefits, the disciplinary process, and incident notification whenever Parakeet personnel are involved.
- **SOP band 700–799 is EMPTY except one orphan.** [[70-Operations/sops/704-disciplinary-action-forms]] is the only file in the band. It has **no SOP ID inside the document, no header table, no version, no effective date, no revision history, no process owner and no status field** — the "704" exists only in the filename — and it has no row on the master tracker. The published wiki's HR folder reads "coming soon" and its 700-series README says "no processes published yet."
- **What SOP 704 does establish, and it is real authority.** **HR — not the Regional Manager — decides the level of corrective action.** Regional Managers gather facts only, blank disciplinary forms are held by Regional Managers alone, and **no written discipline or PIP is valid without HR plus Director of Operations sign-off before it is issued to the employee.** No Regional Manager may independently issue a PIP.
- **The incident clock.** She is the required **same-day written notification recipient on any incident involving Parakeet personnel**, and leads the investigation on employee-related incidents (SOP 108). Resident-related incidents are led by the Regional Manager instead. See [[70-Operations/sops/108-incident-reporting]].
- **What only the CEO can do, which is an HR-relevant limit:** no contract may be signed by anyone but the CEO unless he authorises it in writing, and **only the CEO may make promises about guaranteed or continued employment, effective only in writing and signed by him** (Employee Handbook).
- **The Handbook is the department's main published artefact** and it defines the culture in its own words: "Our team includes our residents, employees, vendors, and stakeholders—together we are a 'flock,' working together and soaring together to new heights," with **"Fly as a Flock"** as a named value. See [[20-Brand-Marketing/online-presence]] for how the same word is used to residents.
- **The Handbook also carries the entire spend-authority table** — District Manager $250 facility / $150 card after 90 days; Regional Manager $500 facility, $500 card with pre-approval thresholds above; Maintenance Manager $300; Facility Manager $3,000; assistants to Jon and Austin $200 then $500; CEO/CIO $5,000 and above with dual approval — plus the rule that a cost must not be split into two invoices to get under a level. Full list on [[10-Business/people/how-we-work-together]].
- **2026 rocks:** **none on file.** Demetria Loukas has no row in the 2026 Rocks & KPIs sheet. The HR-adjacent commitments sit under Accounting & Finance instead: "RESTRUCTURE HEALTHCARE BENEFITS – Renewal in July" is David Berman's Q2 rock.
- **KPIs on the scorecard: none.** HR has no scorecard line ([[10-Business/eos/scorecard]]).
- **Systems it runs on:** Paychex for payroll and benefits administration ([[10-Business/vendors/paychex]]); the AppOne careers portal for job postings; SanMar for uniforms ([[10-Business/vendors/sanmar]]); SharePoint for personnel files. The offshore staffing model is separate and unowned by any documented process.
- **What she is used for beyond HR:** she is one of three people (with Melanie Sanchez-Soto and Albert) Austin considers capable of picking up something that needs doing with no obvious owner. She sat in on the Q2 commission payouts and cross-checked who got paid.

### Named problems

- **One HR manager for roughly 125 people across six states plus offshore.** Two other SOPs route into HR without naming a contact: **SOP 108 lists the HR notification email as literally [TBD]** in a process built on same-day HR notification, and SOP 122 sends PPE non-compliance to "the standard performance improvement process" with nothing behind it.
- **The Employee Handbook has no effective date, no revision date and no version number.** An undated handbook is hard to enforce and harder to defend. Critical on [[00-Meta/open-items]].
- **Its state addendum omits Georgia and South Carolina** — it covers Alabama, Florida, Maryland, Massachusetts, North Carolina and West Virginia. The company operates communities in both GA and SC and **holds dealership licences in both**.
- **Onboarding and training are all on the tracker gap list.** Manager Onboarding and Team Onboarding & Training are Tier 2 with nothing started; Online Onboarding and Triplog Training are Tier 3 with nothing started; "Hiring / Onboarding Responsibilities (HR vs Ops)" is an untiered stub with the split itself undefined.
- **No state employment guidance exists anywhere in the library.** SOP 704 carries an explicit multi-state legal warning, and the 800s compliance block is tenancy law only ([[10-Business/outside-help/counsel]]).
- **No training material exists anywhere in the company.** All 27 `_Video Links.txt` files in the SOP library are empty templates against a schedule that had every populated process covered by the week of 2026-09-28. Zero videos have been recorded.
- **A written bonus policy is owed to HR.** Commission-style payouts were decided in August to convert to a written performance-based bonus policy, in writing rather than on an Excel sheet, so HR has a filed policy covering payout conditions.
- **Field turnover is high enough to be a structural planning input:** more than 3% a year across around 7,000 units, and 13 field employees replaced or upgraded in the six months before the March 2026 board presentation.

### Handoffs

- **To Property Operations** — joint sign-off on every disciplinary action and PIP, and the incident process runs between them. See [[10-Business/departments/property-operations]].
- **To Accounting & Finance** — payroll, benefits and the healthcare restructure. Payroll is allocated by property and geography in the payroll model. See [[10-Business/departments/accounting-and-finance]].
- **To Sales & Marketing** — the Loan Coordinator job description and the dedicated sales and leasing agent roles both come to HR to post and fill, with about four weeks of lead time to have people seated.
- **To Legal & Compliance** — the multi-state employment question SOP 704 raises and nothing answers.
