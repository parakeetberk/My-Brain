### Named problems

- **The live problem, and the owner names it as one of his top three: transactions are not booked until month-end, which leaves the company 30 to 45 days in arrears with no live spend visibility.** Ramp card spend that used to be reviewed weekly is now booked once a month, while many employees hold company cards. The standard being aimed at is a live close — expenses booked as they happen — measured by eliminating the 30-to-45-day lag. **This is a function-level problem, not a verdict on any person.**
- **The communication problem is named in the same terms.** On 2026-08-22, discussing where utilities and operating permits actually sit: "**There's a black box. It doesn't communicate.** No communication and won't share anything, and it's like they took it on, and then it's like, well where is it?"
- **SOP 302 publishes a lender's full wire instructions** — account name, account number, beneficiary bank and **ABA routing number** — in a document distributed through the process wiki. Direct fraud exposure, and the highest-ranked finding on [[00-Meta/open-items]]. The SOP's own note that payment instructions vary by lender and should be taken from the notice is the fix.
- **Four of the seven Tier 1 Critical processes with nothing started are utility processes:** utility onboarding for current communities, utility onboarding at acquisitions, utility recapture reporting and utility recapture review ([[70-Operations/sops/sop-library]]).
- **The same form has three different owners.** The Utility Request JotForm is filed by the Community Manager and routed to Maintenance in SOP 104, submitted by Construction and executed by Accounting in 303a, and submitted by Operations in 303b.
- **SOP 302's missed-lender-deadline consequence, payment timing and Repo Tracker are all [TBD]**, with an email thread as the interim tracker. Those terms are Austin's and David's to close.
- **The accounting back end of infill and rehab is the deck's own worry:** "ACCOUNTING BACK-END TRACKING INCREDIBLY IMPORTANT FOR ACCURATE INFO," "Total cost of home - rehabs / in-fill (lot prep, home, delivery, setup, etc)," "**Accuracy must be 100%**," "Floorplan financing records by home."
- **"Monthly financial reporting - all entities, timely cadence. No excuses."** — the deck's phrasing, against a reported improvement from a two-week turnaround to under 48 hours.
- **A 2025 utility distortion worth knowing:** switching billing vendors moved terms from net 60 to net 30, so in several cases **13 months of utilities were paid in fiscal 2025** (board deck).
- **No loan or mortgage documents exist anywhere in the 430 entity files** — no credit agreement, note, mortgage or guaranty is filed in `10-Business/entity-structure/` ([[10-Business/outside-help/lenders]]).

- **From every department** — Accounting & Finance "is involved in all projects from start to finish, reviewing and processing invoices" (March deck), and with Asset Management is the central hub of all five cross-departmental workflows.
- **To Sales & Marketing** — funds confirmation and the floorplan payoff gate every closing. The March deck flags the seam itself: "ACCOUNTING Workflow Integration – Where is the disconnect?"
