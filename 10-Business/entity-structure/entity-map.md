# Entity map

**What this is:** the readable map over 430 filed documents in `10-Business/entity-structure/` — who sits where in the structure, what paperwork actually exists, what is completely absent, and where the filing itself is broken.

**Status:** current, 2026-09-22 for the registered agents section, added by the Page Update; the map itself is as of 2026-08-30. 430 files: 246 in `Formation Documents/`, 49 in its `Archived/` subfolder, 131 in `Property Management Agreements/`, 3 Asset Management Agreement files, 1 in `Filings/`. 104 distinct entity names appear in the formation folder.

### What the corpus actually contains

Only six document types, across `Formation Documents/` and its `Archived/` subfolder (295 files):

- **99 EIN letters and SS-4s** — the most complete series in the corpus.
- **89 certificates of formation and articles of organization**, covering roughly 95 named entities.
- **71 LLC and operating agreements** (51 in the live folder, the rest archived drafts and superseded A&Rs).
- **22 foreign qualifications, registrations and certificates of authority** — Florida 12, Georgia 4, South Carolina 2, Massachusetts 2, North Dakota 1, plus one archived Florida duplicate.
- **69 unique Property Management Agreements**, plus 1 Asset Management Agreement.
- A handful of one-offs: a Delaware Certificate of Conversion (Town and Country), a Certificate of Amendment (the Gulf Breeze MM rename), a Florida Certificate of Status and a Delaware Certificate of Good Standing.

### What is completely absent

**None of the following exists anywhere in the corpus:** cap tables · loan and mortgage documents · deeds · title documents · closing binders · insurance policies · purchase and sale agreements · subscription documents · K-1s · appraisals. The entity folder proves entities exist and who manages them. It proves nothing about who owns what, what is pledged, or what anything is worth.

### Filing hygiene to flag

- **The Property Management Agreements folder holds 129 PDFs but only 69 unique files** — **60 byte-identical pairs**, caused by two naming conventions running in parallel (`Name - Property Management Agreement.pdf` and `Name-Property Management Agreement.pdf`). Nine files exist as singletons.
- **Files that are not entity paperwork at all.** Six sit in `Formation Documents/Archived/`: a refinancing model (`Lamberton Refi.xlsx`), an org-chart deck (`Lamberton MHC Partners_Org Chart.pptx`), an investor packet (`Investor Packet - EquityMultiple 40, LLC`), a term sheet (`BEDROCK_EM Lamberton MHC - Term Sheet (10-23-17).docx`), a purchase consent (`Lamberton Lake MHC, LLC - Consent to Purchase.doc`) and **orphan signature pages (`signature pages-signed.pdf`) that are an unreadable scan attached to no identifiable document.** Two more sit in the PMA folder: **unexecuted counsel-marked template forms** (`JWAB - Exhibit B - Form Of Property Management Agreement.docx` and its reviewed twin).
- **Filename typos that will defeat a search:** **"Vanrtage Oaks LLC - Certificate of Formation.pdf"** (Vantage), **"Greak Oak-Property Management Agreement.pdf"** (Great Oak), **"Winward Knolls FL MHC LLC"** (Windward Knoll), and **"BEDROCK ( ) EM HOLDINGS, LLC"** with literal empty parentheses in the entity name.
- Several entities have an EIN letter and no certificate, or a certificate and no agreement — the series are not complete against each other.
