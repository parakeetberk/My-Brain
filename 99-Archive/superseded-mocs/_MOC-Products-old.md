# MOC — Products

The product is the community. We rent the land and hold it; we sell the homes on it and shed them. Every page here sits inside that logic.

## Files in this folder

- [[50-Products-Sales/the-community]] — **the core product.** 5,293 MH lots and 950 RV sites. What the resident actually buys.
- [[50-Products-Sales/home-sales]] — new infill and rehabbed park-owned homes. The total-payment model and the 25% starting discount.
- [[50-Products-Sales/lease-with-option-to-purchase]] — the LOP. The bridge when a buyer is lender-declined.
- [[50-Products-Sales/poh-rentals]] — 784 park-owned homes. The thing being shed, not grown.
- [[50-Products-Sales/rv-and-transient]] — 950 sites, snowbird seasonality, Campspot, park-model conversion. Carries the RV compliance gap.
- [[50-Products-Sales/ancillary-income]] — boat slips, the Geiger Key Fish Camp, utility reimbursements, fees.

**Not here, on purpose:** the fund vehicles. JREFI, JREFII, Origins, Core+ and Fund II are capital relationships, not products, and live in `30-Customers/capital-partners/`.
