# MOC — Operations

How the work gets done. The process library that says how, and the recordings of the work being discussed.

This folder was called Delivery. Delivery is template language, not Parakeet language — there is no Delivery department on the org chart. What actually lives here is process and the record of decisions made about it, which is Operations. The 67 communities moved out to [[60-Communities/MOC-Communities]], where they belong: the community is the asset every department lands on, not a deliverable this folder produces.
