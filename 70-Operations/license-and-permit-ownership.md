## Target operating model

### Two functions, not one — they fail differently

| | **Construction & Demo Permits** | **Operating Licenses & Registrations** |
|---|---|---|
| Examples | Demo, build, electrical, septic, driveway, set permits | Dealer licenses, community operating permits, utility permits, occupational licenses, state registrations (e.g. 21st Mortgage community registration) |
| Clock | Per-project, weeks | Annual or biennial renewal |
| Failure mode | **Loud** — a home cannot be set, a project stops | **Silent** — nothing happens until something lapses, then a channel goes dark or a fine arrives |
| Current evidence | `BR-DMO-26-00253` stalled in Incomplete Submittal | Unregistered communities blocking 21st Mortgage financing |

**The silent class is the more dangerous one.** The 21st Mortgage situation is exactly this: a financing channel is unavailable because registrations were never completed, and it took a mystery shop being *denied at Paradise Park* to surface it. Nobody was watching a renewal calendar because there isn't one.

### The role

**Permit & Licensing Coordinator** — one person, one throat to choke. Not a committee, not "Natalie or Creekmore."

Owns end to end: intake → filing → tracking → expediting → inspection scheduling → close-out → renewal calendar.

This is a coordinator-level role, not a manager. The value is relentless follow-up against AHJ clocks, not judgment. It can start as a portion of an existing person's time if it is written down and protected — but it cannot stay a shared responsibility, because shared is what produced the current state.

**Community managers keep local relationships and in-person pickups** (Vantage's 72-hour window needs someone on the ground). The coordinator owns the register, the status, and the escalation.

## The register

Before any process, build the list. Minimum fields:

`Permit/License ID` · `Property` · `AHJ` (county/city/state) · `Type` (construction / demo / operating / dealer / utility) · `Status` · `Filed date` · `Age in days` · `Blocking what` (home, lot, project, financing channel) · `Owner` · `Next action` · `Next action due` · `Expiry / renewal date` · `Cost` · `Document link`

**Where it lives**: a **SharePoint list in Birdcage**, with Power Automate for renewal alerts. This matches the stack already chosen for ops accountability (SharePoint Lists as the data layer, Power Automate for routing and notifications) and lands inside the system of record all MH content is consolidating into. Do not start this in Excel — the renewal alerting is the whole point, and a spreadsheet cannot nag anyone.

**`Blocking what` is the field that makes this matter.** A permit register nobody reads is filing. A register that says *"this permit is why four homes at Applegate are unset"* gets read.

## Phased transition

### Phase 1 — Inventory (Weeks 1–3)
*You cannot manage a portfolio of permits nobody has counted.*

- Pull every open construction/demo permit across all active projects — from GCs, managers, Champion, Cavco, and AHJ portals.
- Pull every operating license and registration, with expiry dates. **Start with the states where communities are unregistered for 21st Mortgage** — that has a known revenue cost attached.
- Search `/sites/UserArchive` for permit files belonging to departed staff.
- **Exit criteria**: a register with every known permit and license, every expiry date populated, and an explicit list of what could not be found. The gaps list is as valuable as the register.

### Phase 2 — Stabilize (Weeks 3–5)
*Stop the bleeding on what is already late.*

- Work the aged items. `BR-DMO-26-00253` is item one.
- Clear anything blocking a delivered-but-unset home. **This is where the money is** — Applegate homes delivered 7/22 and 7/23 are still unset.
- File renewals due within 90 days.
- Complete the 21st Mortgage community registrations.
- **Exit criteria**: zero permits blocking a home that is on site, and no renewal inside 90 days unfiled.

### Phase 3 — Operate (Weeks 5–8)
*Make the process survive without Austin.*

- Standing rule: **a permit application is filed against the home order date, not the delivery date.** Filing at delivery guarantees a set-crew slip. Wire this into the home-ordering workflow so it cannot be skipped.
- Weekly permit-aging report begins.
- Renewal alerts automated at 120 / 90 / 30 days.
- AHJ contact directory built — names, numbers, quirks, per jurisdiction.
- **Exit criteria**: the weekly number has been reported three weeks running without Austin asking for it.

### Phase 4 — Institutionalize (Weeks 8–12)
- SOP written and filed to the Birdcage taxonomy.
- Backup coverage named, so one person's vacation is not an outage.
- Register reconciled against Rent Manager property list to catch communities with no permit footprint at all — absence is a finding.

## The weekly number

**One metric, reported every week, in the Property Ops L10:**

> **Homes on site and unset, with a permit as the blocker — count and total days.**

Supported by a permit-aging distribution (0–30 / 31–60 / 61–90 / 90+).

**Reported by**: the Permit & Licensing Coordinator.
**To**: the Property Ops L10 scorecard.

This is the right metric because it is denominated in dead capital, not in paperwork. "Fourteen permits pending" means nothing to anyone. "Four homes, 118 cumulative days unset, waiting on permits" starts an argument that gets resolved.
