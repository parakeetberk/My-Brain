# 300 — Avid Exchange AP

**What this is:** how a vendor invoice gets submitted, verified, routed to the right approval workflow in AVID Exchange, approved and booked — including the CapEx and park-owned-home routing rules.

**Status:** in review (Tier 3 Standard). Initial release, combining the invoicing process and the workflow-assignment SOP. Read 2026-08-30.

## The facts

- **Owner:** Natalie Magurne, Director of Operations. **Executes:** District Manager / Regional Manager / Property Manager (submit, verify, assign workflow, approve), Accounting Team (receive, upload to AVID, verify assignments, process payment), Construction — John Clark (CapEx approvals), Sales & Marketing — Leslie Croft (POH approvals over $1,000), Director of Operations (escalation). **Trigger:** an invoice is received from a vendor. **Ends when:** the invoice is fully approved in AVID, payment is confirmed, and it is booked correctly in Rent Manager and on the P&L.
- Six phases: submission → review → workflow assignment → approval → payment authorisation & record keeping → vendor communication.
- **The workflow routing table — the operative rule of this SOP:**
  - Standard operational invoice → **Standard Regional Workflow** → Director of Operations, any amount.
  - CapEx project (construction, infrastructure, capital repairs) → **CapEx Workflow** → **John Clark**, any amount.
  - **Park-owned home expenditure over $1,000 → POH Workflow → Leslie Croft.**
  - **Park-owned home expenditure of $1,000 or less → Standard Regional Workflow** → Director of Operations. A $950 POH invoice stays standard.
- **Submission gate — the document must say "Invoice."** Not "Estimate," not "Quote." If it is labelled either, contact the vendor for a corrected document before proceeding; Accounting will not process it.
- **Vendor compliance gate — W-9, current Certificate of Insurance and a signed vendor packet must be on file with Accounting before any invoice is submitted.** Missing any of them, obtain them from the vendor first.
- Payment terms are **NET 30**. If a vendor has not been paid within NET 30, notify Accounting to check AVID.
- Submission format is fixed: email to Accounting copying the Director of Operations, subject line `Property – Invoice for Payment – Vendor`, body "Please see attached invoice to be uploaded to AVID for payment," with the invoice and any missing compliance documents attached.
- **Review daily.** Verify five fields in the left panel against the invoice: invoice number, total dollar amount, invoice date, vendor information, and the property being charged. Confirm line items and totals match the original estimate or quote.
- **Never approve a discrepant invoice** — resolve with the vendor first. **Once approved in AVID an invoice advances automatically and cannot be recalled by the manager**; only Accounting can correct or void it.
- Workflow reassignment path in AVID: open the invoice → "More" dropdown → "Assign Workflow" → select → confirm before exiting.
- **If you are unsure whether something is CapEx or standard operations, consult Accounting before reassigning — do not guess.** Wrong routing sends the invoice to the wrong approver and delays payment.
- After full approval, the invoice and payment confirmation appear in Rent Manager under the property's records. **The manager reviews the monthly P&L to confirm every invoice booked properly** and notifies Accounting immediately if one is missing or wrong.
- **Systems used:** AVID Exchange (AvidInvoice), Rent Manager, email.
- **Known problems:**
  - **Department attribution is inconsistent.** The document is headed "Parakeet Communities · **Operations** SOP · Department: Operations / Accounting" with the Director of Operations as process owner, while the wiki files it in **300-399 Accounting** and the tracker assigns creation to Accounting / Finance. The AP process therefore has an Operations owner in an Accounting band.
  - **A "Part 2" is announced and never appears.** The purpose says the SOP "covers two integrated processes: the general invoicing process for all managers, and the workflow assignment process for CapEx and POH invoices," and the body carries a "PART 1 · INVOICE SUBMISSION, REVIEW & APPROVAL" heading with no Part 2 — workflow assignment was folded in as Phase 3.
  - **The $1,000 POH threshold has no companion control.** Nothing prevents splitting a POH job into sub-$1,000 invoices to bypass Leslie Croft's approval, and no SOP defines what counts as one expenditure.
  - **Two roles are named for the same submission step:** "District Manager / Property Manager — Submit invoices to Accounting" and "Regional Manager (RM) — Submit invoices to Accounting (DM/RM)." The role table never separates DM from RM duties.
  - The tracker flags a housekeeping problem in the source: **"Two Lucid docs (300_Avid + 300_Avid AP Process) — archive one."** Only one export is in the wiki folder; the other is live in Lucid.
  - **A separate "AP Process" row is still unbuilt.** The tracker notes "300 Avid covers system side; this is PO/coding compliance" — Tier 2, not started. So purchase-order discipline and GL coding compliance, which SOP 105b depends on, have no SOP.
  - Last Revised is a blank line awaiting approval.
  - `_Video Links.txt` is an empty template; a 300 video was scheduled for the week of Aug 10, 2026, described in the schedule as training-heavy where "video does the heavy lifting."

- **This process is the control point for Jadian's Responsible Contractor requirement, and does not say so.** The signed vendor packet it already requires *is* the certification, at section IV(e) of the Vendor Guidelines. Two additions: state that the packet carries it, and require the signature to be current. Jadian's implementation plan also requires a **"Responsible Contractor Review" section in internal contract approval emails** — which for capex means the CapEx workflow routing to John Clark. Nothing does that today. See [[10-Business/vendors/responsible-contractor-program]].
- **Threshold conflict.** The Vendor Guidelines require a pre-approved signed bid over $500 and an RM or Facility Manager signature over $1,000; this SOP routes POH work over $1,000 to Leslie Croft; the Employee Handbook sets a different matrix again. Three systems, unreconciled, and every vendor has contractually signed the first.
