# 006 — Lead intake, tracking, application & screening

**What this is:** how a lead becomes an approved (or denied) applicant — logging, follow-up, completeness review, AmRent screening, income verification and the decision. The one process where two generations disagree about **who decides.**

**Status:** in review. Read 2026-08-30. **Out of date on the application step since 2026-09-21**: the apply link came down and the fee went to $0; see the dated line at the foot of the facts.

## The facts

- **Owner:** Director of Sales & Marketing and Director of Operations (per the current draft). **Executes:** Sales, Community Manager, Screening Coordinator, then Directors on escalation. **Trigger:** any inbound lead — phone, email, website, walk-in or paper. **Ends when:** the prospect is Approved (move-in coordination begins), Denied (notice sent) or Lost (documented).
- Six phases, twelve steps: intake & prospect setup → follow-up & application completion → completeness review → screening & income verification → decision & notification → escalation.
- **The intake rule:** whoever receives the lead logs it immediately, at first contact — not after the application is sent, and not at end of week. Check for a duplicate prospect record before creating one; if a duplicate exists, update it, never create a second. Sales also logs the prospect in the CRM.
- Whoever logged the lead sends the application. Application-sent date goes in Rent Manager History & Notes.
- **Weekly follow-up** until the application is complete or the prospect is Lost. Every attempt documented with date, channel and outcome.
- Required documents: government-issued photo ID, income documentation (paystubs, bank statements), signed consent form, all fields complete, application fee paid. The older PDF is stricter: **two recent paystubs, W-2s, two to three months of bank statements (all pages)**, investment summaries if applicable.
- **Screening runs through AmRent inside Rent Manager — never outside either system. Results may take up to 72 hours, and no preliminary decision or outcome may be communicated before full results are received.**
- Report review covers identity, SSN/ITIN, credit history, criminal background, bankruptcy and eviction history.
- **The income threshold: gross monthly income must be at least 2.5x the monthly lot rent.** Below it is a basis for denial; the calculation is documented in Rent Manager. R:I and D:I ratios are both calculated.
- **The denial confidentiality rule: do not send a denial notice to the prospect before notifying the Community Manager.** The CM must know before anything goes out externally.
- Escalation ("Further Review") carries **a limited summary only** — never the full application packet.
- Prospect statuses: New Prospect, Application Completed, Approved, Denied, Lost. The older PDF adds **Application Fee Paid** and sets "Lost" after **five follow-up attempts** with no response.
- Denial notices must comply with fair housing and adverse-action notice requirements; the video schedule adds a Fair Housing note at the start of the recording — "same screen, every applicant."
- **Systems used:** Rent Manager (Prospects, statuses, History & Notes, Run Screening), AmRent integration, CRM.
- **Known problems:**
  - **Two generations disagree on final decision authority.** The current draft (`006-lead tracking-application and background check process V1 draft.docx`, July 11 2026) escalates to **Director of Sales and Director of Operations, deciding jointly.** The older PDF (`SOP - Lead Intake, Tracking, Application Processing & Screening.pdf`) escalates to Community Manager and Regional Manager and states **"Regional Manager has final approval authority,"** with rare escalations to Natalie. Both sit in the same wiki folder. Who approves a borderline applicant is genuinely unsettled.
  - **The PDF names individuals the draft does not:** Screening Coordinator = **Keiter Abreu**; follow-ups may be done by **Jhoiner Perez (District Manager)** or Keiter Abreu. The draft describes the Screening Coordinator only as a role. Neither name appears anywhere else in the library.
  - **The two documents handle a denial differently.** The draft ends at status Denied. The PDF says "Update status to Denied → Notify appropriate Manager → **Move prospect to Lost**" — two different terminal states for the same outcome, which changes reporting.
  - The PDF holds incomplete applications in "New Prospect" status; the draft has no such rule and adds an "Application Completed" trigger the PDF also uses. The PDF's "Application Fee Paid" status does not exist in the draft.
  - Spelling of the vendor is inconsistent within and across documents: **AmRent / Amrent / AMRENT**.
  - The tracker records that 006 "absorbs the two separate AMRENT/Prospect Screening tracker rows" — the merge happened on the tracker, not in the documents.
