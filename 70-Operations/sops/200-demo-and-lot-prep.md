# 200 — Demo & lot prep

**What this is:** demolishing a home and preparing the lot for an infill replacement — seven phases from written authorisation to Regional Manager closeout verification. **The only approved SOP in the library.**

**Status:** APPROVED — the single green row on the tracker and the only process marked approved in the wiki index. Read 2026-08-30.

## The facts

- **Owner:** Construction. **Executes:** Construction, Regional Manager, Community Manager, Maintenance Team, demo contractor. **Trigger:** the TBD Call disposition assigns the home/lot to the Demo + Infill path. **Ends when:** the lot is graded, cleared and safe; documentation is filed; Rent Manager status is "Demo / Vacant Lot"; the Regional Manager has verified the lot; and the infill home has been ordered with lot prep underway.
- Seven phases: pre-demo authorisation → safety & utility shutoffs → vendor & contractor coordination → site security → compliance & permits → day of demo → post-demo closeout.
- **Gate 1 — written authorisation, budgeted and signed, before anything.** No utility shutoff request, no contractor engagement, no physical work. **A verbal approval is not sufficient.** Rent Manager status goes to "Lot Demo" and an issue is created with current photos and condition notes. **If it is an eviction home, that flag must be added to the Rent Manager issue at this step** — it changes resident notification requirements and documentation standards.
- Residents present or recently vacated → the Regional Manager and Community Manager notify them **in writing** of the demo plan and timeline before any work begins.
- **Gate 2 — all utilities confirmed off, by two people, in writing.** Construction requests the power shutoff and confirms lock/tagout; Maintenance caps or disconnects water and sewer; then **the Regional Manager and Community Manager must physically double-check that power, water and sewer are completely off. Verbal sign-off is not accepted** and the confirmation is filed in the Construction file. "A demo that begins with live utilities is a safety violation and a liability — there are no exceptions to this rule."
- **Gate 3 — Certificate of Insurance and permits on file before the contractor is engaged.** Scope confirmed in writing: home structure, skirting, piers, and all debris haul-off. **The contractor applies for the demo permit** (and the replacement/infill permit where applicable); Construction tracks issuance and confirms it is in hand before the demo date. Before and after photos are confirmed in writing as required contractor deliverables.
- **Site security: lock and secure all doors and windows, post a "No Trespassing / Demolition Scheduled" sign — and if the demo is delayed more than 7 days from the scheduled date, the site must be physically fenced or blocked. A sign alone is not sufficient for an extended delay.**
- **Gate 4 — asbestos, lead and pest testing complete before demo, where the city or county requires it.** Construction is responsible for knowing the local requirement — **"the contractor's opinion does not override Parakeet's requirement to verify local jurisdiction rules independently. When in doubt, test."** If no testing is required, that determination is documented in the Rent Manager issue.
- **Gate 5 — day of demo: the Community Manager or Maintenance must be on site and physically confirm three things before the contractor starts — utilities off, signage posted, contractor holding permit and paperwork. If any one is not confirmed, demo is halted and Construction notified immediately.** Photos and video of all three are filed and shared with the Regional Manager.
- **Gate 6 — closeout requires contractor documentation: after photos, debris removal tickets and haul-off receipts. "A contractor claim that debris was removed without documentation is not accepted."** Construction withholds closeout sign-off and escalates if they are not provided, before final payment.
- **Four conditions must all be true to close:** lot graded, cleared and safe; Rent Manager status "Demo / Vacant Lot"; Regional Manager verification by site visit **or** full photo package; infill home ordered and lot prep underway.
- The SOP carries a full **RACI matrix** across all seven phases and five roles, plus an eleven-item Standard Requirements list.
- **Systems used:** Rent Manager (lot status, issues), Construction file.
- **Known problems:**
  - **Approved, but its own revision history calls it a draft.** Status field: "Approved." Review & Revision: "June 2026 | Construction | **Initial draft.**" There is no approval date, no approver name and no signature block — so the one approved process in the library cannot show who approved it or when.
  - **Three items or four?** Phase 7 Task 1: "Construction collects from the contractor: after photos, debris removal tickets, and haul-off receipts. **No closeout is accepted without all three.**" Standard Requirements #7: "Contractor must provide **before photos, after photos, debris removal tickets, and haul-off receipts. Closeout is not accepted without all four.**"
  - **A competing rewrite of this same approved SOP is in circulation as 200.** `200-Demo_to_Infill_SOP_V1.docx` (August 2026) states in its own change log that it was "**Rewritten from original Approved SOP into Parakeet branded format**" with roles reassigned — and it is filed in the drafting room under the number **200**, not 200a. See [[70-Operations/sops/200a-demo-to-infill]].
  - The SOP references "the approving authority" for demo authorisation without naming the role — the single most important signature in the process has no owner.
  - "Infill Home Ordering & Lot Prep SOP" and "Rehab Lifecycle SOP (Path A)" are named as downstream SOPs under titles that do not match the library's actual documents (001 and 105).
  - `_Video Links.txt` is an empty template — and this was the **zero-rework recording slot**, assigned to the week of Aug 10, 2026 precisely because the process was approved. No video exists.
