# 200a — Demo to infill

**What this is:** a full rewrite of the approved Demo & Lot Prep SOP under a new branded template, with the process owner moved from Construction to the Director of Construction and Maintenance replaced by a Facility Manager. It carries the number 200 on disk and 200a in the wiki.

**Status:** in review (Tier 1 Critical). Version 1.0, SOP ID **CONST-002**, effective August 2026. Read 2026-08-30.

## The facts

- **Owner:** Director of Construction (process owner, authorises every phase transition). **Executes:** Director of Construction, Construction Team, Facilities Manager / Facility Manager, Community Manager, Regional Manager, demo contractor. **Trigger:** the TBD Call disposition assigns the home/lot to the Demo + Infill path. **Ends when:** the lot is cleared, graded and documented; Rent Manager reads "Demo / Vacant Lot"; the lot is verified; and the infill home is ordered.
- Same seven phases and the same 26 numbered steps as the approved 200 — pre-demo authorisation, utility shutoffs, contractor coordination, site security, compliance & permits, day of demo, closeout — with the same substantive gates:
  - Written demo authorisation in hand before any shutoff request, contractor engagement or physical work; verbal approval insufficient.
  - Rent Manager status to "Lot Demo," issue created with photos, condition notes and the **eviction flag** where applicable.
  - Power shutoff confirmed with lock/tagout; water and sewer capped; **Regional Manager and Community Manager physically confirm all utilities off, in writing, filed in the Construction file.**
  - COI and permits on file before contractor engagement; contractor applies for the demo and infill permits; before/after photos confirmed in writing as deliverables.
  - Doors and windows secured, signage posted; **delay of more than 7 days → fence or physically block the site.**
  - Asbestos / lead / pest testing complete before demo where the jurisdiction requires it, via a **licensed** vendor; a "no testing required" determination is documented in the Rent Manager issue.
  - Day-of: CM or Facility Manager on site confirms **utilities off, signage posted, contractor holding permit and paperwork** — any gap halts the demo.
  - Closeout: after photos, debris tickets and haul-off receipts before final payment.
- **What this version changes:** all documents must be filed in the **Construction file in SharePoint** and "no phase may close without the required documents on file"; the **Facilities Manager**, not the Regional Manager, verifies the completed lot; the Director of Construction **signs to close the SOP**; and the completed closeout package must be distributed **the same day as sign-off** to the Director of Sales, Director of Operations and Asset Management.
- Reviewed annually by the Director of Construction and Director of Operations.
- **Systems used:** Rent Manager (lot status, issue tracking), SharePoint Construction file, Demo Contractor vendor file.
- **Known problems:**
  - **Two documents claim the number 200, and one of them is the only approved SOP in the library.** `200-Parakeet_Demo_Lot_Prep_SOP_Approved.docx` (APPROVED, Construction) and `200-Demo_to_Infill_SOP_V1.docx` (draft, Director of Construction, SOP ID CONST-002) sit side by side in the drafting room under the same number, describing the same process with different role names. The wiki calls the second one "200a Demo to Infill." The rewrite's own change log admits the overlap: "Rewritten from original Approved SOP into Parakeet branded format. Roles updated."
  - **The wiki's 200a folder is empty of content.** It holds only `_Video Links.txt` and a note, `_Where is the flowchart.txt`, saying the workflow is drafted in Lucid as two competing documents ("200 Demo to infill" and "200a_Demo to infill") and should be exported "when approved." So the published 200a page has no SOP and no flowchart, while the SOP lives unpublished under the wrong number.
  - **Roles were renamed without being reconciled.** The rewrite lists **"Facilities Manager (FM)"** and **"Facility Manager (FM)"** as two separate roles sharing one abbreviation, with different duties. Final lot verification moves to the Facilities Manager in Step 25 — but the signature block still reads "**Regional Manager — Lot Verified**," and the Regional Manager remains in the phase headers. The approved version has the Regional Manager verifying throughout.
  - **A truncated closeout requirement:** Step 22 reads "Collect from contractor: after photos, debris removal. **No closeout is accepted without all three**" — it lists two items and demands three. The approved version says three; its own Standard Requirements say four.
  - Step 5 assigns the utility shutoff request to the "**Construction Team**" while every other step in the phase is owned by the Director of Construction — the only appearance of that role in the document.
  - **SOP ID CONST-002 does not align with the numeric scheme.** 202 is CONST-003, 203 is CONST-203 in its table and CONST-004 in its header banner, and `Parakeet_Lot_Mapping_SOP_1.docx` uses a third form, SOP-LOTS-001. 203 then cross-references "CONST-202" and "CONST-200," neither of which exists.
  - Its "Applies To" says the SOP ends at "lot clearance and verification" while its Finish Line says "**Facilities has verified completion**" — two different verification owners in the same header table.
  - `_Video Links.txt` is an empty template; a 200a video was scheduled for the week of Aug 24, 2026 with a note to export the Lucid chart into the folder while recording.
