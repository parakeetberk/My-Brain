# 001 — Home ordering, delivery & set

**What this is:** how Parakeet orders a new infill home from the manufacturer, receives it, sets it and gets it listed — Sales owns it end to end, across five departments.

**Status:** final draft, ready for approval — the only 000-series process past "in review." Read 2026-08-30.

## The facts

- **Owner:** Director of Sales & Marketing ("Sales owns this process"). **Executes:** Sales, Asset Management (Justin), Operations, Operations Coordinator (Melanie), Construction (John Clark), Accounting, System Administration. **Trigger:** Asset Management identifies an infill need — occupied lot turnover, vacant lot, or demo lot. **Ends when:** the home is listed for sale.
- Eleven steps: infill need → lot due diligence → home selection → order placement → asset setup → date management → storage (if needed) → delivery → insurance/loan → set + punch → listing.
- **Gate 1 — no home may be selected or ordered until lot readiness is confirmed and due diligence is in Rent Manager.** Operations confirms the lot, electrical, water/sewer and whether a survey exists; Construction orders the survey if missing and enters buildable envelope, door swing, electrical, water/sewer and placement notes into Rent Manager UDF fields.
- **Gate 2 — the serial number must be in Rent Manager before delivery day.** The manufacturer provides it when the home goes online (comes off the production line). Without it there is no way to confirm the correct home is on the correct pad. Do not accept delivery without it.
- **Gate 3 — the home is not listed until the interior punch list is complete and the manufacturer packet is confirmed.** Sales verifies six items before listing: 100-point punch list uploaded, manufacturer packet placed for the buyer, insurance active, loan documents uploaded, serial number confirmed, asset status Active.
- Operations must agree the floor plan and production line before Sales signs off the order. Sales signs the invoice, emails the order to the manufacturer copying Accounting, and sends the signed quote to the manufacturer copying the Rent Manager team and Finance.
- Accounting pulls the floorplan line report, earmarks the floorplan line, opens loan-company outreach, opens utility accounts in the company name, then after delivery activates insurance and sends a recurring status notification **every 30 days** until permanent transition or closure.
- Asset Management (Justin) updates Rent Manager **within 24 hours** of any date change notification from Sales. Sales must send a department-wide email on every change to an online date, offline date or delivery date.
- Storage: Construction picks the location (On Lot / Manufacturer / Community / Other), Asset status goes to "In Storage," and Construction reviews storage aging weekly — **over 30 days in storage triggers notification to Asset Management, Sales and Operations.**
- Someone must be onsite at all times during delivery. At delivery: Home Drop Confirmation form in the "TBD app," video or **minimum 5 photos** (each side plus VIN plate), serial number photo, and the exterior checklist based on the manufacturer's checklist.
- The manufacturer packet goes in the refrigerator for the buyer. Manufacturer documents are mailed to the Maryland office after delivery/set; Melanie sends the UPS labels.
- If the delivered home does not fit the assigned lot: notify Justin, Sales and Accounting immediately, Sales opens an MRB issue, and **no set coordination proceeds until a new lot is confirmed and approved.**
- Marketing kickoff is initiated on delivery confirmation, in parallel with set and punch.
- Asset status path: ORDERED → In Storage (if applicable) → DELIVERED → Active → LOT–Avail (POH), from LOT POH Delivered.
- **Systems used:** Rent Manager (asset records, UDF fields, MRB issues, document uploads), the "TBD app," FastField, Cavco weekly import, SharePoint (Manufacturer Park Owned Assets folder structure), email.
