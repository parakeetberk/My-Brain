# 106 — Regional 30-day site visit

**What this is:** the Regional Manager's community site visit — walkthrough, 360 video, an action plan with due dates, and a close-out loop that must finish inside the same week.

**Status:** in review (Tier 3 Standard). Initial draft July 28 2026. Read 2026-08-30.

## The facts

- **Owner:** Director of Operations. **Executes:** Regional Manager (owns and drives it), Community Manager, Maintenance Lead. **Trigger:** the Regional Manager schedules a visit or conducts a surprise visit. **Ends when:** the signed plan is distributed, action items are entered in Rent Manager, and the visit is logged in the Regional Weekly Recap — **all by end of the week of the visit.**
- Three timelines: before, during, after.
- **Before:** schedule with the Community Manager and Maintenance Lead **up to 30 days in advance — or conduct a surprise visit with no notice, permitted at any time.** Review the previous 30-Day Plan and all open items. Prepare the template. **Confirm the GoPro or phone is charged.**
- **During, in order:** full property walkthrough with CM and Maintenance Lead → project status and outstanding maintenance → **AR and eviction status** → new home landings and rehab homes → **walk all vacant homes and verify each is cleaned, powered up and show-ready** → **GoPro 360 video of the community, starting at the property signage** → supporting photos → complete the plan → both signatures before leaving.
- **Anyone 30+ days past due, or with an active eviction, must be documented as an action item on the plan.**
- The plan captures: community name, Community Manager, Maintenance Lead, occupancy rate, time of visit, date of last visit, outstanding AR and eviction count, whether all prior items were completed (and if not, why), and **up to 11 action items, each with a due date.**
- **Prior items not completed must be documented with a reason and carried forward as new action items with updated due dates.**
- **Both the Community Manager and the Regional Manager sign the plan before the Regional Manager leaves the property.**
- **After:** save as PDF named `PropertyName_SiteVisit_MMDDYYYY` → upload plan PDF, GoPro 360 video, all photos and any project/prospect reports to OneDrive under the property's **Site Visits and Inspections** folder → enter every action item in Rent Manager as a task with its due date, tagged **"30-Day Site Visit Follow-Up,"** assigned to the CM or Maintenance Lead and linked to active service issues → distribute the PDF to the Community Manager and to the Director of Operations alongside the Regional Weekly Recap → schedule the next visit and set reminders.
- **All Rent Manager tasks must be entered, and both distributions completed, before end of the week of the visit.**
- **Systems used:** Rent Manager (tasks tagged "30-Day Site Visit Follow-Up", Issues list), OneDrive (property > Site Visits and Inspections), GoPro or phone, Regional Weekly Recap.
- **Known problems:**
  - **The SOP never states a required visit frequency.** It is called a 30-Day plan and the process is titled "Regional 30-Day Site Visit," but the only "30 days" in the scheduling step is *how far in advance* a visit may be booked. Nothing says how often each community must be visited.
  - **Who enters the Rent Manager tasks is contradictory.** The roles table: "Regional Manager … **enters action items in Rent Manager**." Timeline 3 Task 3: "Regional Manager **ensures community manager logs into Rent Manager and enters each action item**." Two owners for the accountability step.
  - The Regional Weekly Recap, which is the delivery vehicle to the Director of Operations, is a **separate process with nothing started** — the tracker lists "Weekly Manager Recap (Jotform)" as a recommended addition found in raw material and never previously tracked.
  - The tracker's related row, **"Site Visit Inspection (Regional)," Tier 2, is still unformalized** raw material ("X-30 Day Site Visit Checklist for Facilities Managers.pdf") even though this SOP references a "Site Visit Inspection" as a completed item on its own checklist.
  - The plan form's paired-column layout is duplicated in the source document ("Outstanding AR & # of Evictions" appears twice in one row), so the printable form is malformed.
  - The tracker notes v2 showed no workflow for 106 when a Lucid flowchart PDF and an SOP draft both existed — the scoreboard was wrong about this process for at least one version.
  - `_Video Links.txt` is an empty template; a 106 video was scheduled for the week of Sep 7, 2026, referencing a "Rent Manager App Action Plan SOP already in SharePoint" that is not in this library.
