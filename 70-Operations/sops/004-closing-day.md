# 004 — Closing day

**What this is:** the day-of-closing sequence — final walkthrough, funds confirmation, keys — and the folder where a second, competing generation of the whole closing process is sitting.

**Status:** in review (Tier 1 Critical). Read 2026-08-30.

## The facts

- **Owner:** Community Manager or Sales Representative running point on the sale (execution); Melanie S., Accounting and Asset Management own the completion half. **Trigger:** the closing package is fully executed. **Ends when:** funds are confirmed, keys are handed over, the Move-In Request is submitted and the asset is marked inactive.
- Sequence: schedule the appointment → final walkthrough with the buyer → open work orders for anything found → **wait for Accounting's "Funds Received — Clear to Close"** → hand over keys → Move-In Request submitted the same day → Accounting sends the Closed & Funded notification → Asset Management moves Prospect to Resident and deactivates the asset.
- **Keys are not handed over until Accounting confirms funds are received.** Repairs found at walkthrough are resolved on-site by Maintenance before handover.
- **Move-In Request must be submitted the same day as closing** (or the same day as the lease start date on an LOP), with Melanie copied.
- Accounting's three confirmations before the asset can close out: wire payment received, home loan paid off on the floor plan, insurance updated. Email subject lines must clearly identify the property and transaction.
- If the buyer does not show: notify the team by email and contact the buyer to reschedule.
- The flowchart adds two steps the SOP text does not: **update the file as the final Home Sales Package, send home files to the community**, and a **Sales 30-day follow-up**.
- The alternate generation (`003-CLOSING STEPS 05.05.2026.docx`) states hard signing and deposit rules the current SOP does not: **all parties must sign at least one (1) day prior to, but no more than five (5) days before, the official closing date**; deposit is collected at time of offer and **submitted within 3 days of signing the PSA**; folder naming is `Asset#_Type of Transaction_Last Name_Move-in Date`.
- **Systems used:** Rent Manager (Prospect record, asset status, move-in request), SignEasy, JotForm (alternate generation), SharePoint, email.
- **Known problems:**
  - **Two generations of the closing process are both live, and the newer one is filed under the wrong number in the wrong folder.** `003-CLOSING STEPS 05.05.2026.docx` carries a *003* prefix, sits in the *004 Closing Day Process* wiki folder, and describes a JotForm-driven process with roles and gates the current SOP does not have: **a Title Coordinator** (verifies title, completes POAs, signs on behalf of the seller, uploads to SignEasy, triggers title work at move-in), **a Make Ready Board gate** (Director of Construction confirms home status; if in rehab the deal goes on temporary hold), an "Offer Invalid" / "Offer Valid – File Complete" button workflow, and a Residency Application verified in the Rent Manager Prospect module. `003- Parakeet_Home_Sale_Closing_SOPv1.docx` has none of this. Nothing in the library says which one the team follows.
  - The Closing Walkthrough Checklist — the artifact that gates key handover — is **[TBD — checklist in development]** in every version.
  - The alternate document has no header table, no version, no owner, no effective date and no revision history; it is dated only in its filename and title line.
  - The 004 wiki folder holds no SOP under its own number — only the misnumbered 003 file and the Lucid export.
  - `_Video Links.txt` is an empty template; a 4-minute 004 video was scheduled for the week of Aug 31, 2026.
