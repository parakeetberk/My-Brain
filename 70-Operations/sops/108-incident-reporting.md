# 108 — Incident reporting

**What this is:** documenting, escalating, investigating and closing any incident at a community — injuries, complaints involving safety, staff misconduct, police involvement. The liability and insurance feed.

**Status:** in review. Effective August 2026, initial publication. **The tracker still records this process as "not started / Tier 1 gap"** — the document exists and is finished. Read 2026-08-30.

## The facts

- **Owner:** Operations / HR (shared). **Executes:** Community/District Manager (Manager on Duty) as first responder, Regional Manager, Director of Operations, HR. **Trigger:** an incident occurs — accident, injury, resident complaint involving safety or misconduct, maintenance issue involving safety risk, staff misconduct, or any situation involving police. **Ends when:** the report is filed with HR, the Director of Operations has made final corrective-action decisions, and follow-up with all parties is confirmed.
- Five phases: immediate response & notification (same day) → documentation (same day) → investigation & review (target 2 weeks) → corrective action → follow-up & filing (within one week).
- **Safety first, paperwork second** — "do not leave the scene until all parties are accounted for and the situation is stable."
- **Collect at the scene, because it cannot be recovered later:** full name, home address and phone for every involved party; **full name and badge or licence number for first responders**; witness names, addresses and phones on a separate witness sheet; and **the police report number before the officers leave.** The report cannot be submitted without the police report number if police responded.
- **Same-day notification, no exceptions regardless of perceived severity:** Regional Manager **and** Director of Operations. If the Regional Manager is unreachable, **escalate straight to the Director of Operations — do not wait.** **HR is notified the same day, in writing, for any incident involving Parakeet personnel.**
- **Both submission methods are required — email AND SharePoint upload (Operations › Incident Reports). Emailing alone does not satisfy the filing requirement.** The incident is also logged in the Community Incident Log on the day it occurs.
- Report sections: person involved, incident information (date, time, location, police involvement, report number, detailed description), injury and medical (injured Y/N, treatment provided Y/N/refused, where), witnesses, reporter information with signature, and an **Actions Taken Log** (date, action, name).
- **Investigation lead splits by type: employee-related → HR leads; resident-related → Regional Manager leads.** Target completion **within 2 weeks.** Confidentiality is absolute — discussed only with personnel necessary to complete the investigation.
- **Every Incident Report must include steps to prevent recurrence at other Parakeet communities** — not just at the one where it happened.
- The Director of Operations makes the final corrective-action decision and assigns owner and target date for each item; the Director also decides whether the incident triggers the insurance / property damage claim process, which runs **in parallel** rather than replacing this one.
- **Follow-up with all involved parties within one week** — "not when convenient." **For harassment between employees, the Regional Manager specifically checks in with the reporting party** to confirm they are comfortable returning to normal duties.
- Final report goes to HR for the personnel file on all incidents involving employees or residents. Stated principle: "BETTER SAFE THAN SUED" — file even if the incident appears minor or resolved.
- Reporting standard: when in doubt, report it. There is no penalty for reporting something minor.
- **Systems used:** Community Incident Log (SharePoint / Jotform — mid-transition), SharePoint Operations › Incident Reports, email.
