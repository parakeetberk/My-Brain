# 303 — Utility activation

**What this is:** getting utilities live on a home before the work that depends on them — two documented variants, **303a new infill homes** (Construction submits) and **303b rehab homes** (Operations submits). In both, Accounting does all the provider contact.

**Status:** in review. Both variants Draft V1, listed on the tracker as "Recommended Addition." Read 2026-08-30.

## The facts

- **Owner:** **Construction** (303a) / **Operations** (303b). **Utility execution in both: Accounting** — all activations and account requests. **Executes:** the owning department (confirms requirements, submits the form, physically verifies), Accounting (contacts providers, opens accounts, coordinates meter installs, confirms), Sales (confirms utilities live before showings), Operations (303a: confirms during walkthroughs), plus a named tracker in each variant. **Trigger (303a):** home assigned to a lot, or delivery scheduled — **whichever comes first**. **Trigger (303b):** the rehab is approved and scheduled, or vendor work requires utilities. **Ends when:** all utilities are verified live, Rent Manager is updated, and teams are notified.
- **The ownership rule, stated in both documents:** "Construction/Operations does not contact utility providers. All provider contact, account opening, transfers and activation requests go through Accounting. Submit the form — Accounting takes it from there." **Form submission is the trigger for Accounting to begin.**
- **Utilities must be proactive, not reactive.** Do not wait until delivery week or the day before vendor work — **new meter installs have significant lead times and must be flagged on the form at submission.**
- Step 1 in both: identify the utilities needed (electric, water, sewer/septic, gas if applicable), confirm the providers for the address, and determine meter status — **existing meter → activate or transfer; no meter → new install required, flag immediately.**
- Form contents: property and lot/unit number, providers and services needed, meter status, **target activation date**, and any known delays or special circumstances.
- Accounting's confirmations for each utility: **account number, activation/service start date, and deposit requirements.** Accounts are opened (303b: opened or transferred) **in Parakeet's name.** New meter installs are coordinated with the provider and the install date and lead time reported back.
- **The verification gate, in both variants: account confirmation from Accounting is not sufficient. Someone must physically verify on site — electric live, water running, sewer/septic functional, gas confirmed.**
- **303a:** utilities must be on before or immediately after delivery, and **the interior punch process does not begin if utilities are not confirmed operational.**
- **303b:** **vendor work does not begin until utilities are confirmed live on site**, and **utilities stay on through the entire rehab** — vendor work, manager walkthrough, punchlist, cleaning and showings. Operations monitors throughout and notifies Accounting immediately of any interruption.
- **If a utility is not live: do not proceed.** Log it in Rent Manager Issues, notify Accounting for provider escalation, and notify Regional (303a adds Construction leadership).
- Infrastructure not ready → log in Rent Manager Issues and update the form; 303b adds "escalate to Regional and leadership. **No work proceeds until resolved.**"
- Completion notification: 303a → Operations (walkthrough and maintenance coordination) and Sales (showings). 303b → Sales (showings) and Regional (awareness and sign-off if required).
- **Systems used:** Utility JotForm, Rent Manager (Issues, MRB), Accounting's provider accounts.
- **Known problems:**
  - **Three SOPs claim ownership of one Utility JotForm.** 303a: **Construction** submits it. 303b: **Operations** submits it. `104-TBD_Reporting_SOP.docx` Phase 4: "**Community Manager** completes the Utility Request Jotform… The form routes to **Maintenance** for follow-up," with Maintenance verifying activation. Accounting — which both 303 documents say does all the work — is not in the 104 version at all.
  - **Two undefined individuals do the same job in the two variants.** 303a role table: "**Derek** — Tracks Form submissions; verify Rent Manager entries against tracking log." 303b: "**Gabe** — Tracks JotForm submissions; verifies Rent Manager entries against tracking log." Neither name appears anywhere else in the library, and the "tracking log" they reconcile against is never identified.
  - **303a has two duplicate, garbled verification steps.** Step 4 is "Verify Utilities Are Live — **At home set completion**" (its body describes verification at delivery) and Step 5 is "Verify Utilities Are Live — **At CO and completion**" (its body describes verification at set). The header table says Construction "verifies utilities live **at delivery and set**," so the two steps are mislabelled and one references a "CO" the SOP never defines.
  - **303a's trigger is stated two ways:** body — "when a home is assigned to a lot (**Issue created**)"; header table — "Home assigned to a lot (**MRB created**)."
  - **The form is called two things in one document:** 303a alternates between "the Utility Form" and "the JotForm" — and per the wider JotForm-vs-FastField problem, no platform is settled.
  - **Neither variant updates Rent Manager as a step.** Both Finish Lines require "Rent Manager updated," and no numbered task in either document does it.
  - **The third utility process is missing.** Both variants cover new and rehab homes; the tracker carries **"Utility Onboarding (Current Communities)" as a Tier 1 Critical row with nothing started** (raw material in ROCK), **"Utility Onboarding (Acquisitions)" as a Tier 1 red row**, and **"Utility Recapture Reporting" and "Utility Recapture Review" as two further Tier 1 red rows.** Four of the seven Tier 1 gaps in the company are utility processes.
  - Resident-facing utility transfers at move-in and move-out are marked "Covered by related SOP — see 303a/b + 102/103" on the tracker, but **SOP 102 has no utility step at all** and 103 has none either.
  - Last Revised is blank in both variants.
  - Both `_Video Links.txt` files are empty templates; two 303 videos were scheduled for the week of Aug 31, 2026.
