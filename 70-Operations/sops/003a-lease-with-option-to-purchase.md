# 003a — Lease with option to purchase

**What this is:** the alternative closing path when a buyer cannot finance or pay cash — a lease on the home with a contractual purchase option at end of term.

**Status:** in review. The document's own header says "Status: Approved"; the tracker, wiki index and wiki folder all say draft. Read 2026-08-30.

## The facts

- **Owner:** Director of Sales and Operations Coordinator (per 003a's header) — 002-combined instead names Director of Sales and Director of Operations. **Executes:** Community Manager or Regional Sales Rep, Director of Sales, Closing Coordinator (Melanie S.), Sales, Operations, Asset Management (Justin). **Trigger:** an offer cannot proceed for financing or funds reasons and an LOP is offered. **Ends when:** the LOP package is fully signed and uploaded, keys are handed over, and the asset is marked inactive.
- **When it is offered:** ideally when a buyer pursuing a financed purchase is declined by the lender, or has another qualifying financial obstacle — change of employment, thin credit history, credit repair, divorce proceedings. Explicitly **not a standard first offer.**
- Before submitting, the Community Manager or Sales Rep must get approval on three commercial terms: **LOP end-of-term purchase price, monthly home payment, and term of agreement.**
- **24 hours** for Director of Sales approval; **24 hours** for buyer e-sign (SignEasy or wet signature). All deadlines anchor to the **lease start date** stated in the LOP, and the lease start date does not move.
- LOP package: LOP Agreement, Community Lease, Rules and Regulations, Prospectus (Florida only).
- **The lease-structure rule that matters:** the Community Lease must reflect **the lot rent portion only**, not the combined home-and-lot payment — the total monthly lot and home rent is accounted for in the LOP itself.
- **The date rule that matters:** the Community Lease must expire **the day before lot rent increases go into effect.** Confirm the lease end date against the rent increase schedule before issuing.
- The Move-In Request Form is submitted **the same day as the lease start date**, with Melanie copied. Asset Management moves Prospect to Resident and marks the asset inactive once LOP funds are confirmed and documented.
- Sales adjusts Rent Manager Asset UDFs to modify the listing feed and updates the CRM; Operations uploads executed documents to the Rent Manager Prospect record.
- **Systems used:** SignEasy, Rent Manager (Prospect record, Asset UDFs, move-in request), CRM, SharePoint, email.
- **Known problems:**
  - **Status field says "Approved"** while every index says draft — same defect as 003.
  - **The rejected-LOP response window is 2 days here and 3 days in `003- Parakeet_Home_Sale_Closing_SOPv1.docx`;** the closing checklist leaves the number blank.
  - **A lender-coordination phase is pasted into an SOP whose whole purpose is the absence of a lender.** 003a's Phase 4 table reads "Buyer initiates and maintains communication with lender… follows up every 48 hours… once lender issues clear to close…" — copied verbatim from the financed closing SOP. The phase list at the top only has four phases and does not include it.
  - **Georgia signs electronically here** ("Florida and Georgia: To be sent to leasee in e-sign platform") while `002-combined` states globally that "South Carolina and Georgia must wet sign all documents."
  - Melanie is called **Operations Coordinator** in the scope list and **Closing Coordinator** in the task tables of the same document.
  - Effective Date June 27, 2026 but the revision table records the initial release as July 7, 2026.
  - Formal onboarding training process: **[TBD]**.
  - The LOP Checklist is listed as "Linked. Compiled in SignEasy" but is not present in the library as a file.
