# 003 — Closing prep & documentation

**What this is:** assembling and executing the closing package for a home sale — three deal paths, one coordinator, and the worst documentation conflict in the library.

**Status:** in review (Tier 1 Critical). Two of the three source documents claim "Approved" in their own status fields; the tracker, the wiki index and the wiki folder all say draft. Read 2026-08-30.

## The facts

- **Owner:** Director of Sales and Director of Operations (joint, per both write-ups). **Executes:** Community Manager or Regional Sales Representative, Director of Sales, Closing Coordinator (Melanie S.), Accounting (David, Moe), Sales team, Asset Management, Operations. **Trigger:** a signed PSA or completed LOP is submitted. **Ends when:** the closing package is fully signed, uploaded to Rent Manager, keys are handed over, and the asset is marked inactive.
- Six phases: submission & approval → deal-type routing → document compilation & e-sign → lender coordination (financed) → closing day → completion & notification. Phases 1–2 apply to all paths; 3–6 diverge by deal type (Cash / Financed / Lease with Option to Purchase).
- **24 hours** for Director of Sales approval from receipt of the PSA or LOP. **24 hours** for buyer e-sign via SignEasy — if missed, the Community Manager or Sales Rep follows up directly and **the closing date does not move.**
- **48-hour** lender follow-up cadence on financed deals. **Do not compile closing documents before the lender issues clear to close.**
- All document deadlines are anchored to the closing date in the PSA, or the lease start date in the LOP.
- **The hard stop at the end:** the asset cannot be marked inactive in Rent Manager until payment is confirmed **AND** the floor plan loan is paid off. Both conditions, not either.
- Cash closing package: Closing Checklist, PSA, Flood Addendum, Closing Worksheet (incl. sales tax), Bill of Sale, payment (wire or certified bank check), Community Lease, Rules and Regulations, Prospectus (Florida only), Rehab Addendum (if applicable), Form 400 and Form 500 (South Carolina only). Financed adds all lender-required documentation.
- Rehab Addendum is collected at time of offer or **within 3 business days** after (checklist).
- Executed packages save to SharePoint: Sales Folder > Agreements and Closing Docs, and upload to the Rent Manager Prospect record.
- Rushed closings are named in the SOP as the primary cause of process failure. Verbal or side-channel approvals are not acceptable.
- **Systems used:** SignEasy, Rent Manager (Prospect record, asset status, move-in request), SharePoint, JotForm (in the 05.05.2026 generation), email.
- **Known problems:**
  - **The rejected-offer response window has three different values across three documents and a missing number in a fourth.** `003- Parakeet_Home_Sale_Closing_SOPv1.docx`: "Buyer has a 3-day window to respond." `002-combined-home sale process.docx` and `003a- Parakeet_Lease_with_Option_to_Purchase_SOP.docx`: "2-day window." `003- Parakeet_Closing_Document_Checklist.docx`: "buyer has -day window to respond" — the number is simply absent.
  - **E-sign versus wet-sign by state contradicts itself inside one document.** `002-combined` states as a global rule: "South Carolina and Georgia must wet sign all documents – e-sign platform not acceptable." Its own Phase 3A then says Florida via SignEasy, SC and GA wet-sign; its Phase 4A says **Florida and Georgia via SignEasy, SC wet-sign**; its Phase 3C says Florida and Georgia via SignEasy. Georgia is on both sides of the rule. 003a also puts Georgia on SignEasy.
  - **Status fields lie.** `003- Parakeet_Home_Sale_Closing_SOPv1.docx` and `003a` both read "Status: Approved" in their own header tables. The tracker, the wiki index and the wiki folder all mark them "Drafted — pending review." One of the two must be wrong, and field staff read the document, not the tracker.
  - Effective dates disagree with revision dates: SOPv1 says Effective June 27, 2026 with a June 27 initial release; 003a says Effective June 27 with a **July 7** initial release; 002-combined says Effective July 7.
  - **Two generations of the closing process are both live.** `003-CLOSING STEPS 05.05.2026.docx` sits in the *004* folder with a *003* prefix and describes a materially different JotForm-driven process — see [[70-Operations/sops/004-closing-day]].
  - The Closing Walkthrough Checklist, which gates key handover, is **[TBD — in development]** in all three write-ups.
  - Formal onboarding training for this process is **[TBD]** in both SOPv1 and 003a, in a document that calls itself "the training reference for all employees and new hires."
  - SOPv1 lists Form 500 only in its forms table but Form 400 and 500 in its reminders; the checklist lists only Form 500. Inconsistent SC form set.
  - `_Video Links.txt` is an empty template; one 003 video was scheduled for the week of Aug 24, 2026.
