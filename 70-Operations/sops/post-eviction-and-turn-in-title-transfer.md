# 800 (title) — Post-eviction & resident turn-in title transfer

**What this is:** the two unnumbered title SOPs in the Legal band — recovering title to a home after an eviction runs its course (auction path), and taking title from a resident who hands the home back voluntarily instead (turn-in path). Both run through Florida Fast Title.

**Status:** in review. Both are unnumbered PDFs — the tracker records "Post Eviction Title Process" as Tier 1 Critical, drafted pending review, "**drafts exist but unnumbered — number in 800s**," and "Resident Turn-In Title Transfer" as a recommended addition, "**drafted but never in tracker.**" Read 2026-08-30.

## The facts

- **Owner:** not named in either document. Post-eviction is executed by **Evictions**; turn-in by the **Title Department**. **Executes:** Community Manager, Regional Manager, Evictions / Title Department, resident or bidder, Florida Fast Title (FFT), Asset Management. **Trigger (post-eviction):** the Writ of Possession is posted. **Trigger (turn-in):** a resident in — or about to be in — eviction offers to hand the home back. **Ends when:** title is received, uploaded to the Asset tab in Rent Manager, copied to the Community and Regional Manager, and the Make Ready Board is updated if applicable.

### The turn-in path — the cheaper of the two
- **The gate: a signed physical title. If it is not signed, the resident signs 3 POAs in blue ink. If the resident is unable to sign a title or a POA, the home must go through the full eviction process instead.**
- **If the resident has no title, a duplicate must be ordered — but only if the home is in their name.**
- **Do not fill in any other fields on the title. The only fields completed are the Seller's Name and signature.** If a purchaser name is needed, the Community Manager asks Evictions for the entity name.
- Community Manager requests a mailing label from the Title Department and mails the title to the Maryland office.
- **Title package: Cover Sheet, Title, POAs (if the title was not signed), copy of resident's ID.** Mailed to FFT with an email carrying the package details and a tracking number.
- **FFT transfers the title into Parakeet's name**, then the Title Department emails FFT an overnight label. Title copy goes to the Community and Regional Manager; the title is uploaded to the Asset tab in Rent Manager; the Make Ready Board is updated if applicable.

### The post-eviction path — the auction route
- After the Writ of Possession is posted, the Community Manager sends Evictions a copy plus the home's identifying detail if found: **VIN / tag / decal number, year, make/model, length, colour. If no VIN is found, FFT investigates or works with the DMV to have a new VIN processed.**
- **Post-eviction package: Cover Sheet, Landlord Questionnaire, Writ of Possession, Authorization Letter.** Emailed to FFT and logged in the Eviction Workflow History/Notes tab.
- **FFT schedules the auction** and emails Evictions, who forwards to the Community and Regional Manager **and creates a calendar invite for the auction.**
- **FFT mails a Letter of Abandonment to all owners, granting approximately 21 days (timing varies) to reclaim the property. Reclaiming requires certified funds — rent, late fees, legal fees and FFT charges.**
- After the auction the Community Manager tells the Regional Manager and Evictions whether a bidder attended. **No bidder → FFT proceeds to obtain title.**
- **Bidder present:** each bids individually; the highest bidder must **pay the awarded bid price including all outstanding amounts (home cost, tax, tag/title fee), sign 3 POAs (electronic or wet), and provide acceptable proof of ID.** Evictions mails the complete title package to FFT to transfer title into the bidder's name.
- Once FFT obtains title, Evictions emails a mailing label to send it to the Maryland office. On receipt the title is emailed to the Community and Regional Manager, **uploaded to Assets in Rent Manager, and the asset is checked off in the Make Ready Board if applicable.**
- Related timing from SOP 107: **title takes approximately 30 days after mailing; 5 to 7 months in declaratory judgment cases. If the auction produces no buyer, Parakeet purchases the home for $1.**
- **Systems used:** Rent Manager (Eviction Workflow History/Notes, Assets tab, Make Ready Board), Florida Fast Title customer portal, overnight mail, email.
- **Known problems:**
  - **Both SOPs are unnumbered.** Neither document carries a process number, a header table, a version, an effective date, a process owner, a status field or a revision history — and both are PDFs, so neither can be revised into the house template without retyping. The tracker's instruction, "number in 800s," has not been carried out.
  - **The same function has two names across the two SOPs and five across the library.** Post-eviction says **Evictions** owns the package; turn-in says the **Title Department** does. Elsewhere: **Closing Coordinator / Operations Coordinator (Melanie S.)** in 003/003a, **Title Coordinator** in `003-CLOSING STEPS 05.05.2026.docx`, **Corporate Title Specialist (Ashlyne)** in the recording schedule and in SOPs 812 and 822. No document defines the Title Department or says who staffs it.
  - **Florida-only, in a five-state portfolio.** Florida Fast Title is the only vendor in both SOPs and in SOP 005. **SOP 822 documents a South Carolina statutory abandoned-home auction at day 20 under SC Code 29-15-10 that appears in neither title SOP**, and **SOP 812 states Georgia has no park-specific title shortcut and routes abandoned homes to the Corporate Title Specialist and Legal** — a path with no SOP behind it. NC and MA have nothing at all.
  - **The reclaim windows do not reconcile.** FFT's Letter of Abandonment gives owners "**approximately 21 days (timing variables range)**"; SC 822 sets a statutory **20 days** after eviction before auction; 107 sets no reclaim window. Three numbers, one concept.
  - **The lien split is missing here.** SOP 107 routes homes with a lien to **Vector Law** and homes without to FFT. Neither title SOP mentions Vector Law or liens at all, so the liened-home title path is documented nowhere.
  - **No deadlines, no escalation, no tracking.** Neither SOP has an internal turnaround, a follow-up cadence with FFT, or an owner to escalate to when a title does not arrive — against a 30-day standard and a 5-to-7-month worst case.
  - **The Make Ready Board update is "if applicable" with no rule for when it applies**, and the Make Ready Board Flow itself is a Tier 1 tracker row with drafts sitting unformalised in raw material.
  - **The master title process does not exist.** The tracker's "Title Work Process (Master)" is Tier 1 Critical, partial draft, "covered piecemeal by 005, Post-Eviction, Resident Turn-In, ROCK: Asset Title Transfer Tab SOP," with the next action "Unify the title SOPs into one master process w/ variants." Separately, "Asset Title Transfer (Entity-Level)" is Tier 2, not started.
  - **Each path has a duplicate flowchart PDF with no version marker** (`Post-Eviction Title Process.pdf`, `Resident Turn-In Process.pdf`) alongside the SOP PDF, and all four files exist twice — once in the wiki, once in the drafting room.
  - `_Video Links.txt` is an empty template in both folders; both videos were scheduled for the weeks of Aug 10 and Aug 17, 2026, with Ashlyne named as recorder, and both notes claim the SOP is "already in SharePoint" — a third location outside this library.
