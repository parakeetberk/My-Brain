# 800 (GA) — Georgia compliance: rent & term changes, dispossessory, notice service, deposits

**What this is:** the four-SOP Georgia block — 811 rent increases and term changes, 812 violations/demand/dispossessory, 813 notice service, 814 security deposits. Georgia has no manufactured-housing-specific chapter, so the **lease** does most of the work the statute does in Florida.

**Status:** counsel review — all four read "DRAFT V1 — Pending Review," drafted 2026-08-06 from the current O.C.G.A. Title 44, Chapter 7 including the 2024 amendments. **Georgia counsel must review before rollout.** Read 2026-08-30.

## The facts

- **Owner:** Natalie Magurne, Director of Operations, on all four. **Executes:** Asset Management / Finance (increase schedule by community and lease anniversary), Legal / Compliance (lease-form audit, template library, **lease vintage determination**, attorney coordination), Operations Coordinator (generates notices from the Georgia templates), Regional Manager (accuracy review pre-service, monthly expired-lease inventory, weekly proof-of-service audit, approves every deposit retention), Community Manager (serves, photographs, uploads same day; move-in and move-out inspection lists), Accounting / Finance (holds GA deposits, issues refunds and statements), Evictions Team (attorney intake).
- **The structural difference from Florida: there is no prospectus, no committee meeting, no mediation window and no Division filing. What the lease says governs — so Legal's lease-form audit and the lease-vintage call are the two decisions the whole block turns on.**

### 811 — rent increases & term changes
- **Fixed-term lease: no mid-term increase unless the lease explicitly allows it.** The increase takes effect at renewal per the lease's renewal-notice clause.
- **Tenancy at will / month-to-month, including expired leases that rolled over: a rent increase is a change in terms, requiring at least 60 DAYS written notice before the effective date** (O.C.G.A. 44-7-7). The landlord needs 60 days to terminate or modify a tenancy at will; the tenant owes 30.
- **Notice method: whatever the lease specifies. Absent lease language, deliver in writing with proof — hand delivery with acknowledgment plus first-class mail with certificate of mailing.**
- **Parakeet internal standard: serve ALL Georgia increase notices at least 60 days out regardless of lease type — one portfolio standard, no per-lease guesswork by field staff.**
- Regional Manager monitors expired-lease inventory **monthly**, because an expired lease silently becomes a tenancy at will and changes the notice rule.

### 812 — violations, demand & dispossessory
- **A written demand for possession of the premises is required before ANY dispossessory filing** (O.C.G.A. 44-7-50(a)).
- **Leases entered into or renewed on or after July 1, 2024: written notice to vacate OR pay all past due rent, late fees, utilities and other charges within THREE BUSINESS DAYS, before filing** (O.C.G.A. 44-7-50(c), the 2024 amendment). **Count three business days — weekends and legal holidays do not count.** Older leases: a written demand for possession is still required.
- **Service: posted in a SEALED ENVELOPE conspicuously on the door PLUS every additional method the lease specifies.** Photograph the posted envelope with the date visible; upload notice, photo and any mail receipts to Rent Manager History & Notes the same day.
- **The tender rule — the one that can undo a filing:** within **7 days of service of the dispossessory summons**, the tenant may tender all rents owed plus court costs; that is a complete defence and **Parakeet MUST accept it — but only ONCE per rolling 12 months per resident** (O.C.G.A. 44-7-52). **Track tender acceptances in Rent Manager so the once-per-12-months rule is actually enforceable.**
- **Non-rent violations have no statutory cure notice — the LEASE governs.** Parakeet standard: written notice with a **7-day cure period** unless the lease says otherwise or safety requires immediate action.
- Cure or escalate: paid in full within the window (rent + late fees + utilities + other charges) → document and stand down. Not cured → Evictions Team builds the attorney intake (ledger, demand, 3-day notice plus proof, lease copy) and files through counsel.
- **Post-judgment: writ and set-out logistics follow county practice through the attorney. Abandoned manufactured homes in Georgia have NO park-specific title shortcut (unlike South Carolina) — route every abandoned home to the Corporate Title Specialist and Legal for the appropriate abandonment / lien path.**
- **Terminating a tenancy at will: 60 days' written notice from the landlord** (O.C.G.A. 44-7-7).

### 813 — notice service & documentation
- Service matrix: **pre-dispossessory demand / 3-business-day notice → sealed envelope posted conspicuously on the door plus every additional method the lease specifies** (44-7-50). **Rent increase, term change or termination of a tenancy at will (60-day) → per lease method; Parakeet default = hand delivery with acknowledgment plus first-class mail with certificate of mailing** (44-7-7). **Violation / cure notices (non-rent) → per the lease clause; default the same dual method.** **Security deposit statement and refund → first-class mail to the last known address** (44-7-34).
- **If the deposit statement is returned and the tenant cannot be located after reasonable effort, the funds become landlord property 90 days after mailing.**
- Community Manager serves, photographs and uploads proof the same day; Regional Manager audits proof of service weekly; Legal owns the templates and maintains a **lease delivery-clause matrix by community.**

### 814 — security deposits
- **Holding: an escrow account in a Georgia-regulated depository, with the tenant informed of its location, OR a surety bond posted with the superior court clerk** (O.C.G.A. 44-7-31, 44-7-32).
- **Move-in list: a written list of existing damage BEFORE collecting the deposit,** which the tenant signs or has the chance to inspect and dissent from (44-7-33).
- **Move-out: inspect within THREE BUSINESS DAYS after termination of occupancy and compile a comprehensive damage list. The tenant may inspect within FIVE BUSINESS DAYS and sign or dissent** (44-7-33).
- **Return or statement within 30 DAYS of obtaining possession** — return the deposit, or deliver a written statement of the exact reasons for retention plus the damage list, by first-class mail (44-7-34).
- **Ordinary wear and tear cannot be retained for — only negligence, carelessness, accident or abuse.**
- **Penalty: non-compliance forfeits the right to retain any portion; bad-faith retention = 3x damages plus attorney fees** (44-7-35).
- Regional Manager approves every retention and audits the clocks weekly.

- **Systems used:** Rent Manager (Georgia notice templates, History & Notes, tender tracking), SharePoint (proof of service, lease delivery-clause matrix), Georgia escrow account or surety bond.
- **Known problems:**
  - **The Georgia citation for the 60-day notice differs from the operating SOP's.** 811 cites **O.C.G.A. 44-7-7** (tenancy at will). `101-lease_renewal_sop.docx` gives Georgia 60 days citing **O.C.G.A. 44-7-60.** Same number, two statutes — one of the two citations is wrong on a notice that field staff serve monthly.
  - **The lease vintage call has no supporting data.** Everything in 812 turns on whether a lease was entered into or renewed on or after **July 1, 2024** — the 3-business-day notice applies to one vintage and not the other — and there is no lease-vintage register, report or Rent Manager field named anywhere in the library. Legal is expected to make the call case by case.
  - **The 7-day tender rule needs a tracker that does not exist.** 812 instructs "track tender acceptances in Rent Manager so the once-per-12-months rule is enforceable," and no field, report or process is specified. Accepting a second tender inside 12 months when Parakeet was not obliged to gives away a filing for nothing.
  - **The lease delivery-clause matrix by community (813) does not exist in the library**, yet three of the four notice types in the matrix default to "per lease method."
  - **814's move-in damage list has no home in the operational SOPs.** It must be produced **before the deposit is collected**; `103-move_in_sop.docx` collects the security deposit at document collection and requires a Move-In Inspection Form only for community-owned homes — so on a Georgia resident-owned lot tenancy, no move-in list is produced at all.
  - **814's 3-business-day move-out inspection is not in SOP 102**, which has no deposit step, no inspection deadline and no interior inspection.
  - **The 800-eviction legacy document cuts across this block.** `800-eviction_sop.docx` states "GA: 3 business days" for demand-notice cure periods but describes a single Florida-shaped monthly eviction calendar with Florida Fast Title and Vector Law as the auction vendors — neither of which appears in the Georgia SOPs, which route abandoned homes to Legal instead.
  - **All four SOPs have blank Effective Dates** and have not been to counsel. The recording schedule HOLDS the GA 3-business-day notice video "until GA counsel signs off."
  - **Every file in this block exists twice on disk** — `Process Wiki/800-899 Legal & Compliance/811-814 Georgia/` and `_SOP Drafts & Master Tracker/811-814 Georgia Compliance/` — byte-identical, with no authoritative marker.
  - `_Video Links.txt` for the Georgia block is an empty template; the 812 notice video was scheduled for the week of Sep 14, 2026.
