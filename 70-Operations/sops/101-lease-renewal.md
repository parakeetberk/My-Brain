# 101 — Lease renewal

**What this is:** producing, sending and activating a renewal lease when a rent increase or expiration hits — five states, five notice periods, and a 30-day signature clock.

**Status:** in review (Tier 1 Critical). Target 2026-10-31. Read 2026-08-30.

## The facts

- **Owner:** Natalie Magurne, Director of Operations. **Executes:** Asset Management Department (approves rent, issues notices, updates billing schedule), Community Manager (generates and executes the lease), Sales Manager (weekly unsigned-lease follow-up), Regional Manager (spot-checks, resident outreach, monthly compliance review). **Trigger:** Asset Management issues a renewal/rent increase notice, or a lease expiration approaches. **Ends when:** the lease is generated with the correct date range, signed (or the Tenancy Lease Acknowledgment is uploaded), status is Active, and Asset Management has confirmed the billing schedule.
- Five phases: identification & preparation → lease creation in Rent Manager Express → execution → quality control & activation → follow-up & compliance.
- **The Community Manager's 30-day action deadline starts at receipt of the renewal notice** and runs to the day the signed lease or acknowledgment is uploaded.
- **State notice periods as stated in this SOP:** Florida **90 days** (FL Stat. § 723.037); South Carolina **90 days** (SC Code § 27-47-420); Georgia **60 days** (O.C.G.A. § 44-7-60); Massachusetts **60 days** (MGL c. 140 § 32P); North Carolina **60 days** (NCGS § 42 / MH Act). **When in doubt, default to 90 days** — it satisfies all five minimums. A late notice makes the increase legally unenforceable until the full period has run from proper notice.
- **Community Managers cannot enter or change rent amounts in Rent Manager — only Asset Management may.** The source of truth for the approved amount is the mailed renewal notice uploaded to History & Notes. If it is not uploaded, contact Asset Management before proceeding. If the system amount looks wrong, do not override.
- **Click "Renew Lease," never "New Lease."** New Lease creates a duplicate record and breaks tenancy history — named as one of the most common errors in the process.
- Correct date range is always the rent increase effective date through 12 months later. Each property has **its own lease template**; if the right one cannot be found, contact the Regional Manager before proceeding.
- Two execution paths, both valid: **Path A digital** (Generate Lease Document → review merge fields → Publish → assign signers → send → track Published / Pending Approval / Signed) and **Path B manual** (print, wet sign, scan, upload to Tenant Profile → History/Notes → Lease Agreement).
- **The rent increase takes effect on the scheduled date whether or not the resident has signed.** An unsigned lease does not delay it.
- **Resident has 30 days from the send date to sign.** Unsigned at 30 days → escalate to the Regional Manager, who contacts the resident directly. Still refusing → complete the **Tenancy Lease Acknowledgment** (internal record only, not a substitute for a lease) and upload it. Generate the lease again next cycle regardless — the resident is month-to-month until officially vacated.
- **Massachusetts additional requirement (MGL c. 140 § 32P):** before any occupancy agreement is signed the resident must receive, **at least 72 hours before signing**, a full written disclosure package — full written occupancy agreement, itemized list of all charges and fees, names and addresses of all community owners, community rules and regulations, the verbatim "Important Notice Required by Law," and **a bona fide written offer of a five-year lease at fair market rates** as an alternative to the standard term. Signed by the owner/operator.
- If a resident says they intend to move out: generate the lease anyway and start the Move-Out SOP in parallel. If they sign after the increase took effect, the lease is still valid.
- Accounting does not currently receive a system notification when a lease activates — Asset Management updates the billing schedule manually, and the CM must confirm with them that it happened.
- **Systems used:** Rent Manager Express (Renew Lease, Generate Lease Document, e-signature, Pending Signatures, billing schedule), Rent Manager History & Notes.
- **Known problems:**
  - **South Carolina is 90 days here and 30 days in SOP 821 — both citing SC Code § 27-47-420.** 821 states "written notice at least 30 DAYS in advance of the effective date of the new rental rate," with a Parakeet internal standard of 60 days for margin. This SOP tells Community Managers 90. Two live SOPs, one statute, three numbers.
  - **Georgia's citation differs too:** 60 days under **O.C.G.A. § 44-7-60** here; 60 days under **O.C.G.A. § 44-7-7** (tenancy at will) in SOP 811.
  - **MA and NC statutory detail sits in this SOP with no compliance SOP behind it.** The 800s block covers FL, GA and SC only. The 72-hour disclosure package and the bona fide five-year lease offer — the most exposed requirements in the portfolio — have no counsel-reviewed SOP, no template and no compliance page.
  - The MA disclosure package is required "before any lease or occupancy agreement is signed," but **SOP 103 (Move In) has no 72-hour disclosure step** and no MA path at all.
  - "Generating a Lease in Rent Manager Express — step-by-step training reference **(to be re-uploaded)**": the training artifact this SOP points to is missing.
  - The Tenancy Lease Acknowledgment is referenced as a form but is not present in the library.
  - Last Revised is a blank line awaiting approval.
  - `_Video Links.txt` is an empty template; a 101 video was scheduled for the week of Aug 31, 2026.
