# 107 — Eviction

**What this is:** escalating an unresolved delinquency through demand notice, attorney intake, court, writ and auction to title recovery. **Primary state: Florida.** The process exists at two numbers, and which one is real is an open decision.

**Status:** in review (Tier 1 Critical). Effective August 2026, initial publication. Read 2026-08-30.

## The facts

- **Owner:** Operations. **Executes:** Community/District Manager, Regional Manager, Evictions Team, Melanie (Operations Coordinator), Justin (Asset Manager), Director of Operations (Natalie), attorney. **Trigger:** a balance remains after the 1st–5th AR review under SOP 100. **Ends when:** title is received and the home transfers to the Post Eviction Title Transfer SOP.
- **The monthly calendar:** 1st–5th AR review (SOP 100) · **6th** Evictions Team runs demand notices from the AR report and sends them to Regional Managers; RM reviews for accuracy; **posting and certified mailing both happen on the 6th**; proof of posting to the RM **by EOD on the 6th** · **7th** RM's compiled proofs to Natalie and evictions@parakeetcommunities.com; notices and posting certificates uploaded to Rent Manager · **8th to the cure date** daily AR review and resident calls · **19th–23rd** intake packets generated and sent to RMs · **by the 25th** completed packets returned to the RM · **last week of the month** packets forwarded to Evictions and on to the attorney.
- **The cure date is 10 business days from the posting date** (per this SOP). If a demand notice is returned as inaccurate and reissued, **the cure date extends 10 business days from the new posting date.**
- Service standard: **post on the resident's door AND send certified mail**, and the manager **signs the Posting Certification**. Community/District Managers must follow their own state's notice laws; the Evictions Team supplies state-specific guidance with each demand packet.
- **Intake packet must attach all five:** resident ledger, demand notice, signature/posting certification page, proof of certified mailing, lease agreement. Email subject: `Intake – [Property Name] – [Resident Name]`. **If an RV is to be demolished, mark it on the intake and answer "No" to "Is home worth more than $500.00?"**
- The Regional Manager calls the resident **before** the intake goes to the attorney — a final attempt to collect. If the intake is still incomplete at month end, the case is **deferred to the following month's cycle.**
- **Account lock:** the Evictions Team places the resident under the Eviction workflow in Rent Manager and locks the account — no online payments. **Only the Evictions Team and the Director of Operations may unlock an account.**
- **Payment to stop an eviction:** attorney fees must be posted to the account by the Asset Manager (after the Operations Coordinator confirms attorney billing) **before any payment is accepted**; the resident pays **cashier's check or money order only**; the CM requests a mailing label from Melanie, mails the payment to the home office, and photographs the check for the RM and Evictions. Evictions then asks the attorney to stop the case, and only closes the workflow **on written attorney confirmation.**
- **Writ of Possession is issued 30–90 days after the court processes the eviction.** It is uploaded to the Evictions tab in Rent Manager **the day it is issued.**
- After the writ, the CM inspects and reports: **VIN / HUD number / decal number / tag number, make and model, year, length and width.**
- **Auction path splits on lien status: no lien → Florida Fast Title; lien exists → Vector Law.** FFT will search for a missing VIN before scheduling. The Community Manager signs no documents at an FFT auction. **If the auction produces no buyer, Parakeet purchases the home for $1.**
- **Title timing: approximately 30 days after mailing; 5 to 7 months if the case is in declaratory judgment.**
- If a resident vacates voluntarily **and** turns over title, no auction is needed — title goes to Justin, the resident is moved out, and the home enters the TBD process. Vacates **without** title → auction proceeds from the lien-determination step.
- Payment plans are possible only during intake, **before** the packet reaches the attorney; after that it is full balance plus all attorney fees in certified funds.
- **Systems used:** Rent Manager (AR report, History/Notes, Eviction workflow, Evictions tab, account lock), Main Eviction Cases Sheet (SharePoint Operations › Evictions), evictions@parakeetcommunities.com, Florida Fast Title, Vector Law.
- **Known problems:**
  - **OPEN DECISION — the eviction process exists at two numbers and both files are live on disk.** `107-Eviction_Process_SOP_V1.docx` (Operations, August 2026, the current draft) and `800-eviction_sop.docx` (older, longer, the legacy Legal placeholder, with its own flowchart). Tracker v3 consolidated the two 800-series eviction rows into 107 and recorded: **"Final number 107 vs 800 still needs a call since 800-block is Legal." The owner was asked on 2026-08-30 and deferred the decision.** Both documents remain in place, unmarked, and neither should be treated as settled.
  - **The two documents disagree on who can unlock a locked account.** 107: "Only the Evictions team and the Director of Operations have permission to unlock an account." `800-eviction_sop.docx` role table: "**Asset Management (Justin) — Locks/unlocks resident accounts in Rent Manager.**"
  - **The 800 version contradicts its own calendar:** Step 16 and its monthly quick-reference put "**Intake packets sent to attorney — 8th of the month**," while its Phase 3 has intake packets *generated* on the 19th–23rd and forwarded in the last week. Packets go to the attorney eleven days before they exist.
  - **Cure periods do not reconcile across the library.** 107: **10 business days** from posting. SOP 100: Florida **5 business days** (FS 723.061). `800-eviction_sop.docx`: "**FL & SC: 5 days · GA: 3 business days · NC: 10 days · MA: 30 days**." SOP 802/803 (FL): **7-day cure plus a 5-day mailbox rule** on mailed notices under the same statute. Four constructions, all live.
  - **107 is explicitly Florida-first** ("with the understanding that other states may require state-specific notice periods and legal steps as directed by the Evictions team") and hands all state variation to the Evictions Team verbally. There is no GA, SC, NC or MA eviction path in the document, and no compliance SOP at all for NC or MA.
  - **The Main Eviction Cases Sheet has two owners.** Step 20 has the Evictions Team updating it; the role table has "Melanie (Operations Coordinator) … **maintains the Main Eviction Cases Sheet.**" The 800 version calls the same artifact the "Evictions Master Sheet."
  - The role table credits the Regional Manager with "**written warning issuance**" — a step that appears nowhere in the SOP body.
  - **RV homes appear only as a $500 checkbox on the intake form.** There is no RV eviction path, and the FL compliance SOPs route RV tenancies (Chapter 513) to Legal for classification before any notice is served.
  - The intake forms and the Posting Certification are central to the process and are not present in the library as files.
  - `_Video Links.txt` is an empty template; two 107 videos were scheduled for the week of Sep 14, 2026 — "the intake packet video will save Natalie hours of rework."
