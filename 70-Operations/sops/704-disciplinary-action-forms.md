# 704 — Disciplinary action forms

**What this is:** who may hold, complete and issue a disciplinary action form — a controlled-document policy that takes diagnosis away from Regional Managers and gives it to HR.

**Status:** draft, and an orphan: the only file in the entire 700-series band, absent from the tracker and from the wiki. Read 2026-08-30.

## The facts

- **Owner:** not stated in the document. **Executes:** Managers/Supervisors (report concerns), Regional Managers (gather facts), Human Resources (determines documentation and level of corrective action, prepares and approves), Director of Operations (confirms the diagnosis and co-signs). **Trigger:** an employee concern is reported. **Ends when:** the completed form and supporting documentation are with HR for retention in the personnel file.
- **The guiding principle, in the document's own order:** 1. Managers report concerns. 2. Regional Managers gather facts. 3. **Human Resources determines the appropriate documentation and level of corrective action.** 4. HR confirms the diagnosis with the Director of Operations. 5. **The employee receiving the write-up can verify the document's authenticity by the HR / Director of Operations sign-off.**
- **Distribution control: blank disciplinary action forms are accessible to Regional Managers only.** Managers, supervisors and other personnel are not to be given blank forms, and **forms must not be stored in shared locations accessible to unauthorised personnel.**
- **Regional Managers gather facts and supporting documentation. They are not required — and not authorised — to diagnose the situation or determine disciplinary action through the forms.** They may ask HR to prepare disciplinary documentation after reviewing the facts. Managers may also submit information directly to HR with the Regional Manager copied.
- **The validity rule: "Failure to have Disciplinary Documentation reviewed, approved (by HR and Director of Operations), and submitted to HR before issuing to an Employee will not be deemed valid."** HR must be consulted before any written disciplinary action issues; HR may prepare, review, revise or approve it.
- **PIPs: a Regional Manager may not independently prescribe, develop, issue or communicate a Performance Improvement Plan. Any PIP requires consultation with and approval from both Human Resources and the Director of Operations before implementation.**
- **Form-fit rule:** Regional Managers hold only a limited selection of forms. **If none fits the incident, do not use an unsuitable form — contact HR for guidance.**
- **The no-filtering rule:** if a Manager emails a Regional Manager to report a concern, **forward it to HR regardless of whether you personally agree with or endorse it.** Regional Managers must not restrict, filter or discourage reported concerns. When forwarding, note that no warning is being issued at that time but the concern is being submitted to HR for review.
- **State-law caution:** "Certain states have different laws that must be factored into disciplinary action… best practice to make sure [you] do not issue something that is not legally sound and compromises the Company (especially in writing)." When in doubt, contact HR before acting.
- **Systems used:** none specified — the process runs on email and the HR personnel file.
