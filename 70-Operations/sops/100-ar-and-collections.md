# 100 — AR & collections

**What this is:** the monthly collections cadence across all five states — AR report to demand notice to eviction intake, on a fixed calendar. The highest daily-use process in the portfolio.

**Status:** in review (Tier 1 Critical). Target 2026-10-31. Read 2026-08-30.

## The facts

- **Owner:** Natalie Magurne, Director of Operations. **Executes:** Community Manager / District Manager (execution and documentation), Operations Coordinator (produces state-specific demand notices), Regional Manager (weekly escalation review, sole approver of Stipulation Agreements). Visibility: all departments. **Trigger:** the 1st of each month, when the CM/DM pulls the AR report in Rent Manager. **Ends when:** the account leaves the AR report — paid in full, an approved and active Stipulation Agreement, or intake forms submitted initiating the Eviction SOP.
- **The calendar is the process.** 1st: AR report + initial reminder email. 3rd: follow-up email. 5th: email **and** text, urgent tone. 6th: Operations Coordinator produces state-specific demand notices; CM/DM posts and/or mails them. Before the notice deadline: proactive follow-up, Stipulation Agreement if appropriate. Before intake: a personal phone call to every remaining resident. **Deadline + 1 day: intake forms submitted.**
- **State cure periods as stated in this SOP:** Florida **5 business days** (5-Day Notice to Pay or Vacate, FL Stat. § 723.061); South Carolina **5 days from due date** (Written Demand Notice, SC Code § 27-47-530); Georgia **3 business days** (Notice to Pay or Quit, O.C.G.A. § 44-7-50); Massachusetts **30 days** with a 14-day cure in the notice (30-Day Termination Notice, MGL c. 140 § 32J); North Carolina **10 days** (10-Day Notice to Pay or Quit, NCGS § 42-3).
- **Massachusetts is flagged as the most protective tenant law in the portfolio** — a 30-day termination notice is required before any eviction action may be filed for non-payment, and the Operations Coordinator must build MA notices around that timeline.
- Every demand notice must state the outstanding amount, the state-specific deadline, and the consequence of non-payment. Delivery method must comply with state law (certified mail, posting, or both).
- **Only the Regional Manager may approve a Stipulation Agreement.** CM/DM may prepare and present one but must not communicate approval to the resident before RM sign-off.
- **A failed auto-pay does not pause or reset the collections timeline.** Residents are responsible for updating their own Tenant Web Access auto-pay after a rent increase. CM/DM checks TWA settings at Step 1 for every delinquent resident.
- An unconfirmed mailed check does not delay the cadence. If a resident pays after intake is submitted, notify the RM immediately — the RM decides whether to proceed or halt.
- "When in doubt, use the most protective (longest) cure period for that situation," and stop and confirm with the Regional Manager before issuing a notice if the applicable state is unclear.
- Everything — emails, texts, notices with delivery method and date, call outcomes, voicemails, stipulation terms and RM approval date, intake submission — goes in the resident's Rent Manager **History & Notes**. Incomplete documentation is named as a compliance risk that may undermine the eviction.
- **Systems used:** Rent Manager (AR report, History & Notes, notice templates), Tenant Web Access, email, SMS.
- **Known problems:**
  - **Florida's cure period has three different values in three live SOPs, two of them citing the same statute.** Here: **5 business days**, FS 723.061. In `800-eviction_sop.docx`: **"FL & SC: 5 days."** In `107-Eviction_Process_SOP_V1.docx`: **"The cure date is 10 business days from the posting date."** And the 800-series FL block (802, 803) describes **7-day cure periods under FS 723.061 plus a 5-day mailbox rule** for mailed notices — a different construction again. Nothing reconciles them, and this SOP hands directly to the eviction process.
  - **This SOP carries full statutory detail for Massachusetts and North Carolina, but the 800-series compliance block covers only FL, GA and SC.** MA and NC are live operating states (confirmed 2026-08-30) — so the operational SOP is running ahead of any compliance SOP or counsel review for two states, including the most tenant-protective one.
  - **RV parks are not addressed.** Chapter 513 tenancies are excluded or routed to Legal by the FL compliance SOPs, and no RV collections path exists here.
  - The "intake forms" that trigger eviction are named but never specified, attached or linked in this SOP — they live in 107 and 800.
  - The **Stipulation Process** is a separate Tier 2 tracker row with nothing started, even though this SOP makes Stipulation Agreements a core step and the RM the sole approver.
  - Last Revised is a blank line awaiting approval.
  - `_Video Links.txt` is an empty template. This was the **first process on the recording schedule** — three videos assigned to the week of Aug 10, 2026. None exist.
