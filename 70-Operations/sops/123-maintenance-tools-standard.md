# 123 — Maintenance tools standard

**What this is:** the standard tool inventory a community's maintenance operation is expected to hold, with 2025–2026 market pricing — a budgeting and equipping reference, not yet a process.

**Status:** draft. Spreadsheet only — there is no SOP document, no flowchart and no owner. Not on the tracker. Read 2026-08-30.

## The facts

- **Owner:** not stated anywhere in the file. **Executes:** not stated; the footer attributes it to "Parakeet Communities — Property Management Standards | Facilities & Maintenance." **Trigger:** none defined. **Ends when:** not defined — there is no procurement, approval or audit procedure.
- Four categories with per-item unit cost, recommended quantity, line total and a use note.
- **Electrical tools — subtotal $577.** Includes a multimeter ($45), **two** non-contact voltage testers ($25 each, "keep 2 on hand"), receptacle tester, **1,000V-rated insulated screwdriver set required for panel work** ($40), wire strippers/cutters/crimpers, circuit breaker finder ($55), portable LED work light, two flashlights/headlamps, and a **Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) kit ($75)**.
- **Plumbing tools — subtotal $629.** Pipe wrench set 10/14/18" ($65), adjustable wrench set, ratcheting PVC cutter to 2", hacksaw, strap wrench, PVC primer and cement (2 kits, "expires — check dates"), Teflon tape (**white for water, yellow for gas**), assorted fittings kit, pipe clamps ½"–2", two plumbing repair kits, trash pump hoses, and a **½ HP minimum submersible pump for flood response ($150)**.
- **Lawn care / grounds — subtotal $8,585**, the dominant cost. **Zero-turn mower with a 48"–52" commercial deck, $4,500** — 39% of the whole standard on one line. Plus a 21" self-propelled commercial push mower ($600), two commercial string trimmers, two 50–65cc backpack blowers, hedge trimmer, 16–18" chainsaw, pole saw, edger, loppers, pressure washer (3,000 PSI, $450), 4-gallon backpack sprayer, **separate labelled gas and mixed-fuel cans**, and a **minimum 6 cones + 2 caution signs** traffic set.
- **Essential general maintenance — subtotal $1,680.** Socket and combination wrench sets, cordless impact driver (18–20V, 1,750 in-lbs min), cordless impact wrench (½" drive, 250+ ft-lbs), cordless drill, spare batteries matched to the existing brand, levels, tape measures, reciprocating saw, circular saw, 4.5" angle grinder, **12 AWG minimum extension cords**, rolling toolbox, caution tape.
- **Total estimated investment: $11,471 per community**, before quantity adjustment.
- Stated basis: "mid-range commercial/professional grade tools (2025–2026 market avg)," sourced from Home Depot Pro, Grainger and Amazon Business. "**All costs are estimates — obtain quotes before purchasing.**" Quantities are to be adjusted per property size and existing inventory.
- **Systems used:** none — the file is a standalone Excel workbook.
- **Known problems:**
  - **There is no SOP.** 123 is a single spreadsheet with no procedure: nobody is named to conduct the inventory, request an order, approve spend, replace a lost tool, or audit compliance. The number implies a process that has not been written.
  - **No owner, no version, no date, no status field** anywhere in the workbook.
  - **Not on the SOP Master Tracker** — no row, no tier, no target.
  - **Not published in the wiki** — no `123` folder, so no flowchart, no `_Video Links.txt`, and no visibility in the index.
  - **A miscited safety cross-reference:** the LOTO kit note reads "Required per **SOP 108** / OSHA 1910.147." SOP 108 is Incident Reporting and contains no lockout/tagout content; the safety SOP is 122, which itself has no LOTO procedure. So a required safety control points at the wrong document, and the right document does not cover it.
  - **The PPE overlap is unreconciled.** This standard includes hi-vis/safety items adjacent to SOP 122's PPE matrix (traffic cones and caution signage here; hi-vis vests and boots there) with no statement of which document governs which purchase, or which budget carries it.
  - $11,471 per community across 67 active communities is a material capital number with no approval route, no phasing, and no link to the CapEx quote step in SOP 110 or the AVID CapEx workflow in SOP 300.
  - Quantities are stated as "Rec'd Qty" for an unspecified property size, so the total is not usable as a budget figure without the sizing rule that does not exist.
