# 103 — Move in

**What this is:** moving an approved applicant in — two paths, resident-owned and community-owned, differing mainly in which documents are required and whether an inspection happens.

**Status:** in review (Tier 1 Critical). Read 2026-08-30.

## The facts

- **Owner:** Natalie Magurne, Director of Operations. **Executes:** Community/District Manager on-site, new resident, Home Office / Asset Management. **Trigger:** the application is approved. **Ends when:** the resident is moved from Prospect to Resident in Rent Manager and the 3-day post-move-in check-in is complete.
- Six steps per path: welcome & digital lease → collect documents → completeness check → Meet & Greet (plus inspection on Path B) → Home Office confirms and updates Rent Manager → 3-day check-in.
- **Path A — resident-owned home.** Required: copy of title, bill of sale, signed lease, 1st month's rent, security deposit, pet fee if applicable, additional occupant fee if applicable. **No Move-In Inspection Form, no walk-through required.**
- **Path B — community-owned home / rental.** Required: signed lease, 1st month's rent, security deposit, **Move-In Inspection Form**, pet fee if applicable, additional occupant fee if applicable. No title or bill of sale. The inspection and walk-through are conducted with the resident and **both the on-site manager and the resident must sign the form.**
- **Gate: no move-in is approved without all required documents and a signed lease on file. Do not schedule the Meet & Greet, and do not move the resident from Prospect to Resident, until every item is confirmed.** Home Office will not update Rent Manager before then.
- Home Office does the system work in both paths: imports all documents, moves Prospect → Resident, changes unit status, confirms recurring charges.
- **The 3-day post-move-in check-in is required in both paths** and documented in Rent Manager. If missed, reschedule as soon as possible and document the attempt and outcome.
- Documenting lot condition at move-in is "always encouraged" for all home types but only required on Path B.
- **Systems used:** Rent Manager (Prospect→Resident, unit status, recurring charges, document imports), digital lease e-signature.
- **Known problems:**
  - **No prospectus delivery step, in either path.** SOP 804 makes prospectus delivery with a signed receipt the gate on every new Florida lot tenancy — "delivered BEFORE the enforceable rental agreement," and it says explicitly that **"Rent Manager move-in checklist blocks on this item."** 103 has no such item, no receipt, and no reference to 804. The video schedule lists "Rent Manager move-in entry + **prospectus receipt upload**" as a 103 video topic. The gate exists in the compliance SOP and nowhere in the operational one.
  - **No Massachusetts path.** SOP 101 requires the full MA disclosure package **72 hours before any occupancy agreement is signed** (MGL c. 140 § 32P), including the bona fide five-year lease offer. 103 sends the digital lease immediately on approval, with no 72-hour hold and no MA branch.
  - **No state variation of any kind** — one document, five states, and security deposit handling reduced to a line item on a checklist while three separate 800-series SOPs govern how deposits must be held, disclosed and returned.
  - The Move-In Inspection Form is central to Path B but is not present in the library as a file.
  - The Move-In Request Form used by the sales/closing SOPs (submitted the same day as closing, routed to Justin with Melanie copied) is not mentioned here, so the closing path and the move-in path describe the same event with different artifacts.
  - Last Revised is a blank line awaiting approval.
  - `_Video Links.txt` is an empty template; two 103 videos were scheduled for the week of Aug 17, 2026, paired with an "X-Resident Move-In Checklist" that is not in this folder.
