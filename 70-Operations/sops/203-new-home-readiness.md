# 203 — New home readiness

**What this is:** the two-gate sign-off a new home must clear before it can be listed or shown — a Construction installation gate, then an Ops & Sales buyer's-eye walk.

**Status:** draft. Version 1.0, effective August 2026. Read 2026-08-30.

## The facts

- **Owner:** Director of Construction. **Executes:** vendor/set crew, Director of Construction, Facility Manager, Community Manager, Sales Representative, manufacturer. **Trigger:** the vendor emails the Director of Construction confirming the home is set on the lot and ready for inspection. **Ends when:** all issues are resolved or formally routed and the Community Manager submits the Home Listing JotForm.
- **Gate 1 — the Construction / installation gate.** The vendor's email (subject line: community name, lot number, date set) is the official start trigger. **The Phase 1 inspection must be completed within 1 week of that notification.** The Facility Manager completes the Construction/Installation checklist on site — structural, mechanical and systems items — and submits it as the Phase 1 JotForm for the Director of Construction to approve. Failures are emailed to the vendor as a written list, corrected, and re-inspected.
- **Gate 1 clearance is a system event, not a conversation.** Approving the Phase 1 JotForm auto-notifies Construction, Operations and Sales. **"Phase 2 cannot begin until the Director of Construction has approved the Phase 1 JotForm and all failed items have been corrected and re-inspected. If the JotForm has not been received, Phase 2 does not start."** The SOP names interdepartmental communication failure as the most common breakdown in the process.
- **Gate 2 — the buyer's-eye walk.** The Community Manager and Sales Representative walk the home **together, within 1 week of Phase 1 clearance**, using the Ops & Sales checklist: does the home look, feel and function the way a buyer would expect — presentation, cleanliness, operation of all systems, curb appeal, **odour**.
- Every issue is logged in the Phase 2 JotForm **and** opened as a Rent Manager work order carrying location, description, photos and category.
- **Routing rule:** hook-up and on-site installation work — connections, sealing, levelling, tie-downs — is the **vendor/set crew's** responsibility. Warranty items — cracks in walls, factory defects in cabinetry or fixtures, appliance malfunctions not installation-related — go to the **manufacturer.** When in doubt, consult the Director of Construction before routing.
- A re-walk verifies resolution; work orders are closed only when all issues pass. Then the **Home Listing JotForm** clears the home for active listing, marketing and showing.
- The companion checklist document splits the physical inspection by department: **Construction** signs off structural installation, mechanical systems and code compliance before handoff to Operations — roof (shingles, ridge vent sealed, no exposed fasteners, eaves and fascia straight, windbreaker holes tarred, vents/boots/flues secured and sealed, flashing), underneath the home (main beam, centreline and perimeter blocked, marriage line seal secured), setup type recorded (piers / runners / full slab / stem wall) with model and serial number. **Facility Manager** completes utilities, mechanical and escalation sections; **Community Manager / Sales Representative** completes exterior through cleanliness before any showing. Every item is Yes or N/A — any No needs a written note and resolution before sign-off. **"Construction sign-off must be on file before this checklist is started. Physically verify every item — if it has not been checked, it is not confirmed."**
- **Systems used:** JotForm (Phase 1 checklist, Phase 2 issue log, Home Listing form), Rent Manager (work orders), manufacturer warranty portal with email as fallback.
- **Known problems:**
  - **The warranty reporting window is [TBD] — the one number that can forfeit money.** Step 12: "[TBD — confirm the number of days from home delivery within which defects must be reported to preserve warranty coverage.]" Interim instruction: submit as soon as identified. Until it is confirmed, nobody can tell whether a claim is late.
  - **The escalation path for a non-responsive vendor or manufacturer is [TBD]** — the FAQ says so outright, with an interim instruction to notify the Director of Construction and document the non-response.
  - **Both routing steps are broken.** Step 11 decides: warranty defect → "route to **manufacturer** (Step 12)"; installation issue → "route to **vendor/set crew** (Step 13)." Step 12 actually emails the Directors of Sales, Operations and Construction. Step 13 emails the Director of Operations and Director of Construction. **Neither step contacts the manufacturer or the vendor.**
  - **Step 13 is assigned to the Regional Manager, who is not in the roles table** and appears nowhere else in the document — while Step 11 hands the routing decision to the Community Manager.
  - **Two competing phase numbering schemes in one document.** The step tables read "PHASE 1 — Home Set Notification," "PHASE 2 — **Phase 1**: Installation Inspection," "PHASE 3 — **Phase 2**: Buyer's-Eye Walk," through PHASE 5. So "Phase 2" means both the second process phase and the second gate, in adjacent headings.
  - **Three SOP IDs for one document.** The header banner says **CONST-004**; the header table says **CONST-203**; and it cross-references "**CONST-202**" and "**CONST-200**" as upstream SOPs — while the actual IDs are CONST-003 (202) and CONST-002 (200a). Every cross-reference in the 200s block is broken.
  - **Not published in the wiki.** No `203` folder exists — the SOP, the by-department checklist and `203-home_readiness_flowchart.html` sit only in the drafting room, absent from the wiki index and the recording schedule.
  - **The tracker has no row for it.** The nearest row, "New Home Walkthrough Checklist" (Tier 2), is marked **Not started / raw material** and points at ROCK files — while a finished SOP and a 52 KB by-department checklist both exist.
  - The Phase 1 and Phase 2 checklists are described as JotForms and reproduced as Word tables in a separate file; no JotForm links exist, so the two artifacts cannot be reconciled.
  - This SOP's own gate ("no home advances to listing without both gates cleared") competes with SOP 001 Step 11, which lists a different six-item pre-listing checklist, and with SOP 105's Home Market Jotform handoff for rehab homes. Three different pre-listing gates.
