# 002 — Home sales

**What this is:** the park-owned-home sale from listing through accepted offer — the front half of the sales funnel, and the one populated process in the library with no SOP document of its own.

**Status:** in review (Tier 1 Critical). Read 2026-08-30.

## The facts

- **Owner:** Director of Sales & Marketing. **Executes:** Community Manager or Regional Sales Representative (submits the offer), Director of Sales (approves), Sales Assistant. **Trigger:** a home is listed and a prospect makes an offer. **Ends when:** the offer is accepted and approved, handing off to 003 closing prep.
- The documented sequence exists only as Lucid flowcharts: listing → offer submitted by form → notification → offer accepted or denied → **Offer Accepted / Pending Approval email** → update listing to Pending via a Change Request Form → application run → prospect approved or denied → cash or finance branch → obtain buyer documentation → closing file generated → compile closing package → alert team.
- The flowchart's closing package contents: finalized PSA, all disclosures, Closing Worksheet, Form 400 & 500 (SC), **Limited POA for Title**, Rules & Regs, Prospectus (FL), Bill of Sale. Executed documents must exist before closing day.
- On denial the flowchart branches to "Discuss other options with Prospect" and a team-notification email; Prospect and Asset records are updated in Rent Manager on every branch.
- The offer-to-approval clock lives in the 003/002-combined write-up, not here: **Director of Sales approves or rejects within 24 hours** of receiving the PSA.
- **Systems used:** Rent Manager (Prospect and Asset records, listing feed UDFs), CRM, JotForm / FastField offer forms, Lucid, email.
- **Known problems:**
  - **002 has no sales-process SOP document.** The file filed under it, `002-combined-home sale process.docx`, is titled "Home Sale Closing & Lease with Option to Purchase" and describes the *closing* process — phases 1 through 6, offer submission through asset deactivation. So the closing process now has three overlapping write-ups (002-combined, `003- Parakeet_Home_Sale_Closing_SOPv1.docx`, `003-CLOSING STEPS 05.05.2026.docx`) while the sales process itself has none.
  - Three Lucid exports sit side by side with no marker of which is current: `002_Home Sales Process - Home Sales Process lucid final.pdf`, `002_Home Sales Process updated -lucid flowchart draft.pdf`, and `_002-004 Home Sales to Closing`. The tracker's cleanup list names a further "Copy of 003-Sale Process" Lucid duplicate to archive.
  - The pieces that would make 002 a real process are all unbuilt: the tracker carries **Home Listing Process, Offer Submission Process, Open House Process, Sales Rep Property Walk, Marketing Photography and "Rehab Home For-Sale As-Is Steps"** as unnumbered recommended additions with partial or no drafts.
  - Two superseded root-level files with no number and no home — `HOME_sales process draft SOP.DOC` and `HOME_Sales process workflow V1.HTM` — sit in the drafting room beside the current material.
  - `_Video Links.txt` is an empty template; the schedule assigned two 002 videos to the week of Aug 17, 2026.
