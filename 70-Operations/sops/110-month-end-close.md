# 110 — Month-end close (community management)

**What this is:** the Community Manager's monthly financial, compliance and operational review of their community, signed off by submitting a JotForm checklist. **The two files that define it state two different deadlines.**

**Status:** in review. Initial release Aug 1 2026. **The tracker records "Month End" as not started** — two finished documents exist. Read 2026-08-30.

## The facts

- **Owner:** Community Manager — "CM owns this process," start to finish. **Executes:** CM/DM (all checklist tasks and the submission), Regional Manager (receives, confirms close, supports escalations), Operations Coordinator (receives completed submissions from the RM, routes to the Director of Operations for final sign-off). **Trigger:** start of the final week of each month. **Ends when:** the JotForm is submitted, the Regional Manager confirms close, and it reaches the Director of Operations.
- **If any item cannot be completed by the deadline, the CM escalates to the Regional Manager before the due date — not after.**
- Five steps, thirty-odd checks:
  - **Ledger & billing** — all payments posted; all miscellaneous charges correct (pet, guest, storage, force charges, violations); all rent increases effective this month posted correctly; late fees charged on all applicable accounts; water, sewer, electric, trash and tax postings validated.
  - **Resident accounts** — every Notice to Vacate received this month sent to regional and evictions and entered in Rent Manager; move-ins and move-outs fully processed; all prospects entered and stale ones closed; all evictions and stipulations posted and submitted.
  - **Compliance & documentation** — all incident reports completed and submitted; all citations, violations and insurance recommendations completed; **all home bills of sale, new leases and prospectuses uploaded to Rent Manager**; Rent Manager notes entered for every AR follow-up, violation, email, site visit and call; violation statuses current; mileage submitted for self and team.
  - **Maintenance & property condition** — all service work orders closed; all maintenance checklists reviewed and signed off; site visit inspections complete; all vacant homes meeting community standards and listed for sale or rent; new home and rehab inspection forms completed; **any incomplete maintenance task carries written documentation to the Regional Manager before the deadline — "no documentation is not an acceptable status at close."**
  - **Capital projects & submission** — all CapEx quotes submitted to the Regional Manager, then submit the checklist.
- **Submitting the JotForm is the official sign-off for the month** (`parakeet.jotform.com/233184482585060`).
- If the due date falls on a weekend or holiday, submit **the preceding business day / preceding Friday.**
- Every item is completed even when it does not apply — note the non-applicability in Rent Manager and submit the form in full regardless.
- **Systems used:** Rent Manager (system of record for every item), JotForm 233184482585060.
- **Known problems:**
  - **The deadline contradicts itself across and within the two files.** The SOP body, its FAQ ("What if I can't complete a task by the **25th**?") and its Rent Manager tracking table all say **12:00 PM on the 25th**. The same SOP's header table says **"Submitted by 12:00 PM on the 27th; month-end confirmed by Regional Manager, and submitted to DOO by 29th."** And `110-Month_End_Close_Checklist.docx` — the form CMs actually sign — says **"Submit completed JotForm by 12:00 PM on the 27th… Final DOO sign-off due by the 29th."** Two dates, two documents, and one of them inside a single file.
  - **The 25th deadline collides with SOP 301.** The Rent Roll Summary Report is *sent out* to Community Managers **on the 25th** for review, with Regional Managers consolidating on the 27th–28th and the Director of Operations approving on the 29th. A CM cannot close the month at noon on the 25th using a report that arrives the same day. The 27th/29th version of 110 lines up with 301; the 25th version does not.
  - **Process Owner is "Community Manager"** — the only SOP in the library whose owner is the role that executes it, with no director-level owner. Escalation, deadline changes and exceptions therefore have no named owner.
  - **The tracker is stale.** It lists "Month End Ops Close Out" (Tier 2, Operations) as **Not started** with raw material in ROCK, and separately "Month End Close (Accounting)" as Not started. Both 110 files are finished and dated August 2026.
  - **110 is not published in the wiki** — no `110 Month-End Close` folder exists, so the process is invisible in the wiki index and has no `_Video Links.txt`.
  - The checklist's compliance section drops items the SOP body includes: the SOP requires new leases and prospectuses uploaded; the checklist says only "all bills of sale have been uploaded."
  - The two 110 files overlap almost entirely — the SOP reproduces the checklist inside itself — with no marker of which one is the artifact of record.
  - Last Revised is a blank line awaiting approval.
