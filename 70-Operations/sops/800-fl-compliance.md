# 800 (FL) — Florida compliance: rent increases, violations, notice service, prospectus, rules changes, deposits

**What this is:** the six-SOP Florida compliance block — 801 rent increases and pass-throughs, 802 violations and enforcement, 803 notice service and documentation, 804 prospectus delivery, 805 rules changes, 806 security deposits. They cross-reference each other constantly and share one gate: get the notice right or the action is void.

**Status:** counsel review — all six read "DRAFT V1 — Pending Review," drafted 2026-08-06 from the 2025 Florida Statutes and the approved community prospectuses. **Florida counsel must review and approve before rollout.** Read 2026-08-30.

## The facts

- **Owner:** Natalie Magurne, Director of Operations, on all six. **Executes:** Asset Management / Finance (increase schedule, pass-through calculations, prospectus matrix), Legal / Compliance (prospectus verification, template library, notice approval, Division filings, mediation), Operations Coordinator (generates every notice from templates, calendars deadlines), Regional Manager (accuracy review before service, weekly proof-of-service audit, quarterly prospectus audit, approves every deposit claim), Community Manager (serves notices, captures proof, uploads same day), Accounting / Finance (holds deposits, issues refunds), Evictions Team (attorney intake). **Scope excludes RV parks (Chapter 513) and Georgia / South Carolina.**
- **The stake, stated in 801:** in Florida a defective increase notice does not just create legal risk — **it extends the resident's existing rental term at the old rate until proper notice is given (FS 723.031(5)).** Where a community prospectus is more restrictive than statute, **the prospectus controls.**
- **Two tracks run through the whole block: Track A — resident owns the home and rents the lot (Chapter 723); Track B — resident rents a park-owned home (Chapter 83, Part II).** Using the wrong track's notice voids the eviction and creates Fair Housing exposure. **RV tenancies and unclear ownership (unrecorded sales, inherited homes) STOP and route to Legal for classification before any notice is served.**

### 801 — rent increases & pass-throughs
- **90 days' written notice** before the effective date, to every affected homeowner **and** the homeowners' association board (FS 723.037(1)). Notice content: current lot rental amount, amount of the increase, effective date, and **each pass-through itemised separately with the name of the governmental entity.** One 90-day notice may cover multiple anniversary dates.
- **Increases no more than annually**; never mid-term except disclosed pass-throughs and disclosed annual adjustments in multi-year agreements (FS 723.031(5)). Non-discriminatory among similarly situated tenants; **homeowners cannot waive the 90-day right.**
- **Pass-through ceiling: never collect more than the amount actually remitted to the tax collector or utility**, and only where disclosed in the prospectus or before tenancy (FS 723.031(6)). Pass-throughs are each homeowner's proportionate share of the actual, documented cost, with the tax bill, assessment or utility invoice attached as backup.
- **Homeowners' committee (max 5 members) meets with the park owner no later than 60 days before the effective date** (FS 723.037(4)). **Mediation petition window: 30 days after the last scheduled meeting** (FS 723.037(5), 723.038) — Legal manages it, and nothing is discussed or negotiated outside that process.
- **Annual Division filing: copies of ALL 723.037 notices given in the preceding year, filed with the Division by January 1** (FS 723.037(2)).
- Phases run on a countdown: **T-150 to T-120** planning and prospectus verification · **T-120 to T-95** notice preparation and Legal approval · **no later than T-90** service, with 100% completion confirmed and the package logged for the January 1 filing · **T-90 to T-30** committee meeting and mediation window · effective date batch update in Rent Manager with a **10% spot-check** · first rent roll after the effective date reviewed for any account still billing the old amount.
- **PROSPECTUS VERIFICATION GATE — do this first, every year.** Every Florida community sits under a specific approved prospectus version (PA, P1, P2…), and residents **within one community may be under different versions** depending on move-in date. The consolidated matrix is `SharePoint > Asset Management & Finance > Prospectus Documents > _Prospectus - FL Consolidated > Prospectus - FL Communities.xlsx`. Confirm the version per resident cohort, and confirm the prospectus discloses the increase factors relied on (increased costs, prevailing market rent, prevailing economic conditions / CPI) and any pass-through authority.
- **Communities with NO PROSPECTUS ON FILE — STOP and route to Legal / Compliance on any rent increase: Geiger Key RV, Great Oak RV, Hunter Springs, Nalls Court, Paradise Lakes, River Oaks RV, Silver Breeze.** Parks under 26 lots are exempt from the prospectus requirement (FS 723.011) but still owe the 90-day notice under 723.037.

### 802 — violations & enforcement
- **Track A first violation: written notice, 7 days after delivery to cure**, specifying the rule or provision violated (FS 723.061(1)).
- **Track A second violation of the same nature within 12 months: no second cure period; eviction notice at least 30 days before the required vacate date — and the first-violation notice must have been given within 30 days of the first violation.**
- **Track A endangerment of life, health, safety or property: notice to vacate within 7 days after delivery, no cure period.**
- **Track A service: post on the premises AND certified or registered mail, return receipt requested. Mailed notice is deemed delivered 5 days after postmark — build that into every deadline calculation.** So a mailed Track A cure deadline is postmark + 5 days + 7 days.
- **Track B: 7-day written notice with opportunity to cure (statutory language required); 7-day unconditional notice to vacate for non-curable or repeat violations within 12 months** (FS 83.56(2)). Service by mail, hand delivery, or posting if the tenant is absent (FS 83.56(4)).
- Rules must be reasonable and disclosed as a prospectus exhibit (Track A) or in the lease (Track B) — **unreasonable or undisclosed rules are unenforceable** (FS 723.033, 723.035).
- **Fair Housing guardrail: same violation, same notice, same timeline, every resident, every time. Every exception requires Director of Operations approval in writing, documented in Rent Manager.** Nonpayment is not this SOP — it follows 100 and 107.
- Cure-window discipline: calendar the deadline, **inspect on the deadline date and photograph the outcome.** If cured, close the issue — but the 12-month repeat clock keeps running.

### 803 — notice service & documentation
- The service matrix: **Ch. 723 eviction-path notices → post AND certified/registered mail, both, not either; deemed given 5 days after postmark.** 90-day notices → written to every affected homeowner plus the HOA board, method per prospectus, certificate of mailing recommended. **Prospectus delivery → physical or documented electronic delivery BEFORE lease signing or occupancy, signed receipt required.** Ch. 83 notices → mail, hand delivery, or posting if absent. **Security deposit claim notice → certified mail to the last known address within 30 days of lease termination.**
- Every notice, every time: verify the resident's **legal** name against the lease or title (not the nickname), the lot, amounts and dates, and the correct statutory form for the track. Regional Manager approves before service.
- **Proof standard: photograph the posted notice ON the door with a visible date/time stamp, keep the certified mail receipt and tracking number, and upload the same day** — notice PDF, posting photo, mail receipt, and a note with service date, time and method. File naming: `[YYYY-MM-DD] [Notice Type] [Lot] [Resident]`. The same package also goes to `Operations > _Legal Notices` in SharePoint.
- **Returned mail is evidence: do not discard it. Photograph the unopened envelope, upload it, notify the Regional Manager.** Return receipts are uploaded to the same thread when they arrive.
- **Prohibited shortcuts, verbatim:** never serve a statutory notice by text, email or door knock alone unless the statute or lease explicitly allows it; **never backdate, predate or "re-print" a notice — the notice date, posting photo and postmark must agree**; never let a third party (maintenance, vendor, another resident) perform service; never start an eviction, late fee or enforcement action on a notice whose proof of service is incomplete.
- Regional Manager audits every open notice weekly; **incomplete files are re-served correctly before any escalation.**

### 804 — prospectus delivery, tracking & remediation
- **Deliver the Division-approved prospectus BEFORE the enforceable rental agreement — prior to execution or occupancy, whichever comes first** (FS 723.011). Delivery happens with the application/approval, **never at closing.**
- **The complete package: prospectus plus ALL exhibits (park plan, rental agreement form, park rules, disclosures). Partial delivery is non-delivery.** The resident signs a receipt recording date, version ID and lot — "the single most important signature in the file after the lease" — uploaded to Rent Manager **before the lease is signed**, and the Rent Manager move-in checklist blocks on it.
- **The lot rental agreement is voidable by the lessee for 15 days after delivery.** Calendar it: no non-refundable commitments inside the window without Legal sign-off.
- Renewals need no new delivery if the original receipt is documented. **Parks with 26+ lots file with the Division; the Division has 45 days to review and silence means deemed adequate.**
- Assumptions: a buyer assuming the seller's agreement gets **the same prospectus version the seller was under**, with receipt; a buyer signing a new agreement gets the current version. **FS 723.058: the park cannot deny a resident's right to sell in the park** — purchaser qualification runs through standard screening (SOP 006).
- **Quarterly audit:** the Regional Manager reconciles all new tenancies against signed receipts on the Prospectus Review Tracker. A missing receipt is delivered immediately — **late delivery does not cure the original defect** — and that resident's increases need Legal review.
- **Remediation list:** Geiger Key RV Resort, Great Oak RV Resort, River Oaks RV Resort (Chapter 513 — confirm classification, no prospectus needed) and Hunter Springs MHC, Nalls Court, Paradise Lakes, Silver Breeze (verify lot count; if 26+, file a prospectus with the Division). **Until an approved prospectus is delivered at a 26+ lot community, treat increases there as statute-only — 90-day notice, no prospectus-based pass-throughs, Legal approval on every notice.** Prospectus status is a Due Diligence checklist item on acquisitions and no post-close increase is scheduled until the matrix is updated.

### 805 — rules & regulations changes
- **90 days' written notice** to every affected homeowner plus the HOA board, describing the change and its effective date (FS 723.037(1)); **residents cannot waive it.** Same **60-day committee meeting** and **30-day mediation petition** windows as 801, and the same **January 1 Division filing.**
- **Rules must be reasonable and not arbitrary or capricious to be enforceable** (FS 723.033); rules are a prospectus exhibit and amendments follow Division filing requirements.
- Countdown: **T-150 to T-120** proposal with a written business reason and Legal reasonableness review · **no later than T-90** notice · **T-90 to T-30** committee and mediation · effective date rollout, with the Operations Coordinator updating Rent Manager Express document templates and the Community Manager updating move-in packets.
- **The Director of Operations approves all rule changes portfolio-wide — "one standard, not fifty."**

### 806 — security deposits
- **Holding: a separate Florida account (non-interest or interest-bearing) or a surety bond. Never commingle with operating funds** (FS 83.49(1)). Lot deposits held over 3 months follow s. 83.49 (FS 723.031(9)).
- **Written holding disclosure to the tenant within 30 days of receiving the deposit** — manner of holding, account type, interest terms — or in the lease itself (FS 83.49(2)). If interest-bearing, the tenant receives **75% of annualized average interest or 5% simple**, per election.
- **No claim → return the full deposit plus any interest within 15 days after termination of the tenancy.**
- **Claim → send the claim notice by certified mail to the last known address within 30 days after termination, using the exact statutory language.**
- **Miss the 30-day notice and Parakeet FORFEITS the right to impose any claim** — it may still sue separately for damages but cannot touch the deposit.
- **Tenant objection window: 15 days from receipt of the claim notice. No objection → deduct and remit the balance within 30 days of the original notice.**
- Regional Manager approves every claim before the notice goes out, and audits open deposit timelines weekly.

- **Retention across the block: 7 years minimum.** Compliance pages are re-verified against current statutes annually, because statutes change every legislative session.
