# 800 (SC) — South Carolina compliance: rent increases, violations & eviction, notice service, deposits

**What this is:** the four-SOP South Carolina block — 821 rent increases and rental agreement requirements, 822 violations/eviction/abandoned home, 823 notice service, 824 security deposits. SC has a manufactured-home chapter (27-47) alongside the landlord-tenant act (27-40), so **track classification comes first** — and it gives Parakeet an abandoned-home auction path Georgia does not have.

**Status:** counsel review — all four read "DRAFT V1 — Pending Review," drafted 2026-08-06 from the current SC Code Title 27, Chapters 47 and 40. **South Carolina counsel must review before rollout.** Read 2026-08-30.

## The facts

- **Owner:** Natalie Magurne, Director of Operations, on all four. **Executes:** Asset Management / Finance (increase schedule by community and anniversary), Legal / Compliance (**rental agreement form audit against 27-47-310**, track classification, template library, auction process under 29-15-10, attorney coordination), Operations Coordinator (generates SC notice packages, itemisation templates and calendars), Regional Manager (accuracy review, **tracks resident continuation responses**, weekly clock audits, approves every retention), Community Manager (service, proof, move-out inspection with photos), Accounting / Finance (holds SC deposits, issues refunds), Evictions Team (magistrate court ejectment intake).
- **Two tracks: Track A — resident-owned home on a rented lot (SC Code 27-47); Track B — park-owned home rental (SC Code 27-40). Unclear title → Legal before any notice is served.**

### 821 — rent increases & rental agreement requirements
- **Track A lot rent increase: written notice at least 30 DAYS in advance of the effective date of the new rental rate** (SC Code 27-47-420). **Parakeet standard: serve at 60 days to leave margin.**
- **The continuation election — unique to SC: the resident must give written notice within 30 days of receiving the new-rate notice stating whether the tenancy continues** (SC Code 27-47-510). Track the responses. **Silence is a flag for follow-up, not an eviction event by itself.**
- **Track A written rental agreements must state, per 27-47-310:** lot location and size, monthly rate, due date and place, services provided, park regulations, fees and deposits, permitted improvements, pet and occupancy rules, and renewal notice requirements. Legal audits the agreement form against this list.
- **No new user fee for a service previously included in rent unless rent decreases correspondingly** (27-47-310).
- **Track B (park-owned home rentals): increases at renewal per the lease; month-to-month changes or termination require 30 days' written notice, week-to-week 7 days** (SC Code 27-40-770).

### 822 — violations, eviction & abandoned home
- **Track A rental agreement breach: written notice; eviction may proceed if the breach is not remedied within 14 DAYS of notice** (SC Code 27-47-530).
- **Track A nonpayment: an eviction ground once rent is 5 DAYS past due**, with written notice, coordinated with the SOP 100 demand cadence.
- Other Track A grounds: code violations after notice, disruptive or dangerous conduct, false statements on the application, health and safety violations.
- **Track A writ timing: a writ of ejectment does not issue until 10 days after verdict** (except health/safety evictions). Calendar that delay before scheduling set-out.
- **The abandoned home path — Track A's advantage: a home left on the lot 20 DAYS after eviction may be sold by the park owner at a commercially reasonable PUBLIC AUCTION under SC Code 29-15-10** — proper advertising, documented costs. **The former resident may reclaim pre-sale by covering filing and advertising costs, collected before release.** Sale proceeds and title work route through the Corporate Title Specialist and tie into the Post Eviction Title SOP.
- **Track B lease violation: 14-day written notice specifying the breach; the agreement terminates on the stated date if not cured** (27-40-710). **Track B nonpayment: 5 days past due plus written notice — and the conspicuous lease clause ("IF YOU DO NOT PAY YOUR RENT ON TIME…") satisfies the notice requirement if it is present in the lease.** Verify whether the lease carries it before generating a separate notice.
- Escalation: not cured → Evictions Team builds the magistrate court ejectment intake, with the Director of Operations and evictions@ copied.

### 823 — notice service & documentation
- Service matrix: **lot rent increase (Track A) → written notice ≥30 days before the effective date (Parakeet standard 60), including the resident 30-day continuation election** (27-47-420, 27-47-510). **Track A breach / nonpayment / eviction notices → written notice specifying the ground; 14-day cure for agreement breaches; keep the delivery proof for the magistrate.** **Track B 14-day cure / 5-day rent → written notice, with the conspicuous lease clause substituting for the 5-day rent notice if present.** **Periodic tenancy termination (POH) → 30 days month-to-month, 7 days week-to-week, written** (27-40-770). **Deposit itemisation → written itemised notice within 30 days.**
- Community Manager serves, photographs and uploads proof the same day; Regional Manager audits proof of service weekly; Legal owns the templates.

### 824 — security deposits
- **Track A: return within 30 DAYS after termination, less itemised documented deductions for unpaid rent or damages, with written itemisation** (SC 27-47-520).
- **Track B: itemised written notice of any deduction and the amount due within 30 DAYS after either (a) termination and delivery of possession, or (b) tenant demand — whichever is LATER** (SC 27-40-410).
- **Penalty on both tracks: wrongful withholding = 3x the amount wrongfully withheld plus reasonable attorney fees.**
- **Normal wear and tear is not deductible — document actual damage with photos against move-in condition.**
- Regional Manager approves every retention and audits the clocks weekly.

- **Systems used:** Rent Manager (SC notice templates, History & Notes, continuation-response tracking), SharePoint (proof of service), SC deposit account, magistrate court through counsel.
- **Known problems:**
  - **South Carolina's rent increase notice period is 30 days here and 90 days in the operating SOP — both citing SC Code 27-47-420.** 821: "written notice at least **30 DAYS** in advance," Parakeet standard 60. `101-lease_renewal_sop.docx`: South Carolina **90 days**, § 27-47-420, and instructs Community Managers to "default to 90 days" when unsure. Field staff are being told 90 while the compliance SOP says the statute is 30 and the company standard is 60. **This is the sharpest single number conflict in the library and it is in active daily use.**
  - **The continuation election has no tracking mechanism.** 821 requires the Regional Manager to track written continuation responses within 30 days of the rate notice, and no Rent Manager field, report or log is specified. Nothing detects a resident who never responded.
  - **824's photo comparison depends on move-in condition documentation that SOP 103 does not require** for resident-owned lot tenancies — so on the most common SC tenancy there is no move-in baseline to compare against.
  - **822's 20-day abandoned home auction is not in SOP 107 or the title SOPs.** 107 runs a Florida-shaped auction through Florida Fast Title (no lien) or Vector Law (lien); the Post-Eviction and Resident Turn-In title SOPs name **only Florida Fast Title.** The SC statutory auction path under 29-15-10 exists in the compliance SOP and in none of the operating or title processes.
  - **`800-eviction_sop.docx` contradicts 822 on the nonpayment threshold:** it groups "FL & SC: 5 days" for demand-notice cure, while 822 makes 5 days past due the *ground* and 14 days the cure period for agreement breaches — two different clocks conflated.
  - **Form 400 and Form 500 appear only in the sales and closing SOPs**, described as "South Carolina only," and are not mentioned in any of the four SC compliance SOPs — so the SC-specific transaction forms have no compliance basis documented.
  - **All four SOPs have blank Effective Dates** and have not been to counsel. The recording schedule HOLDS the SC notice video "until SC counsel signs off."
  - **Every file in this block exists twice on disk** — `Process Wiki/800-899 Legal & Compliance/821-824 South Carolina/` and `_SOP Drafts & Master Tracker/821-824 South Carolina Compliance/` — byte-identical, with no authoritative marker.
  - `_Video Links.txt` for the SC block is an empty template; the 822 notice video was scheduled for the week of Sep 21, 2026.
