# 201 — New home delivery & set

**What this is:** receiving a new infill home on site — arrival documentation, walk-around inspection, Bill of Lading, delivery confirmation form, Rent Manager log. Everything inside 24 hours.

**Status:** draft — "DRAFT: Needs Approval (open TBD items)." Version 1.0, July 2026. Tier 1 Critical. Read 2026-08-30.

## The facts

- **Owner:** Construction. **Executes:** Construction Team Member (primary), Community Manager (first backup), Regional Manager (second backup), Operations (secondary). **Trigger:** the transporter arrives on site with a new infill home. **Ends when:** the delivery confirmation form is submitted and delivery notes are logged in Rent Manager under Issues > Infill.
- Five phases: pre-delivery preparation & arrival → exterior and roof inspection → delivery acceptance & Bill of Lading → delivery confirmation form → Rent Manager update.
- **One of Construction Team Member, Community Manager or Regional Manager must be on site — "delivery cannot proceed without an authorized Parakeet representative present."**
- **The lot must be confirmed vacant, clear and accessible the day before delivery.** Any obstruction is resolved before the delivery date. The representative meets the transporter and guides the home in slowly to prevent damage.
- **Arrival documentation:** video of the home being brought into the community, and photographs on placement.
- **Gate — inspect before you sign.** Full walk-around of walls, siding, windows, doors and roof for dents, cracks, scrapes, loose parts and misalignments; photograph all damage. **"Do not sign the Bill of Lading before this inspection is complete. Signing without noting damage waives Parakeet Communities' ability to file a claim with the manufacturer."**
- **The home is accepted regardless of damage — do not refuse delivery.** Damage is noted on the Bill of Lading before signature and reported with photos; Construction coordinates the repair claim with the manufacturer separately. Both steps are required to trigger a claim.
- Signing the Bill of Lading is the formal transfer of legal responsibility from transporter to Parakeet. Keep a physical copy of all signed documents, scan it, and file it in the Infill Folder.
- **Delivery confirmation form, on a mobile device immediately after signing**, with fields entered in this exact sequence: (1) community name → (2) lot number → (3) home serial number → (4) manufacturer → (5) transporter name. Out-of-sequence entry causes downstream data errors requiring manual correction.
- **Photos uploaded in the order the form prompts; the serial number photo must be legible** — it is the primary identifier for home tracking, and a blurry or dark shot means a return visit. Name, email and a digital signature finalise the submission, and the confirmation is saved.
- Successful submission **auto-distributes the delivery record to Construction, Operations, Sales, Accounting and Ownership.** The Champion's ground team confirms delivery independently during tie-downs.
- **No cell service is not an excuse:** move to an area with service and submit the same day.
- Rent Manager: log delivery notes under **Issues > Infill** and confirm the entry saved.
- **All five steps must be completed within 24 hours of the home arriving on site.**
- **Out of scope:** rehab homes, resale homes, and **tie-down and home setup** (a separate SOP).
- **Systems used:** JotForm ("Jotform Home Drop Confirm"), Rent Manager (Issues > Infill), Infill Folder in SharePoint, Champion Sheet (downstream).
- **Known problems:**
  - **Three open [TBD] items, and the SOP says so itself:** "(1) Jotform Home Drop Confirm link [TBD] | (2) SharePoint Infill Folder link [TBD] | (3) Bill of Lading retention period [TBD]. **This SOP cannot be marked Approved until all three are resolved.**" Two of the three are the locations where the process's two artifacts are supposed to go.
  - **JotForm vs FastField for the same form.** 201 calls it the "**Jotform** Home Drop Confirm" throughout. SOP 001, describing the same event, says "Complete the **Home Drop Confirmation form in the TBD app**" and then "(**Fastfield**- Home Delivery confirmation form)." The tracker's source note for 201 adds a third label: "ROCK: **Home Delivery Confirmation JotForm SOP**."
  - **The damage-report distribution list differs between the body and the role table.** Phase 2 Task 2: email photos and notes to "**Sales, Operations, Accounting and the Manufacturer**." The Phase 2 role table: "Emails damage report to **Sales, Operations and manufacturer**." Accounting is in one and not the other.
  - **This SOP's photo standard is looser than 001's for the same delivery.** 001 requires "video of the delivered home OR a minimum of 5 photos (each side plus VIN plate)" plus a serial number photo and an exterior checklist based on the manufacturer's checklist. 201 requires a video, placement photos and form-prompted photos, with no minimum count and no manufacturer checklist.
  - "Triggers Downstream: **Champion Sheet Update**" — a manufacturer-specific artifact named nowhere else in the library, with no SOP behind it.
  - **The set process is missing.** The number is published in the wiki as "201 New Home Delivery **& Set**" and the tracker row is "New Home Delivery / Set / Utility / Sales Release," but the document explicitly puts tie-down and setup out of scope. Set and sales release live partly in 001 Step 10 and partly in 203 — no single SOP covers set.
  - The wiki folder pairs the SOP with `JC HOME DELIVERY PROCEDURE PP_.pdf` (1.4 MB), described in the recording schedule as "the script" — a second, unversioned source for the same process.
  - `_Video Links.txt` is an empty template; two 201 videos were scheduled for the week of Aug 17, 2026.
