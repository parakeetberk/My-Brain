# 005 — POH title transfer

**What this is:** getting title into the buyer's name after a park-owned home sale closes — a mail-and-vendor process run by the Title Department through Florida Fast Title.

**Status:** in review. Read 2026-08-30.

## The facts

- **Owner:** Title Department. **Executes:** Community Manager (with Regional Manager copied), Title Department, buyer, Florida Fast Title (FFT). **Trigger:** closing is finalised. **Ends when:** title is received, uploaded to the resident's profile and the Asset Transfer tab, and physically handed to the buyer.
- Seven steps: manager emails the closing documents to Title → Title drafts 3 POAs → buyer signs all 3 in blue ink → Title completes the title package → package goes overnight to FFT → FFT processes → title returns and is delivered to the buyer.
- **What the manager must send, with the Regional Manager copied:** bill of sale or purchase agreement, and a copy of the resident's ID. Acceptable ID is narrow: a state-issued driver's licence or ID card with photo, or a US passport with proof of valid visa.
- **Three Power of Attorney copies, signed in blue ink.** Scanned copies go back to Title by email; Title provides a label to mail the originals to the Maryland office.
- Title package contents: Cover Sheet, Title, POAs, copy of buyer's ID.
- **Title transfer through FFT takes approximately 7 to 10 business days.** FFT updates its customer portal once documents are received.
- Title updates the **Asset Transfer tab in Rent Manager** with all transfer details, and on receipt uploads title information to the resident's profile and the Asset Transfer tab.
- Delivery of the physical title is the Community Manager's call — community office or direct to buyer. If it goes to the office, the manager must notify the Regional Manager and Title once it is handed to the buyer. The flowchart adds a step the SOP text omits: **the resident signs an acknowledgement document**, which is scanned and emailed back to the Operations Coordinator.
- **Systems used:** Rent Manager (Asset Transfer tab, resident profile), Florida Fast Title customer portal, email, overnight mail.
- **Known problems:**
  - **No SOP document exists — 005 is a PDF.** Both files in the wiki folder are PDFs (`005-Park Owned Home Sales Title Transfer SOP.pdf`, `005_Home Title Transfer Process.pdf`), so there is no Word source and no header table: no process owner, no version, no effective date, no revision history, no status field.
  - **"Title Department" is undefined.** No SOP in the library says who the Title Department is. The same function is called Title Department here and in the turn-in SOP, **Evictions** in the post-eviction SOP, **Closing Coordinator / Operations Coordinator (Melanie S.)** in 003/003a, **Title Coordinator** in `003-CLOSING STEPS 05.05.2026.docx`, and **Corporate Title Specialist (Ashlyne)** in the video recording schedule. Five names for one desk.
  - **Florida-only.** Florida Fast Title is the only vendor and the only path documented, in a five-state portfolio. There is no GA, SC, NC or MA title process anywhere in the library.
  - The 7–10 business day estimate is a vendor estimate with no internal deadline, no escalation path and no tracking artifact beyond the Asset Transfer tab.
  - The flowchart's resident-acknowledgement step and Operations Coordinator handoff are not in the written SOP.
  - A superseded draft, `005- draft MS Sales Title Transfer Process workflow.pdf`, sits in the drafting room with no `_Archive` folder to hold it.
  - The tracker flags the real gap: **"Title Work Process (Master)" is Tier 1 Critical, partial draft, "covered piecemeal by 005, Post-Eviction, Resident Turn-In"** — nobody has unified them.
  - `_Video Links.txt` is an empty template; a 005 video was scheduled for the week of Sep 14, 2026 with Ashlyne named as recorder.
