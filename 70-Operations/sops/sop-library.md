# SOP library — the process wiki

**What this is:** the readable layer over Parakeet's process library — 242 files in `70-Operations/sops/`, covering the 24 documented processes across nine department bands, and what is wrong with them.

**Status:** current as of 2026-08-30. The wiki itself is v1, assembled 2026-08-07; the scoreboard is `SOP_Master_Tracker_v3.xlsx`, rebuilt 2026-08-06.

## The facts

- **One approved process in the entire library.** 200 Demo & Lot Prep. Everything else is draft, in review, or awaiting counsel.
- **Zero videos exist.** All 27 `_Video Links.txt` files are empty templates, against a recording schedule that had every populated process covered by the week of 2026-09-28.

### The four zones

- **`Process Wiki/`** — the published layer, v1 2026-08-07. 22 numbered process folders plus five state/title folders, each holding an SOP, a flowchart and a `_Video Links.txt`. Four scaffolded band folders (400s–700s) hold only `_README.txt` files listing what is queued.
- **The flat root** — the drafting room. Mostly byte-identical copies of the wiki files, plus everything drafted *after* the wiki was assembled: 104's newer Ops SOP, 105b, 108, 110, 122, 123, the 200 rewrite, 202, 203, 704. Ten documented processes exist only here and are invisible to anyone reading the wiki index.
- **`_SOP Drafts & Master Tracker/`** — the 14 state compliance SOPs (a second byte-identical copy of the FL, GA and SC blocks) and the current tracker, `SOP_Master_Tracker_v3.xlsx`.
- **The ROCK raw material** — already moved out to `99-Archive/rock-processes/` (77 files), the source of most "Raw material — needs formalizing" tracker rows.

## Known problems

Nineteen issues, by theme. Each is stated in full on the relevant process page.

- **One open decision the owner has deferred.** The eviction process exists at two numbers — `107-Eviction_Process_SOP_V1.docx` (Operations, Aug 2026, current) and `800-eviction_sop.docx` (older, longer, the legacy Legal placeholder). The tracker says "final number 107 vs 800 still needs a call." **The owner was asked on 2026-08-30 and deferred.** Both files remain on disk; neither is authoritative, and they disagree on cure periods and on who may unlock a locked account. See [[70-Operations/sops/107-eviction]].
- **Statutory numbers contradict each other between the operating SOPs and the compliance block.** Florida's cure period has four live values (5 business days in SOP 100, 7 days plus a 5-day mailbox rule in 802/803, 10 business days in 107, 5 days in 800). South Carolina's rent increase notice is 90 days in SOP 101 and 30 days — with a 60-day company standard — in SOP 821, both citing SC Code 27-47-420. Georgia's 60-day notice cites 44-7-60 in SOP 101 and 44-7-7 in SOP 811. Field staff are following the operating SOPs.
- **Two states operate with no compliance SOPs at all.** SOPs 100 and 101 carry full statutory detail for Massachusetts and North Carolina — including the MA 72-hour pre-signing disclosure package and the bona fide five-year lease offer, flagged as the most tenant-protective law in the portfolio — while the 800s block covers only FL, GA and SC. **The owner confirmed on 2026-08-30 that MA and NC are live operating states, so this is a live compliance gap, not a documentation artifact.**
- **RV parks are largely unaddressed.** SOPs 801, 802 and 804 explicitly exclude or route them — "RV parks are Chapter 513 — no prospectus applies; route to Legal" — and **no Chapter 513 / RV compliance SOP exists anywhere**, despite RV being a large part of the portfolio. RV appears in the operating SOPs only as a $500 checkbox on the eviction intake form.
- **A hard compliance gate that no operating SOP implements.** SOP 804 makes prospectus delivery with a signed receipt the gate on every Florida lot tenancy and states the Rent Manager move-in checklist blocks on it; SOP 103 has no prospectus step. The FL/GA/SC deposit SOPs set 15- and 30-day clocks running from termination of tenancy; SOP 102 has no deposit step. Communities with no prospectus on file — **Geiger Key RV, Great Oak RV, Hunter Springs, Nalls Court, Paradise Lakes, River Oaks RV, Silver Breeze** — must STOP and route to Legal on any rent increase.
- **The closing process is documented three times and the numbering is wrong.** `002-combined-home sale process.docx` is filed under 002 Home Sales but its content is the closing process — so 002 has no sales SOP, only Lucid flowcharts, while closing has three overlapping write-ups. `003-CLOSING STEPS 05.05.2026.docx` sits in the 004 folder with a 003 prefix and describes a materially different JotForm-driven generation with a Title Coordinator role and a Make Ready Board gate that SOPv1 lacks. Two generations, both live.
- **The same fact has different values inside single documents.** The rejected-offer response window is 3 days, 2 days, 2 days and a missing number across four closing documents. E-sign versus wet-sign by state contradicts itself inside `002-combined` — a global rule says SC and GA must wet-sign, and its own Phase 4A puts Georgia on SignEasy. SOP 110's own header says the 27th while its body, FAQ and tracking table say the 25th. SOP 200 demands "all three" closeout documents in one place and "all four" in another.
- **Status fields lie.** `003- Parakeet_Home_Sale_Closing_SOPv1.docx` and `003a` both read "Status: Approved" in their own tables while the tracker, the wiki index and the wiki folder all mark them drafts. The one genuinely approved SOP, 200, has a revision history that says "Initial draft" and carries no approver or approval date.
- **The tracker is stale on at least seven processes.** It records Incident Reporting and Month End as "not started / Tier 1 gap" while `108-Incident_Report_SOP.docx` and `110-Month_End_Close_SOP.docx` are finished documents. It has **no row at all** for 122 (Safety & PPE), 123 (Maintenance Tools), 202 (Site Condition Assessment v3.0), 203 (New Home Readiness) or 704 (Disciplinary Action Forms).
- **Ten documented processes are missing from the published wiki.** 104's current Ops SOP, 105b, 108, 110, 122, 123, the 200 rewrite, 202, 203 and 704 exist only in the drafting room. The wiki's 200a folder holds no SOP and no flowchart — only a note saying the chart is still in Lucid. The 700-series README says "no processes published yet" while 704 sits unnumbered in the root.
- **Form platform is unresolved, sometimes inside one document.** `105-POH rehab process` names the **FastField** punchlist form in one task and the **Jotform** punchlist four lines later; 105b calls it the Rehab Walkthrough Jotform. SOP 001 uses FastField for delivery confirmation; SOP 201 uses JotForm for the same form. The tracker's own fix — "Jotform Process (Form Ecosystem): consolidate all Jotform SOPs into ecosystem doc" — is a Tier 2 row with nothing started.
- **The same form has three owners.** The Utility Request JotForm is filed by the Community Manager and routed to Maintenance in SOP 104, submitted by Construction and executed by Accounting in 303a, and submitted by Operations in 303b.
- **A competing ID scheme runs through the 200s.** 200a is CONST-002, 202 is CONST-003, and 203 is CONST-004 in its banner and CONST-203 in its table — then cross-references "CONST-202" and "CONST-200," neither of which exists. `Parakeet_Lot_Mapping_SOP_1.docx` uses a third form, **SOP-LOTS-001**. None of it aligns with the nine-band numeric scheme.
- **Roles are named inconsistently or not at all.** The title function is called Title Department, Evictions, Closing Coordinator, Operations Coordinator, Title Coordinator and Corporate Title Specialist across six documents. Six individuals appear in exactly one SOP each and nowhere else: **Clarissa** (301), **Derek** (303a), **Gabe** (303b), **Alex S** (104), **Keiter Abreu** and **Jhoiner Perez** (006 PDF). 200a lists "Facilities Manager (FM)" and "Facility Manager (FM)" as two roles with one abbreviation. SOP 104's data-entry draft has Process Owner **[TBD]**; SOP 704 names no owner at all.
- **Open [TBD] markers block approval on their own documents.** 201 states it "cannot be marked Approved until all three are resolved" (JotForm link, SharePoint folder link, Bill of Lading retention). 203's manufacturer warranty reporting window is [TBD] — the number that decides whether a claim is late. 302's missed-lender-deadline consequence and its Repo Tracker are [TBD], with an email thread as the interim tracker, while the SOP publishes a lender's full wire instructions and ABA number. 108's HR email is [TBD]. 105's 1-week extension process is [TBD]. 001 has a "PENDING DECISIONS" heading with no content. 105b's GL codes are all placeholder `[GL-XXXX]`.
- **Artifacts that processes depend on do not exist.** The Weekly PPE Inspection Checklist form is "to be created" (122). The Closing Walkthrough Checklist that gates key handover is "[TBD — in development]" (003/004). The Repo Tracker, Data Pack Template, Tenancy Lease Acknowledgment, Move-In Inspection Form, 100-point punch list, intake forms, Posting Certification, lease delivery-clause matrix and Prospectus Review Tracker are all named and none is in the library. SOP 101's training reference is "(to be re-uploaded)."
- **Duplication with no marker of authority.** **48 sets of byte-identical files, 96 files in total** — every numbered process exists twice on disk (drafting room and published wiki), and the 14 state compliance SOPs exist twice again. **Four Master Tracker versions coexist** — `SOP_Master_Tracker.xlsx` (in `99-Archive/rock-processes/`), `Copy of SOP_Master_Tracker (002).xlsx`, `SOP_Master_Tracker_Combined_v2.xlsx` and `SOP_Master_Tracker_v3.xlsx`; only v3 is current.
- **The standards doc's own archiving rule is not being followed.** `00-Wiki Standards & Page Template.docx` requires superseded versions to move to an `_Archive` subfolder inside each process folder — "never deleted, never left beside the current version." **No `_Archive` subfolder exists anywhere in the library**, and superseded drafts sit beside current versions throughout: three 002 Lucid exports, two 005 workflows, two "Weekly Rehab & New Home Inspection Checklist" PDFs (one spelled "editeble"), two rehab handoff Jotform PDFs, `105- Parakeet_Rehab_Workflow_draft V1.docx`, and the pre-numbering `HOME_sales process draft SOP.DOC` / `HOME_Sales process workflow V1.HTM` pair.
- **No training material exists.** All 27 `_Video Links.txt` files are empty templates. `00-Video Recording Schedule.xlsx` assigns one video per owner per week across five tracks from the week of 2026-08-10, and states "at this pace all 22 populated processes have video coverage by the week of September 28, 2026." **Zero videos have been recorded**, including for 200 — the approved, zero-rework-risk process scheduled first.
