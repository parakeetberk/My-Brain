# 104 — TBD unit review & allocate

**What this is:** deciding the fate of a vacant park-owned home after move-out — rehab, demo or sell as-is — on a weekly TBD Call. The number holds **two unrelated documents** from two different departments.

**Status:** in review (Tier 1 Critical). Read 2026-08-30.

## The facts

- **Owner:** Natalie Magurne, Director of Operations (Ops reporting SOP); the companion data-entry SOP lists Process Owner **[TBD]**. **Executes:** Community Manager, Operations Admin, Regional Team / Regional Manager, Maintenance, Asset Management, Director of Operations. **Trigger:** the resident confirms move-out and the home is returned to Parakeet. **Ends when:** the disposition decision is recorded and the downstream SOP is initiated.
- Five phases: move-out notification → weekly TBD report generation → documentation review → utility verification → TBD Call & disposition.
- Asset Management processes the move-out in Rent Manager; **the home then appears automatically on the TBD list.** If it does not appear, contact Asset Management — nothing can advance until it does.
- Operations Admin pulls the TBD report **weekly** and circulates it with report links **before** the call, not during. Regional Team members must arrive prepared.
- **Documentation gate: interior photos, exterior photos and the Unit Turn Inspection Sheet must be complete and saved, properly labelled, in the Park Owned Asset folder before the home reaches the TBD Call.** Incomplete or mislabelled files "do not count"; an incomplete home is deferred to the following week's call.
- **Utility gate: all four utilities — electric, water, sewer/septic, gas — must have a known status.** Anything needing activation, transfer or a new meter means the Community Manager files a **Utility Request Jotform**, which routes to Maintenance to verify and recheck. A home with any unknown utility status is not ready for the call. (Exception in the FAQ: the Regional Team may still advance a home with an unresolved utility, noting it as a tracked follow-up before rehab starts.)
- **Disposition decisions:** REHAB — External Vendor → Path A Rehab SOP; REHAB — Internal Team → Internal Rehab SOP; DEMO → Demo / Infill SOP; SELL AS-IS → Sales process.
- **The hard gate after the call: Asset Management creates the Rent Manager issue immediately, and no downstream work — vendor assignment, internal rehab, demo — begins until it exists. "No Rent Manager issue = no work starts."** The issue number is communicated to the Regional Manager and Community Manager.
- Internal vs external rehab is decided on the call by the Director of Operations and Regional Manager, on scope, cost, timing and vendor/team capacity.
- The companion data-entry SOP adds the rules that govern the same decision from the Asset Management side: **every asset must be dispositioned within one week of the TBD call — "no TBD purgatory."** 100% certain demo → status **To Be Demoed** (exits the rehab pipeline); **any doubt → Needs Rehab**, which auto-creates the Issue # on the Make Ready Board. Reverting to demo later is expected and acceptable; sitting in TBD is not.
- Further rules from that document: **statuses flip off dates entered in Rent Manager — never hand-set a status**; every artifact carries the rehab Issue #; internal build cost stays blank until the accounting GL pull is configured, with the memo convention (Issue # + lot on every Home Depot / Lowe's transaction) mandatory now; rehab detail never becomes permanent asset fields; if an asset appears on the vendor's list but is not Needs Rehab in Rent Manager, **that is an audit finding, not a tracker row.**
- Vendor deposit on external rehab is **approximately $2,500**, paid at kickoff and logged as Rehab Deposit = Y. If the vendor says the home is not worth saving, the asset flips to To Be Demoed and the deposit applies to the demo cost.
- **Systems used:** Rent Manager (TBD list, move-out processing, Issues / Make Ready Board, asset status, UDFs), Utility Request Jotform, Park Owned Asset folder.
- **Known problems:**
  - **104 holds two unrelated documents.** `104-TBD_Reporting_SOP.docx` (Aug 26, Operations, Natalie) is a reporting-cadence and disposition-call SOP. `TBD rehab and data entry Draft.docx` (July 23, **Asset Management**, "Used-Home Rehab — Data Entry") is a data-architecture standard about where rehab fields live. They overlap at the TBD Call and diverge everywhere else. Only the data-entry draft is published in the wiki folder; **the newer Operations SOP exists only in the drafting room**, so the wiki's 104 page shows the wrong document.
  - **The reporting SOP contradicts itself on how many dispositions exist:** the matrix note says "every home receives one of these **three** dispositions" above a table of **four**, and Phase 5 then says "one of the **four** decisions in the Disposition Decision Matrix above."
  - **Three owners for one form.** The Utility Request Jotform is filed by the **Community Manager** and routes to **Maintenance** here; SOP 303a says **Construction** submits it and **Accounting** executes; SOP 303b says **Operations** submits it. Same form, three owners.
  - Process Owner on the data-entry SOP is literally **[TBD]**, and it names "Asset Management (Alex S)" for issue-level data entry alongside "Asset Management (Justin)" for status — two different Asset Management actors, one of them identified only by a first name and initial.
  - The data-entry SOP assigns title updates to "**Melanie**" in the task steps and to "**Operations Coordinator**" in the role table, for the same work.
  - Internal rehab cost tracking is **blocked on an unconfigured accounting GL pull** — the SOP instructs staff to leave build cost blank indefinitely and rely on a memo convention.
  - The utility gate has a stated rule ("not ready for the TBD Call") and an FAQ that overrides it ("may still advance the home to the TBD Call").
  - The rehab data-entry PDF in the wiki 105 folder is named **v1.1** but its content says "v1.0 DRAFT" — filename and document disagree.
  - `_Video Links.txt` is an empty template; two 104 videos were scheduled for the week of Sep 21, 2026.
