⚠ Key Deadlines Demand notices sent & posted: 6th · Proof of posting to Regional Mgr: EOD 6th · Regional Mgr compiles & sends: 7th · Intake packets generated: 19th–23rd · CDM completes intake: 25th · Intake to attorney: last week of month · Title receipt: ~30 days (5–7 months if declaratory judgment)
◆ Key Decisions (1) Demand notice accurate? → Yes: forward / No: redraft, extend cure date by 10 business days · (2) Proof of posting received? → No: escalate to DOO, written warning · (3) Intake complete? → No: return for corrections by end of month · (4) Resident pays? → Path A (stop eviction) / No → Path B (proceed) · (5) Lien on home? → No: Florida Fast Title / Yes: Vector Law · (6) Auction sold? → No: Parakeet buys for $1
→ Handoffs This process triggers: Post Eviction Title Transfer SOP (once title is received and resident is removed from Rent Manager) · References: Collection Process 2024.docx (Phase 1, 1st–5th) · Marketing a Home SOP follows after Post Eviction Title Transfer completion
