How this wiki works. Every process gets its own folder holding three things: the written SOP (the step-by-step guide), the swim-lane flowchart (who does what, in order), and _Video Links.txt (paste Stream/Loom walkthrough links there — that's the video shelf until embedded pages come online). Numbering follows the company standard: 000s Sales & Marketing, 100s Operations, 200s Construction, 300s Accounting, 400s Asset Management, 500s Acquisitions, 600s Systems, 700s HR, 800s Legal & Compliance. Status lives on the document itself and on the SOP Master Tracker. Full rules: Wiki Standards & Page Template.
