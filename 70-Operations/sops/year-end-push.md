# Year-end push — the SOP program

**What this is:** the single plan for getting the process library from 1 approved to done by year-end. Twenty-one of the twenty-six findings in [[00-Meta/open-items]] were SOP findings; this page absorbs them into one sequenced program with one owner, so they stop reading as twenty-one separate fires.

**Status:** current — 2026-08-31

## The facts

### Why this is one thing and not twenty-one

- **It is Natalie Magurne's rock.** Q1 was "list all department processes across sales, operations and construction" — done. Q2 was "flow chart and document all listed processes." Q3 is "complete process documentation (SOPs) and implementation across all departments." Austin is working it with her.
- The findings are not independent. Most of them are downstream of **four decisions nobody has made** and **one governance rule nobody is following**. Fix those and roughly half the register closes without anyone writing a new document.
- **The endgame is not documents.** Austin's operating thesis is that the process lives inside the tool people use, not in a separate document nobody reads. So an approved SOP is the input to a form, not the finish line. The BPM decision gates how much of this library ever becomes a FastField or QuickBase form rather than a .docx — see [[10-Business/tools-and-systems]].

### Where it actually stands

- 109 processes tracked across nine documented bands. **1 approved** (SOP 200 Demo & Lot Prep) — and even that one carries no approver name and no approval date.
- 22 processes have real documents behind them. 36 are drafted pending review. 59 workflows and 43 SOP documents are not started. 71 workflows are still needed.
- Four of nine bands are empty: 400 Asset Management, 500 Acquisitions & Dispositions, 600 Systems/IT/Reporting, 700 HR & Training (one orphan).
- 14 state compliance SOPs are written and **all 14 sit at "counsel review" with none signed off.**
- 27 training video slots, **zero recorded**, against a schedule that completes the week of 2026-09-28.
- 48 sets of byte-identical files. Four coexisting tracker versions. No `_Archive` folder anywhere, against the library's own standard.
- Full detail: [[70-Operations/sops/sop-library]].

### Phase 2 — September to October: counsel

- **Sign off the 14 state compliance SOPs** that are already written and waiting. This is the single largest block of finished work sitting behind one gate.
- **Massachusetts and North Carolina.** Both live operating states, no compliance SOPs. MA is the most tenant-protective law in the portfolio — a 72-hour pre-signing disclosure package and a bona fide five-year lease offer, both already documented inside SOPs 100 and 101 with nothing reviewed behind them.
- **RV, Chapter 513.** No process exists anywhere. SOPs 801, 802 and 804 exclude RV and route it to Legal, and Legal has nothing to route to. Geiger Key, Great Oak and River Oaks operate with no documented path on increases, notices, violations, evictions or deposits.
- **Responsible Contractor.** A Jadian requirement with no policy on file, no owner and no process. If its thresholds resemble the standard institutional ones, it lands on competitive bidding and contractor certification across most of the 2026 capital plan — and neither SOP 200 nor SOP 300 carries either requirement today. Get the actual policy from Jadian before writing anything.
- **The prospectus gate.** SOP 804 makes delivery with a signed receipt the gate on every Florida lot tenancy; SOP 103 has no prospectus step. Seven communities have no prospectus on file and must stop and route to Legal on any increase: Geiger Key RV, Great Oak RV, Hunter Springs, Nalls Court, Paradise Lakes, River Oaks RV, Silver Breeze.
- Counsel is Woods Oviatt Gilman and Lowenstein today — see [[10-Business/outside-help/counsel]]. Which firm takes the multi-state landlord-tenant work is an open question for the kickoff.

### Phase 3 — October: approve what is already written

- **Clear the [TBD] markers that block their own documents.** SOP 201 states outright it cannot be approved until three are resolved. SOP 203's manufacturer warranty reporting window is the number that decides whether a claim is late. SOP 302 carries at least five. SOP 108's HR email is [TBD] in a process built on same-day HR notification. 105b's GL codes are all placeholders.
- **Fix the status fields.** Two documents declare themselves Approved while every system of record calls them drafts. The one real approval has no approver and no date. Add an approver, a date and a review date to every approved document.
- **Publish the ten invisible processes.** 104's current Ops SOP, 105b, 108, 110, 122, 123, the 200 rewrite, 202, 203 and 704 exist only in the drafting room. Anyone reading the published wiki cannot see them.
- **Build the missing artifacts.** The Repo Tracker, the Closing Walkthrough Checklist that gates key handover, the Weekly PPE Inspection Checklist, the Data Pack Template, the Tenancy Lease Acknowledgment, the Move-In Inspection Form, the 100-point punch list, the Posting Certification, the lease delivery-clause matrix and the Prospectus Review Tracker are all named in live SOPs and none exists.
- **Resolve the in-document contradictions.** Rejected-offer window (3 / 2 / 2 / blank), e-sign versus wet-sign by state, month-end on the 25th or the 27th, the utility form's three different owners, and the title role called six different things.

### Phase 4 — governance, running alongside from September

This is the "recenter" half, and it is cheap compared to the writing.

- **One authoritative location.** Every numbered process exists twice on disk with no marker of which governs. Pick the published wiki as authoritative, and make the drafting room explicitly non-authoritative.
- **Enforce the archive rule the library already has.** Superseded versions move to an `_Archive` subfolder inside each process folder. No such folder exists anywhere today.
- **One tracker.** v3 is current; three older versions are live in the same tree. Retire the other three and rebuild v3 against what is actually on disk — it is stale on seven processes and blind to five more.
- **One ID scheme.** The CONST-nnn scheme running through the 200s and SOP-LOTS-001 in the lot mapping SOP do not align with the nine-band numbering, and 203 cross-references two CONST numbers that do not exist.

### Phase 5 — November to December: the empty bands, then adoption

- **400 Asset Management** and **600 Systems, IT and Reporting** first — Zach Schwartz. The 400 band must include a **compliance calendar carrying the Jadian obligations**: half-yearly ESG reporting and the Responsible Contractor requirement, neither of which has a date today. See [[30-Customers/capital-partners/jadian-requirements]]. Asset Management is named the central hub of all five cross-departmental workflows and has no documented process of its own, which is the sharpest gap in the library. The 600 band is where the form ecosystem, Rent Manager issue ownership and the CRM finally get written down.
- **700 HR & Training** — Demetria Loukas. Manager onboarding, team onboarding and training, hiring responsibilities.
- **500 Acquisitions & Dispositions** last. Zach's DD flowchart exists as raw material.
- **Training videos.** 27 slots, zero recorded, 3 to 8 minutes each, one task per video. Start with SOP 200 — approved, stable, zero rework risk.
- **Implementation.** The Q3 rock says implementation, not documentation. The lease-signing initiative's 95% completion target is the model for what "implemented" means: a number, measured, per property.

### What success looks like at year-end

State it as a number or it will not be measurable. Proposed at the kickoff, not yet agreed:

- The four Phase 1 decisions made and recorded, with dates.
- All 14 state compliance SOPs signed off by counsel, plus MA, NC and an RV process written.
- Every one of the 22 documented processes approved, with an approver name, an approval date and a review date.
- Zero [TBD] markers in any approved document.
- 400 and 600 bands populated; 700 started.
- One authoritative location, one tracker, one ID scheme, `_Archive` in use.
- Videos recorded for the approved set.
