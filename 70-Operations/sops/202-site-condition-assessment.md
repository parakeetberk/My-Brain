# 202 — Site condition assessment

**What this is:** the pre-permit lot audit — eight sections walked and signed by the Facility Manager before any home is selected or any permit is pulled, valid for 90 days.

**Status:** draft. **Version 3.0**, SOP ID CONST-003, effective August 2026 — versions 1.0 and 2.0 are not on disk. Read 2026-08-30.

## The facts

- **Owner:** Director of Construction. **Executes:** Facility Manager (conducts, completes, signs, submits), Director of Construction (schedules, reviews, decides), Regional Manager (receives the checklist, notified of failures), vendor/contractor (remediation). **Trigger:** the Director of Construction identifies a lot for potential new home placement. **Ends when:** the assessment is submitted, reviewed, and the Director of Construction determines next steps.
- Four phases: assessment setup → on-site assessment → documentation & submission → director review & decision.
- **The pre-audit gate: if a vacant or existing home must be demolished before placement — STOP. Demo must be completed, debris removed and the lot cleared before this audit may be conducted. Do not schedule delivery until demo is confirmed complete.** A Demo Documentation section is still completed in full (existing home description, year/make/model, estimated demo scope, hazardous materials concerns, utilities to disconnect, contractor, estimated completion date, demo authorised by, photos taken).
- Eight checklist sections, each item marked Yes / No / N/A with **a written note required on every No**: **A** existing structures & demo (including underground hazards — old foundations, buried tanks, cisterns, septic remnants); **B** lot grading & drainage (standing water, flood-prone low spots, settlement, sinkholes, soft spots, erosion); **C** setbacks & site dimensions (usable footprint against target home size, front and side/rear setbacks measured and recorded, no encroachments including overhead lines); **D** electrical service (present/absent/condition/location, **meter number recorded**, capacity adequate, overhead clearance); **E** water service (municipal or well, meter number, route feasible); **F** sewer/septic (location and condition, capacity, no backup, odours, wet spots or septic failure); **G** gas/propane (type, location, condition, meter number — N/A if all-electric); **H** environmental & safety (**FEMA flood zone confirmed and flood insurance requirement noted**, no hazardous materials or soil contamination, no protected wetlands, conservation easements or deed restrictions).
- **Photograph and date everything** — utility connections, grading, setback measurements, concerns — and upload with the completed checklist to SharePoint. Specific observations and measurements go in the Notes column for every item, especially utilities and setbacks.
- **Sign and submit to the Director of Construction within 24 hours.**
- Director's three outcomes: **proceed to permitting** (no blocking issues); **remediate first** (issue a written scope to the responsible vendor with a timeline; Facility Manager confirms completion on site and updates the notes); **do not proceed** (site unsuitable).
- **The assessment is valid for 90 days from the Facility Manager's signature.** Permits not pulled within 90 days → re-walk the lot before proceeding.
- **An assessment is required even on a replacement lot** — existing utility conditions and setbacks must be re-confirmed against current requirements every time a home is removed and a new one planned.
- **Utility status unknown is a failure, not an assumption:** mark it No with "status unknown — requires utility locate or inspection." Never assume a line is active or inactive.
- On sign-off the signed assessment is filed in SharePoint and distributed to the Director of Sales (home selection timeline), Director of Operations (resource planning) and Asset Management (lot record).
- Reviewed annually. Related standard: HUD Installation Standards (CFR 3285).
- **Systems used:** SharePoint (checklist and dated photo log); no Rent Manager integration is specified.
- **Known problems:**
  - **Version 3.0 with no version 1 or 2 on disk.** The change log describes edits to sections that no reader can compare against: "Consolidated B section to 2 items (removed natural grade and delivery access). Removed E3 (pressure/flow)… Status language changed from Pass/Fail to Yes/No. Signature block updated: Director of Construction replaces Regional Manager."
  - **The Pass/Fail-to-Yes/No rename was left half-done.** The checklist marks Yes/No/N/A, but Section 5's instruction ("All **Fail** items must be reviewed by the Director of Construction before permits are pulled"), Phase 4's decision ("REMEDIATE FIRST = address **Fail** items"), the Regional Manager's role ("Notified immediately of any **Fail** items") and two FAQs all still say Fail. Field staff are told to escalate a status the form no longer produces.
  - **A leftover role and a leftover artifact.** The roles table includes "Vendor / Contractor — Completes all deficiency remediation identified on the **punch list** before re-inspection." There is no punch list in this SOP.
  - **The Regional Manager is in the roles table and in no step.** They are said to receive the completed checklist, be notified immediately of failures, acknowledge receipt and file it in SharePoint — but Phase 3 sends the assessment only to the Director of Construction, and the Phase 4 distribution list is Sales, Operations and Asset Management. The Regional Manager never receives it.
  - **Whether a site plan is required contradicts itself.** Phase 1 Step 2: obtain survey, zoning map, prior utility records and site plan — "**Not required** — document what is available; gaps will be noted." Related Resources: "Approved Site Plan — Issued by local authority. **Required before audit begins.**"
  - Reviewed annually "by the **Construction Manager** and Director of Operations" — a role that appears nowhere else in the document or the library.
  - **SOP ID CONST-003 does not match the number 202**, and SOP 203 cross-references this document as "**CONST-202**," which does not exist.
  - **Not published in the wiki.** No `202` folder exists — the SOP sits only in the drafting room, so there is no flowchart, no `_Video Links.txt`, and it is absent from the wiki index and the recording schedule.
  - **The tracker has no row for it.** No "Site Condition Assessment" or "Site Approval" row exists in v3, so a Version 3.0 document has never been on the scoreboard.
  - Nothing links this assessment to SOP 001 Step 2, which requires Operations and Construction to confirm lot readiness and enter due diligence in Rent Manager UDF fields before home selection — the same work, described twice, in different systems.
