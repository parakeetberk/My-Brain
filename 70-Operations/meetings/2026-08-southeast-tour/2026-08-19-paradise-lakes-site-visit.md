## Summary
An evening walk of the excavation and utility work at Paradise Lakes in Deltona, and the clearest decision of the day: no more digging until the locating and mapping is done. The thirty thousand dollar locating and mapping item that John raised has to be finished and verified first, because nobody knows where the older water, power and sewer lines run and the crews cannot keep digging blind. The project is three weeks in, one area was effectively a test run, and it has already produced damage — a machine tipped over, rubber boot connectors were pulled loose when pipe sections came out, and the crew has not consistently reported what they broke. The plan is to lay the mains first and then branch into blocks and lots, hand digging the tight areas between homes. Access control is the other theme: the gate is damaged including lightning damage, people are using the broken gate as a shortcut to the dumpster and exiting through it at night into obstacles they cannot see, and the group wants a more sophisticated system with individual codes and entry-exit tracking in next year budget. The new pole near the road cannot move back until Spectrum removes its pole and wires, with no timeline from Spectrum, so temporary barriers go in first.

## Decisions
- Stop all further excavation until the utility locating and mapping is complete and verified — the roughly thirty thousand dollar item comes first.
- Put the upgraded gate system — individual codes, easy code changes, entry and exit tracking — into next year budget; the current system is too old to keep patching.
- Install temporary warning barriers at the roadside pole and at the broken-gate exit route, using plastic corner markers, four-by-fours with rope or metal posts.
- Switch from caution tape to orange construction fencing where the work needs to be marked.
- Lay the main sections first, then branch into individual blocks and lots, hand digging where equipment will not fit between homes.
- Dig out the damaged sewer sections, install new pipe and reconnect the plumbing where the rubber boots were pulled loose.
- Do not give gate codes to the person being removed from the community.
