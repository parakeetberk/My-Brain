# Visit notes for the field, August 2026 roadtrip

**What these are:** one branded one-pager per community, holding only that community's items from the trip. Built 2026-09-02 from [[70-Operations/meetings/2026-08-southeast-tour/2026-09-02-jon-field-report]].

**Why one per community and not one report.** Austin's call on 2026-09-02. "Our notes from the visit with them" means the notes from *their* visit. A regional forwards a single file to an on-site manager and nothing in it is about a park that manager has never seen. The 32-community report stays the internal record; these are what goes out.

**Two formats, on purpose.** The `.docx` is for the regional - they can edit, add status, forward. The `.pdf` is for the on-site manager, because it opens the same on every phone.

## What is in each one-pager

Header with the community, its location, unit count, portfolio and regional manager. A one-line count: items logged, how many need attention now, how many are waiting on a decision, how many are for next year's budget, how many are working well. Then every item grouped by the report's own seven categories, each carrying its flags and its `PF-` reference number, with Jon's field photo underneath the item it was taken for. A closing ask.

**The reference numbers are the point of the whole thing.** Every item traces to a row in [[60-Communities/open-items]]. When a manager replies *"PF-0386 is done"*, it closes against the register instead of into an inbox.

## What was deliberately left out

**Eight items raised on these visits are not in these notes.** Seven are personnel matters - hiring attempts, a candidate assessment, a departing maintenance team's recommendations, an account of former staff conduct, a vendor crew characterization - and one is an unproven allegation against a former on-site manager. Guardrails 5 and 7 in [[CLAUDE]] cover both. They stay with Austin. The affected one-pagers say a count was withheld and why, rather than reading as complete when they are not.

Also excluded upstream by the report itself: compensation discussions and individual resident hardship.

## Sending them out

Three cover emails are drafted in `_cover-emails.md`, one per regional. **Nothing was sent.** Outlook is read only in these sessions - `Mail.Send` and `Mail.ReadWrite` are not admin-consented - so the drafts sit here for Austin to send.
