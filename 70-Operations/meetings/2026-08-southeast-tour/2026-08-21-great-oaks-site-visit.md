## Summary
A short walk of Great Oak RV Resort focused on the clubhouse and the common areas. Interior finishes: grey oak vinyl tile for the clubhouse floor, the drop ceiling, a lamp post to replace, and the bulletin board outside the office to replace — with the purple and the pool area colours singled out as the worst of what is there. The bulletin board is to move to the mailbox cluster, mounted like a wall on the side of the units, provided mail carrier access stays clear and the community room entrance is not blocked. Outdoors, the grill and hibachi bar gets used in season and bocce is played, but one participant considers the current grill setup a fire hazard that should be removed; the plan is to redo the bocce area, relocate the grill beside it, keep it on propane unless a gas line is available, and possibly put a metal roof over the whole area. Two constraints came up: power cannot be brought to where it is needed without rerouting and upgrades, and the site is on a boil water notice with the aerator pump out. Smaller observations: the storage area is wide open, and a solar light turns on but never off because the sun does not reach it, so residents ignore it.

## Decisions
- Move the mailbox bulletin board to the mailbox cluster area and replace it, keeping mail carrier access and the community room entrance clear.
- Replace the lamp post.
- Redo the bocce area and relocate the grill next to it; keep it propane unless a gas line is available, in which case bring in a plumber. Consider a metal roof over the area.
- Use grey oak vinyl tile for the clubhouse flooring.
- Orient porches on the main street to face out, porch to porch rather than porch to rear, and flip the banks as units get replaced.
