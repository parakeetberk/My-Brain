## Summary
A short exchange recorded between stops on the morning of 2026-08-19, thinking out loud about why landscaping enforcement keeps failing. The pattern described is bushes that grow into massive things, landlords who do not manage their lots, and homeowners who are simply too old to do the work. The observed lever is blunt and effective: telling an owner "you either need to trim it or we are going to take it out" gets a response, because once removal is on the table people come looking for you. From there the speaker moves to the structural fix — fold trimming and landscaping into rent rather than relying on individual compliance, which with this many landlords in the community would change the look of the place immediately, and would help with rule enforcement generally. Nothing was costed and no vendor or start date was discussed.

## Decisions
- Direction set: centralize landscaping instead of relying on individual homeowner and landlord compliance — bundle mowing and trimming into rent on the rental side, and put a trim-it-or-we-remove-it ultimatum to homeowners. Directional only; no cost, vendor or start date agreed.
