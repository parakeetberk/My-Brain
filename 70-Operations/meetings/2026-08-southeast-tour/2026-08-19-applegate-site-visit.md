## Summary
A drive-through of Applegate in DeLand that turned into a list of homes sitting undone. 227 was delivered on July 23, another home on July 22, 248 is confirmed as a demo and still not set after going to TBD in the last round, and 267 is also waiting; the double that went to public auction on August 14 came up in the same breath. One home has no wood under it and is about to fall over. Champion was absent for the second time, directly blocking at least two more homes, and the speakers called it a big problem rather than a scheduling slip. On placement, the difficult back lot was rejected: access will not allow the move to the left, it is way out of the way for everybody, effectively behind another home space, and nobody would walk it — the extra lot next to a previously demoed home is the better option. Two smaller items: a wellhead with no white PVC sleeve, which they suspect is a regulation rather than an oversight, and the practice of handing 10 dollar gift cards to residents who paint or keep up their homes, on the logic that the recipient tells everybody else.

## Decisions
- Do not use the difficult back lot for the next set — it is impractical and out of the way; use the alternative extra lot instead, even though it sits next to a previously demoed home.
- Start handing out small gift cards, around 10 dollars, to residents who visibly maintain their homes, as positive reinforcement rather than penalties.
- Treat Champion repeat absences as an escalation, not a scheduling issue — it is blocking at least two homes.
