## Summary
A walk of Lucky Clover in Melbourne after the road work, and the property shows it: new roads, painted speed bumps, and fresh paving that makes the roads look wider than they are. A side effect noted is that the disruptive residents moved out during the improvement phase. The improvement list centres on the underutilized shuffleboard court — resurface it or convert it to pickleball — and on the sun shades over it, which sag, hold water and degrade fast in the Florida climate, so a permanent metal roof is the better answer. New common-area lighting will run off an external line to the photocell on the dumpster area light so it cycles with the sun automatically, the rotting perimeter fence gets replaced, the flagpole gets a larger American flag with a parakeet flag under it, and glass-enclosed bulletin boards go behind every mailbox cluster so notices are actually seen. The conversation then widened into the infill playbook: incentivize residents to trade older RVs and homes for new manufactured homes to push parks from around 50 percent RV toward 100 percent homes, with Champion setting and Cavco trimming out and sealing. The set timeline target is 45 to 60 days and the team is currently averaging 37, with soft sets of about 15 days permitted for park models in places like River Oaks. On systems, business process automation moves onto dedicated Power Automate server accounts instead of individual drive access.

## Decisions
- Either resurface the shuffleboard court or convert it to pickleball, and replace the sagging sun shades with a permanent metal roof.
- Run an external power line to the photocell on the dumpster area light so the new common-area lighting cycles automatically with the sun.
- Replace the rotting perimeter fence in the common area.
- Upgrade the flagpole with a larger American flag and a parakeet flag underneath.
- Install glass-enclosed, weatherproof bulletin boards behind every mailbox cluster.
- Centralize business process automation on dedicated Power Automate server accounts and stop running it off individual drive access.
- Push the parks from roughly 50 percent RV occupancy toward 100 percent homes by incentivizing trade-ins of older RVs and mobile homes for new units.
- Send residents a notice with a cover letter setting out the engineering and septic work status.
- Deploy the internal AI agent against the existing org chart so the automated tools inherit the same permission levels as the employees they assist.
