# 08-19 Strategy to Centralize Landscaping for Consistent Property Maintenance

## Core Synopsis

The leadership team is diagnosing a systemic issue with property maintenance and rule enforcement, particularly with overgrown landscaping. The core insight is that inconsistent enforcement and a reliance on individual owner/tenant compliance creates aesthetic liabilities and operational drag. The proposed solution is to centralize landscaping services, absorb the cost into rent for tenants, and present a clear "trim it or we remove it" ultimatum to homeowners, effectively forcing compliance through a service-based model. We are taking this bet that the efficiency gains and improved property values will outweigh the initial administrative effort of consolidating the service.

## The Problem and Proposed Solution

### The Gap: Inconsistent Maintenance and Enforcement

- **Status Quo:** Property maintenance, specifically landscaping like trimming bushes and trees, is the responsibility of individual homeowners or tenants. This decentralized approach leads to inconsistent results.
- **The Pain:** Many properties suffer from overgrown landscaping, which degrades the overall aesthetic and value of the community. This is caused by two primary factors:
  1. **Landlord Negligence:** Landlords are not actively managing their properties' landscaping.
  2. **Owner Incapacity:** Some homeowners, particularly older residents, are physically unable to perform the necessary yard work.
- **The Critical Need:** A standardized, reliable system is required to ensure all properties meet a minimum maintenance level, thereby protecting the community's appearance and enforcing rules without relying on inconsistent individual action.

### The Solution: Centralize Landscaping Services

- **Mechanism:** The proposal is to integrate landscaping services (mowing, trimming) into the community's operational model.
  - **For Rental Properties:** The cost would be bundled directly into the monthly rent. Given the high number of landlords, this would have a significant, immediate impact on property appearance.
  - **For Homeowners:** A direct ultimatum would be issued: "You either trim it, or we will remove it." This forces a decision and leverages the threat of removal to encourage proactive maintenance or compliance.
- **The Advantage:** This shift moves the association from a reactive, penalty-based enforcement model to a proactive, service-based one. It solves the problem directly, improves community aesthetics, and provides a clear benefit to residents who are unable to do the work themselves.
