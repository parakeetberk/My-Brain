## Summary
A walk of River Oaks RV Resort in Ruskin, mostly clubhouse, pier and amenities. The clubhouse needs the restroom upgraded, a microwave, new windows and probably some walls redone, and although the roof was redone after the hurricane the building is still leaning and still leaking; an inspection report is to be reviewed. Power to the pier is unfinished — George started it — and since residents fish constantly, a proper fish-cleaning station was suggested. The bingo machine is broken and expensive new, so the decision was to buy a used one off the marketplace with cash. Residents are genuinely active, decorating for holidays and organizing events, so the plan is a survey to find out what they actually want before committing to bocce, horseshoes, a dog wash, a car wash or Tesla charging; pickleball is out at the suggested spot because of an underground electrical transformer, and outdoor movie nights by the pier came up as an easy win. On the site, digging a new well could reclaim six to eight lots if the horseshoes area moves, which was judged cost-effective. Sales issues: homes are not listed in Rent Manager at all, a 15 percent discount for current RV residents was proposed to get momentum, and someone suggested holding off on more homes until some of the current ones sell. The fountain is dead and the quoted aerator is the better, lower-maintenance replacement.

## Decisions
- Buy a used bingo machine off the marketplace with cash instead of replacing it new.
- Install the quoted aerator and keep it running, rather than repairing the fountain.
- Favour a wooden swing over hammocks for safety, and consider Adirondack chairs.
- Survey residents to prioritize new activities and amenities before committing to any of them.
- Move the well if it reclaims six to eight lots — the trade is worth it, and the horseshoes area moves.
- Address the broader infrastructure — water lines, seawall and roads — on a defined phased timeline rather than piecemeal.
