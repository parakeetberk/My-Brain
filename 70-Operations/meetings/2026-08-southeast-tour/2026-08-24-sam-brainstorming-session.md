## Summary
The sales and marketing planning call two days after the tour ended. It opens with a data fix: Brunswick Estates shows 21 Gressel on the website and in Rent Manager, but that is an actual home inside the community — the correct address is 221 Robarts Road, and the signage is wrong too. Then the main thread. Infill has run ahead of plan — 130 homes delivered and 40 listed and ready — so the constraint has moved to sales, described as the last frontier and, over the next six months, the determinant of the recap. The group wants the funnel mapped touch by touch — new prospect, engaged prospect, tour completed, application completed — with a named owner at each stage, since it is currently unclear whether a manager, AI or a specific person carries each one. Structurally they want to replicate the Fox Creek model: dedicated sales and leasing agents distinct from community managers at high-volume properties like Vantage and potentially across the East Coast, treated as permanent structure rather than a spike hire, sized against turnover of more than 3 percent a year across around 7,000 units. A loan coordinator role is going out immediately to hold buyers hands on financing. They are explicitly willing to spend real money on the human and non-human infrastructure to make it work before the October ramp.

## Decisions
- Correct the Brunswick Estates address to 221 Robarts Road in Rent Manager, on the website and on the signage — 21 Gressel is a resident home inside the community.
- Publish the Loan Coordinator job description immediately.
- Create dedicated sales and leasing agent roles, separate from community managers, at high-volume communities on the Fox Creek model, as permanent long-term structure.
- Split the 2026-08-26 planning call in two: the first hour with the core team to get to a concise position, the second hour with the CRM and listing people.
- Spend real money on the human and system infrastructure for sales ahead of the October demand ramp — sales momentum over the next six months determines the recap.
- Hold funnel responsibility assignments until after the Unity capability review, so the tool capability is understood first.
- Circulate the reporting dashboard for the whole group to sign up to it.
