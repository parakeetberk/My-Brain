## Summary
A quick drive-through of Palm Spring in Tarpon Springs. There is no entrance sign — there is a place for one and nothing in it — and the internal signs are random and a mess. Against that, the property is visibly better: much cleaner behind the office, and a batch of the worst homes has been demoed, including the ones the speakers describe as the cracked ones they got rid of. Permits were believed to be filed, and one home was noted as a tough approval that only just came through. The open question is the remaining older homes. One speaker position is that as long as homes can be replaced the lots are worth keeping, filling with park models over time; the other suggested incentivizing owners at other communities to sell their newer homes, which could then be moved here. Neither was settled, and the recording ends with agreement that the place is tough but looks far better than it did.

## Decisions
- Redo the internal signage — it is random and inconsistent across the community.
- Keep the lots and replace homes over time rather than write the community off, using park models as the replacement stock.
