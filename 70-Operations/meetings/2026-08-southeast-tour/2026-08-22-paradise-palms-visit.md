## Summary
An evening walk of Paradise Palms in Lakeland, a 55+ community, that turned into a construction quality review. Most of the concrete pads and steps in the park are done correctly — described as perfectly done — but a subset of homes has stairs set the wrong way against the door swing, so residents have to walk around the landing to get in. Alongside that: exposed wood where stair skirting was never applied, a missing post cap, and misaligned hardscape transitions near the steps. The pattern is consistent with two crews working to different standards, or a mid-project change in stair orientation that never reached the doors, the skirting or the concrete. In a 55+ community the access and appearance consequences matter more, and unskirted stairs plus an unprotected drain field also carry liability. Other items raised: fencing the new drain field so vehicles cannot drive onto it unnoticed, tree roots torn out during electrical trenching, gate codes that did not work for the houses or the clubhouse, and two small punch list items — paint the office front door and replace the missing cap. The fix is not a materials problem; it is a standard and supervision problem, and the same job is being paid for twice.

## Decisions
- Standardize the spec: door swing dictates stair orientation, stair skirting goes on all visible wood, and concrete step alignment follows the approach path.
- Remediate the outlier homes — either rehang the doors to match the existing stairs or reposition the stairs to match the door swings — then finish with skirting.
- Log every exception into a punch list with lot numbers and photos.
- Fence or barrier the new drain field so vehicles cannot drive onto it.
- Fix the entry codes for the houses and the clubhouse, redistribute working codes to authorized people, and retire the ones that do not work.
