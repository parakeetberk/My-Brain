## Summary
A walk of Riverwalk in Gibsonton, and the install quality is the story. On the new homes: metal treads sitting on wood, doors hung off centre, door swings wrong, stair concrete not wide enough for the home, and some steps that look like they are tipping over. Lock boxes are on some homes but not on the two new ones, and there is an open question about a window that was supposed to be removed. A list of the issues for all lots has been sent to John. On the site itself: RV owners on the three lots that cannot be built on get about a month and a half to move, most RVs are believed to be on trucks-only lots which needs confirming, and the dumpsters should sit at the end of the street rather than on the road — though roll-offs could damage the pavement either way. Cosmetic items are matching fencing and a covering over the mailbox area, which currently lets carriers deliver without leaving the vehicle. General cleanliness needs one good clear-out of old items, and crews should be cleaning up before they leave each day. New street signs, installed the week before by Brandon and Fernando, got praised. The team also wants to get the Hillsborough County home replacement program available at this community.

## Decisions
- Give RV owners on the three lots that cannot be built on about a month and a half to relocate.
- Put the dumpsters at the end of the street rather than on the road, weighing the risk of roll-off damage to the pavement.
- Crews clean up before leaving each day.
- Add matching fencing and improve the mailbox area, possibly with a covering, while keeping the drive-up delivery arrangement.
