## A new standing protocol, agreed on the call

**While a rehab is in progress the crew now checks HVAC, hot water heater, roof and, as far as possible, windows.** Operations asked for it on the basis of the age of the homes and the Belle Haven experience; the vendor accepted it and named the limit honestly - **it cannot be caught during leveling, and a roof leak is often only findable while it is raining.** **This is a change to how a rehab is scoped and it should reach the SOP**, which currently does not say it. `TD-0448`.

## Fox Creek HVAC, and the reason it is a program question rather than a repair

Operations has been watching AC repair invoices come through and wants the whole picture rather than another invoice. **Fox Creek started with 120 rentals, has had a heavy run of AC repairs, and has a batch of replacements coming due.** The ask has two halves: **check the HVAC on every rehab home there**, and **price the occupied rentals too**, because if twenty units are done together the pricing should be better than one at a time. Existing quotes are held from two sources including a local repair contractor. **The units are large package units at two to two and a half tons.**

**The vendor's counter-proposal is the interesting part.** At Silver Breeze he has been installing **mini splits and tankless water heaters** and rates them highly: the mini splits are a ton each so two of them cover a two-ton home, **about $3,500 installed for the pair**, Bluetooth capable, quiet and efficient, and the tankless unit kept up with every fixture in the house running. He was careful to say they are new and unproven in time - *"they might all blow up in three weeks"* - and that he leaves the warranty cards in the home. **A cheaper, better-performing alternative to package units at scale is a capex question, not a maintenance one.** `TD-0449`.
