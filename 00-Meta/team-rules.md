## The working rules

1. **Pull before you edit.** Get the latest version of the vault before changing anything, every time.
2. **Plain-English commit notes.** Every save-and-push says what changed in words a teammate understands. "Updated Fox Creek after the August site visit", not "changes".
3. **CLAUDE.md changes need Austin's sign-off.** No exceptions — it's the constitution.
4. **LOCKED means locked.** Content marked `LOCKED` doesn't get reworded by anyone, human or Claude, without Austin unlocking it first.
5. **Not sure where something goes?** Put it in `05-Inbox/` — never guess it into someone else's folder.
6. **The Brain is shared truth.** Personal notes and half-private drafts stay out of it.
7. **No resident names in anything that leaves this vault** without the process owner signing off. Notices, demand letters and denials are drafted here and served through the SOP, never straight out of a page.
8. **No individual's compensation goes in this vault, ever.** Not in a page, not in a ledger row, not in a transcript summary.
