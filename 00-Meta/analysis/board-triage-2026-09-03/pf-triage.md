## Three ways to work this down, and the one I would pick

Per the standing rule that no issue reaches Austin bare. **The decision is not which items matter, it is what shape of work clears them.**

**Path A. Work the 81 P0s as a single sweep and leave the rest.** Two weeks, the three regionals plus John Clark, one list, closed or scheduled by the end of it. What it costs: the P2 layer keeps aging and the rehab and delivery reconciliations stay broken. What is still exposed when it is done: nothing new prevents the next 81. **Wrong choice if the tour repeats in six months, because it will produce the same register again.**

**Path B. Stand up the six agent jobs first, then work whatever survives.** Four to six weeks before the field sees anything. It fixes the mechanism: an expiry register that cannot go stale, a utility consumption trend, a weekly three-way home reconciliation, and portal list building that removes about 62 rows on its own. What it costs: the P0s wait, and the fence at the wastewater plant does not care about a mechanism. **Wrong choice while teenagers are in the plant.**

**Path C. Rebuild the register from the systems of record rather than from transcripts.** Every row re-derived from Rent Manager, the Cavco workbook, the rehab tracker, the permit files and the GL, with the tour transcripts demoted to evidence. It would settle the duplicate problem permanently and expose the items nobody said out loud on the trip. What it costs: six weeks of somebody's whole attention, and the systems disagree with each other, which is `KP-0040`. **Wrong choice until at least two of the three books agree.**

**Recommended: A and B in parallel, on a two-week split, and not C yet.**

A alone recreates the problem; B alone lets somebody get hurt while a register is being built. Run the 81 P0s as a named sweep with the three regionals owning their own communities, which is the decision `TD-0459` already puts to Austin and which unlocks 115 rows the moment he says yes. **In parallel, build agent job one only**, the permit and licence expiry register, because `PF-0178` proves the folder cannot be trusted and because it is the single job that turns a whole class of P0 into a calendar. The other five agent jobs graft on behind it in priority order, starting with the utility consumption trend, since `PF-0084` and `PF-0460` are the same finding at two properties and one of them is already in front of a lender.

**Hold C, but take one piece of it now**: the fifteen bulk patterns above are the C-shaped insight without the C-shaped cost. Buying one mailbox standard, one fence spec and one equipment fleet decision closes about sixty rows without touching a system of record.
