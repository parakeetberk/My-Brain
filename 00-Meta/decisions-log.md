**Status:** current — 2026-08-30

## How this works

- One line per decision: the date, the decision, who made it, and where it was decided. A `[[wikilink]]` to the filed transcript or page where the detail lives.
- A decision also goes on the page it affects, in that page's own Decisions section. This log is the chronological spine; the pages are the topical memory.
- Never rewrite a line. If a decision is reversed, add a new line saying so and reference the old one.
- Decisions made in a recording get here through [[95-Agents/transcript-to-action]]. Decisions made in conversation get here because Claude writes them down in the same session.
- 108 decisions were extracted from the August 2026 portfolio tour and live on their community and department pages, and in the 27 filed notes in `70-Operations/meetings/2026-08-southeast-tour/`. They are not duplicated here. This log starts from the Structure Interview forward.
