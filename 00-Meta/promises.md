# Promises register

**What this is:** the sixth register. **Every promise made to a named person that has not been closed out.** Not tasks - tasks are [[10-Business/eos/to-dos]]. A promise has a person on the other end of it, and that is the field no other register carries.

**Status:** current - 2026-09-01. Created on Austin's ruling when the road-trip tracker was folded into the board. **36 rows, all from the August 2026 southeast tour.**

## Why this exists as its own register

- **`owner` is who does the work. There was no field anywhere for who it was promised to.** A promise routed into the to-do register loses the person, and the person is the whole point.
- **Turnover is the stated reason [[00-Meta/open-items]] `KP-0022` is urgent.** Thirty-six things the CEO told named employees he would do, sitting unread in a spreadsheet for six days, is a retention artefact and not a task list.
- **A promise closes when the person is told, not when the work is done.** *"We decided not to"* closes a promise honestly. Silence does not.
- Source: `parakeet-road-trip-tracker-2026-08.xlsx`, Promises to Team tab, read in full 2026-09-01. Archived at `99-Archive/2026-08-southeast-tour/`.

See [[00-Meta/budget-week-2026]]. **That is the shape of this register: a third of what was promised to staff costs money nobody has budgeted.**
- **`PR-0014`, backing Angel after the gun incident, closed as handled by the onsite team** - the same disposition as the incident. What backing her looked like is not recorded anywhere.

## The facts

- **Owners are recorded as the tracker states them.** Where it said UNASSIGNED, the row says unassigned. **No owner has been inferred** - 31 of 36 have none, and that is the finding, not a gap in the import.
- **Status is the tracker's own**: OPEN, or VERIFY where something was said to have happened and nobody confirmed it.
- Grouped by property so one property's promises can be read before a visit.
