## The three paths considered

**Freeze and Attest.** Freeze the vault, hash manifest it, issue a signed Statement of Archive Contents and Known Gaps naming every hole with an owner, close the four items carrying legal consequence. Two weeks. **Buys posture and closes nothing.**

**One Door, One Name.** One written promotion standard, one naming grammar, one physical `_INTAKE` door. Prove it on the 68 K-1 finals landing in twelve days, then push the 1,513 file backlog back through the same door rather than editing it in place. Gate live in five days, 1.8 clean in three weeks.

**Order the Originals.** Re request every document class from whoever holds the original: the e signature platforms, Citrin, the banks, the QI, title, counsel. Ten to sixteen weeks, schedule owned by third parties.

## Recommended: One Door, One Name

**It is the only path that produces the thing a partner with a grievance actually asks for, and the only one that leaves a system behind when Austin stops looking at it.**

**Murad's request is sectioned by party and entity. Khodara's grievance is about whose name a distribution was paid under for six years. Both are per party questions.** One Door's promotion register carries one row per file with the party resolved through a canonical party register plus the identity evidence that fixed it, which **is a per investor completeness matrix as a byproduct.**

**Why Freeze and Attest loses.** Its own residual risk concedes the fund still cannot produce that matrix. **That is Austin signing a document, in front of the person most motivated to exploit it, conceding he cannot answer the central question, while the answer is three weeks and one worksheet away.** Honest disclosure of an irreducible gap is a shield; a disclosed decision not to do cheap work is not. Its stated failure trigger is already partly live: the demand was served through counsel 2026-05-10 with a subpoena mentioned in writing.

**Why Order the Originals loses.** Right instinct, wrong source. **The originals for the class that matters most are not at DocuSign, they are at `ns:5655481408` inside Dropbox, intact and correctly named.** It spends ten to sixteen weeks and a platform retention gamble to obtain what a byte join plus 179 file opens produces in three, and its first output is a schedule of letters to other people while there are twelve days to a filing deadline.

**Three specifics that decided it.** One Door has the correct theory of the defect and shuts off the mechanism, since the damage was introduced at bulk copy time. It is the only path that addresses the live ACH and W-9 exposure by moving them to a restricted section rather than only revoking links. And it is timed to the inflow, so the 68 finals arrive compliant.

**The honest risk, stated rather than buried: renaming roughly 1,500 files while under a books and records demand can read as a party editing its own records.** That is why the hash manifest is action one and is not optional.
