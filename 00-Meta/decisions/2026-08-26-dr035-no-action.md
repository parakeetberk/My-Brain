**Date**: 2026-08-26

## The decision

**No action on DR-035.** No errata, no corrected reissue, no disclosure to Jadian Capital.

## What DR-035 is

Divergence 35 of 91 in `RCM_Divergence_Register` (721 lines, SharePoint Birdcage → `Data  Analytics/Claude Cowork/Corporate`).

A hard-costs subtotal in the shipped **Riverwalk** and **McGregor** replacement-cost FINALs is labeled as summing "sitework through amenities" when the formula actually sums through personal property. Both reports were delivered to Jadian Capital. Branch rules forbid editing a shipped FINAL.

Root cause is clone lineage — McGregor was seeded from `Riverwalk FINAL_v1`, propagating the label.

This claim was in the briefing at the time of the decision but does not change it.

Austin was briefed on the above and elected no action on DR-035 specifically.

**The other 52 unresolved divergences are not covered by this decision** and remain open. They sit under the Jon Wyss decision queue — see `00-Meta/commitments.md` item 3.
