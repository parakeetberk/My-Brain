# Daily close

**What this is:** one file per weekday, written by `90-Skills/end-of-day.md`. It says what changed that day, what got filed, what needs a yes or no from Austin, and the three things for tomorrow.

**Austin writes nothing to produce one.** The sweep runs at 6pm on weekdays whether or not he shows up, and it can be fired manually any time.

**The Confirm or correct block is the reason this folder exists.** Rule 11 says the absence of a written reply is not evidence that something is open. Items get settled on calls and in cars, and the register never hears about it. Each night the close picks at most three items most likely to have been handled offline and asks. A one word answer is a complete answer.

**How answers get back in:** any way at all. Typed under the question, said in chat the next morning, or spoken. Claude acts on them and routes the result to whichever register owns it.

**A question that goes unanswered three closes running** stops being asked and moves to `00-Meta/parked-decisions.md` as something waiting on his ruling.

**Retirement:** the weekly brain review sweeps closes older than a month into `99-Archive/daily-close/`. Nothing is deleted, per rule 2.

**Schedule note:** the task runs at 22:00 UTC, which is 6pm Eastern during daylight time and 5pm Eastern once EST starts in November. Shift it to `0 23 * * 1-5` then if 6pm matters.
