- **The Southeast portfolio sale closed 2025-12-30** — the most significant milestone in the wind-down. It covered **10 of the 13 portfolio investments**.
- **It was structured as two related transactions:** the sale of the underlying real estate, and a later payoff of the seller note, because the buyer financed part of the purchase price via a note held by the fund.
