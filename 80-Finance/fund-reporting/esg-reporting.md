## Why this matters

The obligation now has a requirer and an owner, which it did not this morning. What it still lacks is a date. A half-yearly commitment to an equity partner, with no calendar entry and two periods unaccounted for, is exactly the thing that gets discovered late by the partner rather than early by us.
