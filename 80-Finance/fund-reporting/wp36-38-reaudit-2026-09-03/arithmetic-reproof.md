## Method note

All sums were recomputed from cell values, never read off a total row. Excel serial dates were converted with the 1900 system (serial 43101 = 2018-01-01). The `.xls` distribution files were read with xlrd 2.0.2; the `.xlsx` workbooks were unzipped and the worksheet XML parsed directly against `xl/sharedStrings.xml`. Note for anyone summing the distribution files by filename: **`2019.xls` contains the sheet "2020 Distribution Report" and `2020.xls` contains "2019 Distribution Report".** The contents are correct; the filenames are swapped. All figures in this paper were taken from the sheet name, not the file name.
