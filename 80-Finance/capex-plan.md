# CapEx plan 2026

**What this is:** the 2026 capital plan as it actually sits in the source workbook — totals, portfolio and category splits, the biggest projects, the monthly run-rate, and the four things wrong with the plan.

**Status:** current — 2026-08-30. Source workbook `CapEx Overview - REDO OF CAPEX PLAN.xlsx`, `Data` sheet, 245 line items.

## The facts

### Totals

- **245 line items.** Original cost column totals **$11,588,600**; the revised cost column totals **$12,416,500**.
- **Excluding the 53 rows marked "Deleted": 192 items, $10,300,100 original and $10,984,000 revised.**
- **Line items by status: 169 Existing, 53 Deleted, 18 Complete, 5 New.** The five New rows carry a revised cost but no original cost and, in three cases, no dates.
- **By portfolio, on the original cost column:** JREFI **$6,747,600** across 21 properties; JREFII **$4,072,000** across 19; Legacy **$769,000** across 13. On the revised column: JREFI $7,380,000, JREFII $4,108,500, Legacy $928,000.
- **By responsibility:** 158 items to **Construction** ($10,411,500 revised), 17 to **Operations** ($216,500 revised), and **70 items with a blank Responsibility field** ($1,788,500 revised) — no owner at all.

### Where the money goes

- **Fifteen project categories.** The three utility categories total **$5,978,500 on the original cost column — 52% of the whole plan**: Sewer $3,030,500 (16 items), Electrical $2,660,000 (5 items), Water $288,000 (7 items).
- Next largest categories: Paving & Roads $1,140,100 (18 items), Amenities $1,108,000 (58 items — the most line items of any category), Miscellaneous $781,000, Landscaping $675,000, Seawall $518,000 (2 items), Permanent Structures $304,000, POH $260,000, Signage $230,500.
- **Largest by property (original cost):** Paradise Lakes **$2,825,000**, of which **$2,400,000 is a single electrical line** running 2026-01-31 to 2026-12-31; Druid Oaks **$1,360,000**, of which **$1,300,000 is the sewer tie-in and paving**; Silver Breeze **$800,000**; Northgate **$595,000**; Triple S **$518,000**, all of it seawall.
- **Two projects run to 2031-12-31**, both sewer tie-in and septic retirement: **J Bar J at $360,000** and **Carley's at $72,000**, both starting 2026-01-31. J Bar J is the larger of the two.

### The run-rate

- **Monthly spend through 2026 runs $710,976 to $994,810**, peaking in **October 2026 at $994,810**. January 2026 is $816,867 and December 2026 is $835,038.
- **2027 falls to $72,667–$202,667 a month** from January through November, then drops to **$6,000 in December 2027**.
- **From 2028 the tail is $6,000 a month through December 2031**, with two small bumps — $19,333 a month May–July 2028 and $16,000 a month May–July 2029.

### Grants

- **Lucky Clover's sewer tie-in retired all 58 septic tanks and converted the whole community to city sewer, with $1.4M (86%) of the $1.6M project cost covered by a SOIRL grant.** Expected completion was December 2025 against a current target of March 2026.
- **The same Brevard County programme is prepared to fund Silver Breeze** — a $650,000 water system upgrade, public sewer connection and paving, engineering complete, out to vendors for bids, targeted December 2026.
- Tradewinds is pursuing grant funding for a city utility tie-in "similar to what was secured at Lucky Clover."
- The deck's own JREFI community-improvement figure of $5.1M is stated as gross project cost **before roughly $800k of expected grant funding reimbursed in 2026**.

### Completed, per the March 2026 board deck

- **Shady Oaks sewer tie-in and paving** — retired 18 septic tanks, tied all 37 homes into city sewer, completed February 2026, cost in line with the $400k budgeted, against a budgeted timeframe of August 2025.
- **Fox Creek paving and curbs** — completed January 2026, **$350k against a $450k budget**.
- **Riverwalk dock replacement** — completed February 2026, **$72.5k against a $75k budget**.
- **Auburn Ridge duplex remodels (3)** — completed February 2026, **$135k against a $150k budget**; rent moved from $800 to $1,200.
- **Brunswick Estates SFH and POH remodels (3)** — completed February 2026, **$235k against a $300k budget**; a $0-rent unit to $1,500.
- **Silver Hill paving improvements** — completed September 2025, **$145k against a $100k budget** — the one completed project that came in over.
- **The Oaks major tree removal and fencing** — completed October 2025, **$95k against a $235k budget**.

### What is wrong with the plan

- **The two summary pivots disagree by $2,386.36.** `Summary (PROP x PROJ)` totals **$11,588,600**, matching the Data sheet. `Summary (PROP x DATE)`, which spreads each project across its months, totals **$11,586,213.64** — a rounding gap in the monthly-amount column.
- **70 of 245 line items have no owner** in the Responsibility field, covering $1,788,500 of revised cost.
- **Planned versus unplanned CapEx is not tracked.** The executive strategy deck asks it as an open question: "planned vs unplanned capex tracking, how do we prevent/budget for unplanned?"
- **The signage plan and the CapEx signage rows contradict each other.** The workbook's own signage roster (`Sheet1`) covers 65 communities and calls for **51 full new sign packages** — 47 High End, 4 Low End — against 14 marked "Nothing". In the `Data` sheet, **all 27 Signage line items are marked Deleted**. The plan says 51 packages; the budget says none.
- **Related, and unresolved:** CapEx Tracker 2.0 is an open Q1 2026 rock for Zach Schwartz with no completion evidence, and "FINALIZE CAPEX TRACKING — Working on improved tracker 2.0" is still a 2026 year-end goal in the March board deck.

## What the plan does not reach: approvals in the field

Added 2026-08-31. This page is the **plan**. The **execution** side runs through Adobe Sign kickoff and closeout documents and lands in [[60-Communities/projects]]. Eight of those documents were extracted from their signed PDFs on 2026-08-31 and the join between the two sides does not currently hold.

- **Seven of the eight carried no budget allocation at all.** Six read `UNPLANNED, NO BUDGET ALLOCATION` and one reads `INFILL, NO BUDGET ALLOCATION`. The Budget Allocation field exists on the form and was left empty on seven of eight approvals in a single batch.
- **The eighth is the one that shows why that matters.** Lake Saunders 11055 carries a **$30,000.00 budget allocation and a $48,000.00 project estimate** on the same page, sixty percent over, and was approved by every signatory including Austin and Jon. It is also the only one of the eight marked **PLANNED**.
- **The plan is a category plan; the approvals are project documents.** This page's 245 line items are categorised spend. The signed documents carry a project number, a contractor, an estimate and a target date. Nothing links a line item to a document number, so the plan cannot currently be reconciled to what was actually approved.
- **Rent Manager holds a capex budget of its own.** A `BudgetCompare` run on 2026-08-31 returned **$715,976.07** of Property Level Cap Ex budget across eleven 94xxx categories, with actuals in that band reading zero for August. That figure is a third view of the same subject and reconciles to neither this workbook nor the signed documents. Unresolved, see [[60-Communities/projects]].
