# Board reporting

**What this is:** the March 2026 board deck — what it argues, what it admits went wrong in 2025, and what it asks the board to decide. The most candid document in the vault about the company's own performance.

**Status:** current — 2026-08-30. Source: the March 2026 board presentation, dated 2026-03-11, 76 pages, marked CONFIDENTIAL.

## The facts

### Shape and thesis

- **Central thesis, verbatim: "The engine is built: execution is accelerating."** The framing is that the last six months went into team, systems and process, and 2026 is the execution year.
- **Six sections:** 1. Corporate · 2. JREFI Review · 3. JREFII Review · 4. OpCo Review · 5. Capital Structure Initiatives · 6. Appendix.
- Headline execution counts at March 2026: **398 site plans complete, 201 homes ordered (17 delivered), 52 rehabs complete (45 more in progress), 25 homes sold.**
- **42 total corporate employees** across departments including offshore and support staff.

### The exit-value argument, sourced to Eastdil

- Eastdil's guidance is that **proof of concept on infill raises both the value per vacant lot and the certainty buyers assign to forecast infill cash flows** — so it lifts the price and lowers the discount rate at the same time.
- The illustration: **$50,000 vacant lot value + $65,000 home cost + $35,000 cost to infill**, producing **$81,703 of value creation** and **$166,703 total** with five years of cash flow plus exit, at $600 lot rent increasing $30/year, an 85% incremental margin and a 5.75% year-6 cap rate.
- **Unlevered IRR 15%, or 39% if vacant lot value is treated as zero.**
- Eastdil is engaged for a full portfolio BOV of JREFI with the recap in mind. Its initial commentary: no obvious outliers that turn buyers off, clustering is a strength, POH concentration is not an issue for this portfolio (it becomes a focal point above 25%), and RV-to-park-model conversion proof of concept matters with an ideal threshold below 20% of the portfolio.

### What the deck says went wrong — the most useful part of it

- **JREFI missed 2025 NOI by about $700k on occupancy that was flat year on year**, partly offset by better expenses. Average core occupancy came in at 83% against an 87% budget.
- **Roughly 60% of the $920k rental income miss came from lighter RV occupancy, concentrated at River Oaks, Vantage Oaks, Lucky Clover and Paradise Lakes.** The other 40% is attributed to the infill initiative taking longer than expected to get moving.
- **The Geiger Key Fish Camp missed income by $275k.** The restaurant's GM departed at the start of Q3 and performance in the first half was affected while she was checked out; she has been replaced.
- **Interest expense ran $1,403,926 over budget (+19%)** because interest was projected in September 2024 and the SOFR curve did not come down as anticipated in 2025.
- **A utility billing vendor switch from net 60 to net 30 meant 13 months of utilities were paid in fiscal year 2025** at several properties.
- **Insurance ran 22% over budget** — $873,853 actual against $715,273 budgeted, $158,580 over.
- **2025 acquisition volume came in at $171.5M against a $273.8M budget** — 66 properties and 6,028 units against a budgeted 83 and 7,398.
- **OpCo lost money in 2025 and is budgeted to lose more in 2026:** net loss **$(2,071,077)** in 2025 against a budgeted $(1,455,369), and a **2026 budget of $(3,128,894)** on income that is essentially flat.
- **The Northgate WWTP replacement is a lender-required repair** — $600,000, targeted August 2027, overhauling the first train and adding two digesters.

### What the deck asks the board for

- **Approval of the 2026 KPI-driven bonus plan structure for corporate employees: Company 25% / Department 50% / Discretionary 25%, paying 80% at downside, 100% at target and 120% at upside.** Each executive's department KPIs are named — NOI and occupancy are the company component for all of them.
- **Approval of 2026 equity compensation awards.**
- **Direction on the founder accrued bonuses — $900k in aggregate as at 2025-12-31, deliberately left unpaid to preserve cash** through the rate-increase period. The founders state there is no personal urgency to pay themselves and ask the board to **discuss contributing the accrual as equity at the valuation associated with the year-end of the bonus** (2023 and 2024).
- **Endorsement of the founder bonus metric framework** — the 2025 review and the proposed 2026 metrics, weighted across JREFI/JREFII NOI, OpCo gross expense, JREFI infill and JREFII POH sales, with a discretionary component.
- **Direction on the JREFI recap and the Core+ pairing, and on the Eastdil engagement.** Both portfolios are fully financed with no maturities until mid-2027; the recap process is to launch by year-end 2026.
- **Sign-off on the disposition slate:** **Geiger Key** on a two-pronged sale process (Marcus & Millichap to RV park operators, JLL to hotel and lodging developers); **Congress** with a **$5,000,000 LOI signed from Philips International** ($200,000 deposit, 45-day DD, ~$2.7M net proceeds after debt paydown); **Shell Pointe** with a buyer selected at **$6,600,000** ($4.1M cash at close plus a $2.5M six-month 4% I/O seller note); and the **Northgate agricultural parcel** — 44.57 acres, zoned AU Agricultural Residential — listed with a broker at a **$995,000 ask**.
