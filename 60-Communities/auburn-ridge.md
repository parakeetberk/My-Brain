## The facts

- Lot mix per the Property Data Sheet (`PDS Clean`, rows 5-72, as of 2026-08-30): 133 MH lots, 24 other units, totalling 157 Total Core Units. The sheet's "Total Units" column reads 40 on every row and is broken, so it is not used.
- Site is 40.9 acres in Barrow County, GA - All-Ages, flood zone No (X) (Property Data Sheet).
- Acquired 03/06/2025 for $13,000,000 - Off-Market deal, Operator seller (Property Data Sheet).
- Occupancy was 92% as of December 2025 (Property Data Sheet).
- 2026 gross lot rent: MH $675 (Property Data Sheet).
- 2026 rent benchmark: $684 in place, $574 adjusted, $716 market (2026 Rents + Mkt Rents workbook, 2026 plan).
- Park-owned homes: 45 as of the Property Data Sheet.
- Utilities per the Property Data Sheet: water Public, paid by Resident; sewer Septic, paid by Community; trash paid by Resident.
- Regional manager: Liznette Santiago (Parakeet Staffing Org Chart, dated December 2025).
- On-site manager: Justin Robbins (Parakeet Staffing Org Chart, dated December 2025).
- 2026 infill plan: 12 total vacancies against an occupant-gain goal of 12 - 1 vacant lot and 11 park-owned homes vacant; 9 rehab & sell, 3 infill & sell, 0 straight sell, leaving 0 remaining vacancies at an occupancy goal of 157 occupied lots Source: [[80-Finance/planning/2026-executive-strategy/Infill Strategy Planning (hardcodes).xlsx]], a March 2026 snapshot; the live numbers are managed by the team on shared sheets reviewed weekly.
- Manager commentary on the Infill sheet, verbatim: "Jeremy is good."
- Marked complete on the capex plan: Utility Trailer ($3,000).
- Cut from the plan (marked Deleted): AC Units for Duplexes; Stairs for Duplexes; Playground for duplexes; Sign Package; Dupex Renos (4); Dupex Renos (4); Dupex Renos (4); Dupex Renos (3).
- Signage plan: FULL NEW SIGN PACKAGE, High End structure. Note on the plan: Use existing sign structure, match green to other signs; stucco over cinderblock (CapEx Overview workbook, `Sheet1`).
- Payroll model: 2026 budgeted revenue $1,410,702; payroll allocation 4.0% of revenue against a 5% target; salary per pad $363 across 157 pads. That leaves a surplus of $13,615 against the 5% allocation (Payroll Breakdown Allocation by Property, `Sheet1`, 2026 budget).
- The 2026-08-18 site visit recorded roads in good condition with about three speed bumps still needing paint.
- That visit recorded 128 lots on the property, with lots 1-58 sub-metered on Metron meters and other homes on city meters costing about $5,000 each; this does not reconcile with the Property Data Sheet's 157 Total Core Units and the discrepancy is unresolved.
- A significant share of spend goes to buying water from the City of Auburn; water pipes are 5-6 feet deep, making leak detection difficult.
- Path Kids Club, a nonprofit renting a home on the property for children's programmes, received a $500 gift card for flooring at the visit; police report a significant reduction in crime since new ownership.
- A new maintenance candidate was interviewed at the visit, and a utility golf cart was identified as needed to cover the property.
- The Infill sheet commentary says "Jeremy is good", but the December-2025 org chart assigns Justin Robbins to Auburn Ridge and Jeremy Gray to Oakside, Hershey Park and Greene Pointe. The two sources disagree on who is meant.
