# The three regions

**What this is:** how Property Operations is split on the ground, and the roll-up for each region. The org chart runs Director of Property Operations to Regional Manager to the on-site manager at each community.

**Status:** current - 2026-08-31

## The facts

- Regions are recorded here, not in the folder tree. Properties get reassigned and regionals change - Stephanie Roth held a region in the December 2025 org chart and has since been terminated, with Alexandra Pontes holding the same territory. Encoding the region in the folder path would mean moving files and rewriting links every time that happens.
- Natalie Magurne is Director of Property Operations. Every regional below reports to her.
