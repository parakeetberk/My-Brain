## Detail

**What this is:** A 213-unit manufactured housing community in Brunswick, GA (212 MH lots, 1 other units), held in the JREFII portfolio.

## The facts

- Lot mix per the Property Data Sheet (`PDS Clean`, rows 5-72, as of 2026-08-30): 212 MH lots, 1 other units, totalling 213 Total Core Units. The sheet's "Total Units" column reads 40 on every row and is broken, so it is not used.
- Site is 20.0 acres in Glynn County, GA - All-Ages, flood zone Yes (AE) (Property Data Sheet).
- Acquired 11/06/2024 for $11,600,000 - Off-Market deal, Operator seller (Property Data Sheet).
- Occupancy was 74% as of December 2025 (Property Data Sheet).
- 2026 gross lot rent: MH $600 (Property Data Sheet).
- 2026 rent benchmark: $510 in place, $478 adjusted, $419 market (2026 Rents + Mkt Rents workbook, 2026 plan).
- Park-owned homes: 26 as of the Property Data Sheet.
- Utilities per the Property Data Sheet: water Well, paid by Community; sewer Lagoon, paid by Community; trash paid by Community.
- Regional manager: Liznette Santiago (Parakeet Staffing Org Chart, dated December 2025).
- On-site manager: Meghan Vaughn (Parakeet Staffing Org Chart, dated December 2025).
- 2026 infill plan: 55 total vacancies against an occupant-gain goal of 52 - 46 vacant lots and 9 park-owned homes vacant; 7 rehab & sell, 45 infill & sell, 0 straight sell, leaving 3 remaining vacancies at an occupancy goal of 210 occupied lots Source: [[80-Finance/planning/2026-executive-strategy/Infill Strategy Planning (hardcodes).xlsx]], a March 2026 snapshot; the live numbers are managed by the team on shared sheets reviewed weekly.
- Manager commentary on the Infill sheet, verbatim: "No concerns here"
- 2026 capex plan: 1 live projects totalling $12,000 (CapEx Overview - REDO OF CAPEX PLAN, `Data` sheet).
    - Miscellaneous - Golf Cart, $12,000, 2026-01-31 to 2026-03-31, low priority, owned by Operations.
- Cut from the plan (marked Deleted): Signage.
- Signage plan: FULL NEW SIGN PACKAGE, High End structure (CapEx Overview workbook, `Sheet1`).
- Payroll model: 2026 budgeted revenue $1,178,484; payroll allocation 2.5% of revenue against a 5% target; salary per pad $138 across 213 pads. That leaves a surplus of $29,495 against the 5% allocation (Payroll Breakdown Allocation by Property, `Sheet1`, 2026 budget).
- At the 2026-08-19 visit vacant homes were concentrated on Front Street and State Street, all former rentals and in poor condition; a renovated 3-bed 3-bath home at lot 21 rented for $1,850.
- Unpermitted structures and additions are a significant issue and are being met with dollar violations, which is reported to be helping.
- Hiring for the maintenance position was on its fifth attempt, previous hires not wanting outdoor work; a Gator was suggested to aid retention.
- Extensive tree work is needed - pine trees drop needles and have shallow roots, and several trees have roots lifting homes.
- The community pond has an active EPD violation over a broken cement wall causing seepage, reportedly damaged by the previous company, plus duckweed, overgrowth and two alligators.
- There is significant land available for new units, with clearing costed at about $15,000 an acre; Georgia was noted as a relatively easy state for expansion. White three-rail PVC fencing reinforced with steel beams is planned at every turn, plus a vinyl privacy fence around the lift station.
