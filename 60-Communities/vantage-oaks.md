- Acquired 03/11/2022 for $5,000,000 - Off-Market deal, Mom & Pop seller (Property Data Sheet).
- Occupancy was 71% as of December 2025 (Property Data Sheet).
- 2026 gross lot rent: MH $600, RV $600 (Property Data Sheet).

- At the 2026-08-21 site visit paving, curbs and driveways had been rebid at $683,000, down from a $1,800,000 quote.
- An inspection dispute - one inspector approving a 12-inch pipe pre-pour, a second requiring 15 inches and a specific concrete slope - forced demolition and re-pour and cost about six weeks.
- A flood-zone concern at the back of the site was resolved: flood elevation 20 feet against ground grade of 16-17 feet plus 3.8 feet standard set height, about 20.8 feet, so no elevation is needed.
- The Okeechobee Building Department requires two copies of each home's installation manual per permit packet and physical pickup of paid permits within 72 hours, so the team collects permits in person; permit processing was three weeks behind.
- The inspector requires a 200-amp pedestal for any 200-amp home, and generators cannot legally power full 200-amp homes while the homes are commercially owned; small portable generators will run fans at open houses until permanent power arrives.
- The clubhouse roof was recently replaced; gas is not allowed and a 220-volt upgrade was judged infeasible, so the plan is a covered concrete barbecue pad at one end and a small sink-only kitchen room at the other.
- On-site marketing planned at the visit: fifty "For Sale" signs and twenty flags, plus flyer flags on end lots negotiated with residents for $100 Amazon gift cards; management is preparing for throughput of twenty new home sales a month against a pipeline of roughly 500 used-stock sales to work down.
