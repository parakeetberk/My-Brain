Evid. § 408 Settlement Communication"* and is privileged; nothing from it goes to a resident, a broker or anyone outside the company.
    - **The claim**: the **November 2023 lot rent increases** at this community and its sister park, brought under the **Manufactured Housing Act and Chapter 93A**, with class claims and individual plaintiffs' claims. Plaintiffs seek *"at least double and likely treble damages for overpayments of rent and attorneys' fees."* No court or docket number appears in the chain.
    - **Counsel**: Melissa Allison, with Martell Johnson and Sean Grammel, Anderson & Kreiger LLP, Boston, for plaintiffs. Zachary M. Wallack with Gabriel Dym, Eckert Seamans, for Parakeet.
    - **A $400,000 lump sum is agreed by both sides.** Offered 2026-07-14 as best and final, accepted 2026-07-31. **The only open issue is the rent schedule** from December 2028, when the agreed credit framework expires.
    - **The impasse**: Parakeet proposes the lease amount **plus $50** from 2028-12-01; plaintiffs propose **$20 a year** for owner-tenants.
    - **The legal disagreement underneath it**: Parakeet's position is that the Manufactured Housing Act asks whether the **resulting** rent is a fair market rental rate, not how large the increase was. Plaintiffs reject that framing and call the increases themselves unlawful, at **23 to 25% for owner tenants and 35 to 42% for rental tenants**. Whether the test is the rate or the increase is the case.
    - **Where it stands**: two paths are on the table, share the CBRE analysis and argue the number, or hand it to a jointly selected appraiser whose determination binds both sides. Counsel spoke to plaintiffs' counsel on 2026-09-01 at 11:00. See [[70-Operations/meetings/2026-08-31-settlement-strategy-and-auburn-rent-control]] and `00-Meta/commitments.md` item 20.
