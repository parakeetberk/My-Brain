# MOC — Communities

The 67 active communities. One page each, and every page opens with a hot sheet: the snapshot, the watch list, and the live block that Rent Manager fills.

The community is where the business happens, so it is a top-level folder.

## The record sheets, and why they are counted rather than listed

**Three generated registers sit under this folder. Each sheet links out to its community page, so none of them is an orphan; listing 166 of them here would bury the map they belong to.** They are rebuilt by the community pulse refresh, not written by hand.

- `lots/` - **99 live lot sheets**, one per lot with something open on it, named `<CODE>-<lot>`. Each opens with a `LOT SHEET` line naming its community and its open count.
- `lots/_superseded/` - **57 retired lot sheets**, kept rather than deleted. These are earlier naming generations, mostly `<CODE>-home-<lot>` and date-suffixed forms, superseded when the lot-code convention settled. **Nothing points at them by design; they are the audit trail of the rename.**
- `assets/` - **10 asset sheets** tracking a physical home or an order through its states: `asset-id`, `cavco-order` and `cavco-quote` records currently reading ordered, moved or disputed. Each names the community and lot it currently sits at.
